Your Impact
  
Cisco CX Engineering is seeking a Software Development Engineer to drive innovation and develop solutions for customer’s most pressing usecase with a focus on incorporating AI and ML. This role requires deep technical expertise in network automation, full-stack development, and applicable AI use-cases.
  
  
**Key Responsibilities: Application Development:**  

* Design, develop, and maintain scalable software applications across the full stack (front-end, back-end, and integration layers).
* Architect and develop robust systems with an emphasis on scalability, reliability, and performance.

**AI/ML Integration:**  

* Incorporate AI and ML-driven features into product solutions, ensuring real-world applicability and responsible AI practices.

**Collaboration& Process:**  

* Work closely with UX/UI designers, product managers, and fellow engineers to deliver high-quality products.
* Participate in agile ceremonies, sprint planning, and CI/CD pipeline management.

**Quality& Security:**  

* Conduct thorough code reviews, automated testing, and maintain comprehensive documentation.
* Ensure adherence to security best practices, code quality, and maintainability standards.

**Minimum Qualifications:**  

* Bachelor’s degree with 0-2+ years of related experience, or Master’s degree with 0-2+ years of related experience, or PhD with 0+ years of related experience.
* Experience with one or more of the following: Golang, Python, Pytest, SQL (preferably PostgreSQL), Angular, JavaScript, or TypeScript.
* Experience with one or more of the following: microservices, Kubernetes, containerization, virtualization, or cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
* Experience integrating AI features (AI-assisted code generation), event-driven architecture (Apache Kafka) and real-time data pipelines.
* Experience with RESTful API design, API security, or hierarchical and relational data modeling.

Preferred Qualifications
  
  

* Experience with DevOps tools and practices such as Jenkins or GitHub Actions.
* Experience with Git, system design, or application performance optimization.
* Experience with testing, debugging, and technical documentation.
* Experience with secrets management tools such as HashiCorp Vault or object storage platforms such as MinIO or Amazon S3.
* Problem-solving, communication, and customer-facing delivery skills.
