**About Position**
We are seeking a highly skilled Senior AI Application Developer with 5 to 8 years of experience in building enterprise-grade Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions. The ideal candidate will possess strong expertise in Google ADK, AI agent frameworks, Large Language Models (LLMs), Knowledge Graphs, Snowflake integration, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). You will be responsible for designing, developing, and deploying AI-powered applications and enterprise agents that drive automation, intelligent decision-making, and business innovation at scale.
  
  

* Role: Senior AI Application Developer - Agentic AI & Generative AI
* Location: All Persistent Locations
* Experience: 8 to 12 Years
* Job Type: Full-Time Employment

**What You'll Do**

* Design, develop, and deploy enterprise AI agents using Google ADK and Agent Harness frameworks.
* Extend and customize agent orchestration frameworks to support evolving business requirements.
* Architect intelligent Agentic AI solutions with robust context management, grounding, memory, and multi-agent collaboration capabilities.
* Define and implement strategies for tool invocation, workflow orchestration, state management, and error handling.
* Build scalable Generative AI applications supporting enterprise use cases such as automation, knowledge management, decision support, and intelligent assistants.
* Develop and optimize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and knowledge retrieval solutions.
* Work with Knowledge Graph architectures, including entities, relationships, metadata management, and semantic search capabilities.
* Integrate AI agents with Snowflake-based enterprise data platforms to enable secure and efficient access to business information.
* Deploy, monitor, and manage AI applications on Google Cloud Platform environments.
* Design high-performance backend components supporting AI agent communication and orchestration workflows.
* Implement enterprise-grade security, reliability, scalability, and governance controls within AI applications.
* Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions and Git-based development workflows.
* Troubleshoot complex AI system issues and optimize agent performance, responsiveness, and reliability.
* Participate in architecture reviews, design discussions, proof-of-concept development, and technical innovation initiatives.
* Create technical documentation, developer guides, and operational runbooks for AI applications.

**Expertise You'll Bring**

* 8 to 12 years of software engineering or AI application development experience.
* Proven hands-on experience building Generative AI and Agentic AI applications.
* Strong expertise with Google Agent Development Kit (Google ADK).
* Experience working with enterprise agent orchestration frameworks and agent lifecycle management.
* Deep understanding of context management, grounding techniques, memory architecture, tool calling, and multi-agent interactions.
* Strong knowledge of Large Language Models (LLMs) and prompt engineering techniques.
* Experience implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures and semantic search solutions.
* Familiarity with vector search, embeddings, document retrieval, and enterprise knowledge systems.
* Strong understanding of Knowledge Graph concepts, including entities, relationships, metadata, and semantic modeling.
* Experience integrating Snowflake and enterprise data platforms with AI applications.
* Hands-on expertise with Google Cloud Platform (GCP) deployment and cloud-native application architecture.
* Strong proficiency in Git, GitHub, and CI/CD automation using GitHub Actions.
* Experience implementing scalable, secure, and production-ready AI solutions.
* Strong debugging, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.
* Understanding of application architecture, distributed systems, API integration, and software engineering best practices.
* Excellent communication, collaboration, and stakeholder management capabilities.
