What you'll do

● Own the technical design of LLM-based and agentic systems that serve Sales, Service and Supply Chain

workflows, including assistants, retrieval over enterprise knowledge, tool-using agents that act against

● Deliver those systems into the channels people actually use: web and in-app chat, email and SMS. Own

the conversational contract across them, including threading, context carry-over, handoff to a human,

● Take ideas from prototype to production: define the architecture, break it into workstreams, and stay

accountable for what ships.

● Build the parts that make AI safe to run in an enterprise: input and output guardrails, identity-aware

access to tools and data, prompt versioning, and clear separation between internal and supplier-facing

surfaces.

● Design and maintain the tool layer, including MCP servers and internal tool interfaces, so agents reach

business systems through contracts that are versioned, permissioned and testable.

● Establish how we measure quality, including evaluation datasets, regression suites, tracing and

production telemetry, so model and prompt changes are judged on evidence, not opinion.

● Own the unit economics. Track cost per conversation and per resolution, and bring it down through

model routing, context trimming, caching, batching and right-sized retrieval.

● Integrate with the systems of record: ERP, supplier portals, service and CRM platforms, logistics and

planning systems. Most of the difficulty in this role is integration and data, not model selection.

● Review designs and code across the team, mentor engineers, and raise the technical bar through what

you demonstrate rather than what you mandate.
