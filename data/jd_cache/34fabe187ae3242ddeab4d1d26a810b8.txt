**What Will You Do?** In order to grow and build a successful career with The Toro Company, you will be responsible for:

**Platform Administration**
---------------------------

* Administer, maintain, and optimize enterprise data platforms including **Snowflake, Fivetran, dbt, Microsoft Azure, and AI services**. Manage users, roles, permissions, authentication methods, networking policies, integrations, service identities, and environment configurations while ensuring security, governance, and operational stability across production and non-production environments.
* Monitor platform health, performance, data pipelines, and deployment activities. Troubleshoot connectivity, ingestion, transformation, and infrastructure issues, partnering with engineering teams and vendors to resolve incidents and minimize business impact.
* Support platform scalability and reliability through workload management, access controls, service monitoring, upgrade planning, and operational best practices while ensuring alignment with enterprise standards and compliance requirements.

**FinOps & Cost Optimization**
------------------------------

* Drive cloud and data platform cost management across **Azure, Snowflake, Fivetran, dbt, and AI services** by analyzing consumption, tracking budgets, forecasting spend, and creating actionable cost and usage dashboards for stakeholders.
* Partner with Finance, Procurement, and business teams to identify optimization opportunities, investigate cost anomalies, allocate shared expenses, and measure realized savings while maintaining agreed service levels.
* Optimize infrastructure, licensing, storage, compute, and AI consumption through continuous monitoring, rightsizing, automation, and governance initiatives.

**Reliability, Security & Automation**
--------------------------------------

* Own platform operations, monitoring, alert management, incident response, root cause analysis, and service reliability initiatives to ensure high system availability and performance.
* Implement and enforce security best practices including least-privilege access, RBAC, SSO, MFA, credential rotation, access reviews, audit readiness, and compliance with organizational security standards.
* Automate provisioning, access management, reporting, monitoring, and operational workflows using **Terraform, Python, PowerShell, Bash, APIs, Git, and CI/CD pipelines**, while maintaining documentation, runbooks, and disaster recovery procedures.

**Required Qualifications**
---------------------------

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT, Engineering, or equivalent experience.
* **5+ years** of experience in cloud operations, platform engineering, DevOps, data platforms, or database administration.
* Strong experience with **Snowflake Administration** and **Microsoft Azure Operations**.
* Hands-on experience with **Fivetran, dbt, SQL, Terraform, Git, CI/CD pipelines**, and scripting (Python/PowerShell/Bash).
* Experience in cloud cost management, governance, monitoring, security, and performance optimization.
* Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.

**Preferred Qualifications**
----------------------------

* Experience with **Microsoft Foundry, Azure Machine Learning, Azure AI Search, Snowflake Cortex, MLOps, or GenAIOps**.
* Experience building **Power BI** cost dashboards and managing FinOps practices.
* Knowledge of SSO/SCIM, Infrastructure as Code, disaster recovery, SLOs, and platform automation.
* Relevant certifications in **Azure, Snowflake, Terraform, or FinOps**.
