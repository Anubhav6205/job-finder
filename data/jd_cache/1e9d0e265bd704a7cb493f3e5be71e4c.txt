Prompt Engineer – Generative AI  
Role Purpose  
Centrica is looking for an experienced Prompt Engineer – Generative AI to design, develop, evaluate and optimise prompt based solutions using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI.  
The role will work closely with product, engineering, data and business teams to translate real business problems into effective AI experiences and workflows.  
The Prompt Engineer will take ownership of prompt design, prompt optimisation, evaluation, testing and guardrails, helping move Generative AI use cases from experimentation and proof of concept into reliable, secure and scalable production solutions.  
The role requires a combination of strong analytical capability, understanding of LLM behaviour, hands on prompt engineering, experimentation, evaluation and sufficient technical knowledge to work effectively with AI engineers and software development teams.  
Key Responsibilities  
1. Prompt Engineering & Optimisation  
  
. Design, develop and optimise prompts for Large Language Models and Generative AI applications.  
. Create reusable prompt templates and prompt patterns for different business use cases.  
. Apply techniques including: Zero shot prompting Few shot prompting Structured prompting Contextual prompting Role/task based prompting Tool/function oriented prompting  
. Experiment systematically with different prompt structures, instructions and context.  
. Improve output accuracy, consistency, relevance and reliability.  
. Reduce hallucinations and undesirable model behaviour.  
. Optimise prompts considering quality, latency and token/cost efficiency.  
  
2. Generative AI Solution Development  
  
. Work with AI and software engineers to build Generative AI solutions that deliver measurable business value.  
. Translate business requirements into effective LLM based interactions and workflows.  
. Support use cases such as: Enterprise knowledge assistants Customer/service agent assistance Document summarisation and analysis Information extraction Classification Content generation Question answering Workflow automation Decision support applications  
. Help move Generative AI capabilities from experimentation into production ready solutions.  
  
3. LLM Evaluation & Testing  
  
. Develop systematic approaches for evaluating prompt and model performance.  
. Define evaluation criteria covering: Accuracy Relevance Completeness Consistency Groundedness Hallucination Safety Response quality  
. Build representative evaluation datasets and test scenarios.  
. Conduct comparative testing across prompts, models and configurations.  
. Perform regression testing following prompt or model changes.  
. Analyse failure patterns and identify opportunities for improvement.  
. Establish measurable acceptance criteria before prompts are promoted into production.  
  
4. Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)  
  
. Work with engineering teams to design prompts for Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions.  
. Optimise how retrieved enterprise context is supplied to LLMs.  
. Design system and user prompts that encourage grounded responses based on approved information.  
. Support optimisation of retrieval and generation workflows.  
. Evaluate whether responses are properly grounded in retrieved source material.  
. Help identify whether poor results originate from retrieval, context, prompt design or the underlying model.  
  
5. Prompt Libraries & Reusable Patterns  
  
. Develop and maintain a governed library of reusable prompt templates.  
. Define standard patterns for common Generative AI use cases.  
. Maintain appropriate prompt documentation and version history.  
. Ensure prompt changes can be tested, reviewed and traced.  
. Promote reuse rather than duplication across AI solutions.  
. Capture lessons learned and effective prompting practices for wider engineering teams.  
  
6. Responsible AI, Safety & Guardrails  
  
. Ensure prompt based solutions comply with Centrica's security, privacy, governance and responsible AI requirements.  
. Design appropriate safeguards against: Prompt injection Jailbreak attempts Hallucination Sensitive information disclosure Harmful or inappropriate outputs Unintended model behaviour  
. Define system level instructions and constraints for production AI applications.  
. Work with Security, Data and AI Governance stakeholders to identify and mitigate risks.  
. Support testing for bias, safety, privacy and explainability.  
. Ensure human oversight is incorporated where appropriate.  
  
7. Business Requirement Translation  
  
. Work directly with business and product stakeholders to understand problems that could benefit from Generative AI.  
. Translate business requirements into clear AI tasks and prompt strategies.  
. Facilitate discovery and experimentation sessions with users.  
. Understand user journeys and determine where AI can genuinely improve outcomes.  
. Challenge use cases where Generative AI may not be the appropriate solution.  
. Convert user feedback into measurable prompt and solution improvements.  
  
8. AI Experimentation  
  
. Experiment with different Large Language Models and model configurations.  
. Compare models based on: Output quality Accuracy Reasoning capability Latency Cost Security Context window requirements  
. Conduct controlled experiments to determine the most effective approach for each use case.  
. Document findings and provide evidence based recommendations.  
. Stay current with developments in LLMs, Generative AI and prompt engineering techniques.  
  
9. Production AI & Monitoring  
  
. Work with AI engineering teams to integrate prompts into production applications and services.  
. Ensure prompts are version controlled and deployed through appropriate engineering processes.  
. Define monitoring requirements for prompt/model performance.  
. Analyse production feedback and failure patterns.  
. Support continuous improvement of live Generative AI services.  
. Identify prompt/model drift or degradation.  
. Contribute to incident investigation where AI behaviour affects production services.  
  
10. Collaboration with AI Engineering  
Work closely with:  
  
. AI / ML Engineers  
. Software Engineers  
. Data Engineers  
. Data Scientists  
. Product Managers / Product Owners  
. UX / Conversation Designers  
. Solution Architects  
. Security teams  
. Data Governance teams  
. Business SMEs  
  
Ensure prompt design is integrated into the overall engineering lifecycle rather than treated as an isolated activity.  
11. Documentation & Governance  
  
. Document prompt purpose, design decisions and expected behaviour.  
. Maintain prompt versions and associated evaluation results.  
. Document known limitations and failure scenarios.  
. Define appropriate usage guidance.  
. Support governance reviews for production AI solutions.  
. Ensure sufficient traceability between business requirements, prompt versions, testing and production releases.

Candidate Profile  
The ideal candidate is not simply someone who is good at writing prompts in ChatGPT.  
We are looking for someone who understands how to engineer reliable LLM behaviour in an enterprise environment and can answer questions such as:  
How do we measure whether this prompt is actually better? How do we prevent hallucinations? How do we ground the answer in enterprise data? How do we protect against prompt injection? How do we version and regression test prompts? How do we monitor quality after deployment?  
The strongest candidate would combine:  
Prompt Engineering + LLMs + Evaluation + RAG + Python + Responsible AI + Production Engineering Awareness  
with the ability to work closely with business stakeholders and translate real Centrica use cases into effective AI solutions.
