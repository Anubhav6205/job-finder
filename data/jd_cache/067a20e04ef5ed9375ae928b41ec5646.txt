You’ll define standards for safe model deployment, governance, and observability — and mentor a growing team to deliver innovative, compliant, and reliable AI features that scale across the LPL enterprise. Here you will:

* Help build a first-of-its-kind AI platform at the intersection of finance and responsible automation
* Influence the enterprise AI roadmap and technology strategy
* Collaborate with cross-functional leaders in engineering, compliance, and innovation
* Enjoy a culture of innovation, autonomy, and long-term impact

**Responsibilities:**

* Lead the end-to-end development of agentic AI systems leveraging AWS (SageMaker, Lambda, Step Functions, ECS/EKS) and open-source orchestration frameworks (LangChain, Hugging Face, etc.)
