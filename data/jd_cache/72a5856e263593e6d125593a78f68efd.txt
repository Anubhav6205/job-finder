**Roles and Responsibilities :**

* Design, develop, test, and implement automation solutions using PLCs, SCADA systems, HMIs, and DCS/Drive systems.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify process improvements and implement changes to increase efficiency and productivity.
* Troubleshoot issues with existing automated processes and provide recommendations for optimization.
* Develop documentation of automated systems including user manuals, technical specifications, and maintenance procedures.

**Job Requirements :**

* 4-5 years of experience in Automation Engineering or related field.
* Proficiency in programming languages such as Ladder Logic (Rockwell) or Function Block Diagrams (SCADA).
* Strong understanding of communication protocols like Profibus, DeviceNet, Modbus RTU/TCP etc.
