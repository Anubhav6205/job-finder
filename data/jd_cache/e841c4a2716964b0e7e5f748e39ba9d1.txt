Job Description

**Job Summary**

We are looking for a GenAI Developer – Snowflake with 4–6 years of experience in Data/ML Engineering or Software Development, including 1–2 years of hands-on GenAI/LLM experience. The candidate will design and develop production-ready GenAI solutions using Snowflake Cortex AI, Cortex Search, Cortex Analyst, Python and RAG frameworks.

The role involves building chatbots, copilots, document Q&A and summarisation solutions; implementing RAG pipelines; prompt engineering; LLM evaluation; and integrating AI solutions through APIs and Streamlit. Strong knowledge of Python, Snowflake, embeddings, vector/hybrid search and AI security/governance is required.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Gen AI Solution Development • Design and build GenAI applications on Snowflake Cortex AI, including chatbots, copilots, summarisation tools and document Q&A.
* Use Cortex AI Functions (AISQL) such as AI\_COMPLETE, AI\_CLASSIFY, AI\_EXTRACT, AI\_SENTIMENT, AI\_FILTER, AI\_AGG and AI\_EMBED to enrich and analyse structured and unstructured data at scale.
* Build RAG pipelines with Cortex Search, covering document parsing (AI\_PARSE\_DOCUMENT), chunking strategy, embeddings, hybrid search and re-ranking.
* Develop natural-language-to-SQL experiences with Cortex Analyst, including authoring and tuning semantic models and semantic views.
* Build and configure Cortex Agents and Snowflake Intelligence agents that orchestrate across Cortex Search, Cortex Analyst and custom tools.
* Integrate Cortex capabilities into applications through the Cortex REST API, Python SDK and Streamlit in Snowflake.

**Data Engineering and Pipelines**

* Work with data engineers on data models, data quality and incremental refresh of AI outputs.

**Quality, Cost and Governance**

* Write and version prompts, and choose the right model (for example Claude, Llama, Mistral, OpenAI or Snowflake Arctic) for accuracy, latency and cost.

* Set up evaluation for LLM outputs, including groundedness, relevance, hallucination checks and regression test sets, using tools such as Snowflake AI Observability or TruLens.

* Apply Cortex AI Guardrails, PII redaction (AI\_REDACT), role-based access control, masking policies and row access policies so that AI applications respect data security.

* Monitor credit consumption and query performance, and tune warehouses, batch sizes and model choice to keep costs predictable.

**Delivery and Collaboration**

* Translate business problems into GenAI use cases with clear success metrics.

* Run proofs of concept, then harden them into production releases with CI/CD, documentation and runbooks.

* Present solutions and results to technical and business stakeholders.
* Mentor junior developers and share reusable patterns across the team.

**Required Skills and Experience**

* 4 to 6 years of total experience in ML engineering or backend development.

* At least 1 to 2 years of hands-on GenAI or LLM work, with one or more solutions taken to production.

* Practical experience with Snowflake Cortex AI: AISQL functions, Cortex Search and Cortex Analyst at a minimum. Cortex Agents experience is a strong plus.

* Strong Python skills, including Snowpark, pandas and REST API integration.

* Solid grasp of RAG: chunking, embeddings, vector and hybrid search, re-ranking and context window management.

* Prompt engineering skills: structured outputs (JSON), few-shot prompting, system prompts and prompt versioning.

* Experience evaluating LLM output quality and fixing hallucination, retrieval and grounding issues.

* Working knowledge of Snowflake security and governance: RBAC, masking policies and secure data sharing.

• Experience with Git and CI/CD for data or AI workloads (GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, Jenkins or similar). • Clear written and verbal communication, and comfort working directly with business users.

**Good to Have**

* Snowflake certifications: SnowPro Core, SnowPro Advanced (Data Engineer or Architect), or the SnowPro Specialty GenAI certification.

* Experience with Document AI, Cortex Fine-tuning, Snowflake ML (Model Registry, Feature Store) or Snowpark Container Services.

* Familiarity with Cortex Code, Openflow, Snowflake Notebooks or the Snowflake MCP server.

* Exposure to other GenAI stacks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI.

* Experience with dbt, Airflow or similar orchestration tools.

* Front-end skills for AI applications: Streamlit, React or similar.

* Domain knowledge in financial services, insurance, healthcare, retail or life sciences.

* Client-facing consulting or pre-sales experience, including building demos and estimating effort.

**Education**

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Science, Engineering or a related field. Equivalent hands-on experience will also be considered.
