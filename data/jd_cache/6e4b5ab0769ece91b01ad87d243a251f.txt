Junior Legal Associate Real Estate

**Experience:** 02 Years (Freshers can apply)  
**Qualification:** LL.B (Mandatory)  
**Location:** Chikkaballapur , Nandi Hills  
**Languages:** Kannada (Mandatory), English

  

**Job Summary**

We are looking for a Junior Legal Associate to assist the senior legal team in real estate documentation, property title due diligence, legal drafting, registration and compliance activities.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Assist in property title due diligence and collection of property documents.
* Organise title flow records and maintain property documentation.
* Prepare first-level drafts of JDA, Sale Agreements, GPA and other legal documents.
* Review, format and proofread legal documents.
* Maintain physical and digital legal records.
* Track pending documents and coordinate follow-ups.
* Coordinate with Sub-Registrar Offices for property registrations.
* Assist in document execution and registration processes.
* Coordinate with advocates and support litigation-related documentation.

  

**Skills Required**

* Basic understanding of property documents and real estate law.
* Knowledge of Sale Deed, EC, RTC and Khata (Karnataka).
* Basic understanding of registration procedures and stamp duty.
* Good legal drafting and documentation skills.
* Strong attention to detail and coordination skills.
* Ability to meet deadlines and follow instructions.

  

**Eligibility**

* LL.B degree is mandatory.
* LL.M is an added advantage.
* 02 years of experience; freshers are welcome.
* Kannada proficiency is mandatory.
* Good English communication skills.
