About the Role

AIOTY Labs is looking for an enthusiastic AI Engineer specializing in computer vision and deep learning to develop AI-powered inspection, defect detection, object recognition, and industrial automation solutions.

The candidate will work on real-world AI vision applications deployed in manufacturing and industrial environments.

Key Responsibilities

* Develop, train, and optimize computer vision and deep learning models.
* Work on object detection, classification, segmentation, OCR, and anomaly detection.
* Build AI-powered visual inspection systems for industrial applications.
* Develop solutions using Python, OpenCV, PyTorch, and TensorFlow.
* Train and fine-tune models using industrial image datasets.
* Work with YOLO architectures and other computer vision frameworks.
* Integrate industrial cameras, USB cameras, IP cameras, and Raspberry Pi cameras.
* Optimize AI models for NVIDIA GPUs and edge computing devices.
* Develop real-time image and video processing pipelines.
* Deploy AI models in production environments and improve inference performance.
* Perform dataset collection, annotation, preprocessing, and model evaluation.

Required Skills

* Strong fundamentals in Python programming.
* Knowledge of machine learning and deep learning concepts.
* Understanding of CNNs and computer vision algorithms.
* Experience or academic exposure to OpenCV.
* Familiarity with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
* Knowledge of object detection models such as YOLO.
* Basic understanding of Linux environments.

Preferred Skills

* Experience with Anomalib, ONNX, or TensorRT.
* Knowledge of NVIDIA CUDA and GPU acceleration.
* Familiarity with image segmentation and industrial defect detection.
* Experience with Flask, FastAPI, or REST APIs.
* Knowledge of Git and Docker.
* Exposure to edge AI devices and industrial cameras.

Qualifications

B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, ECE, or related fields.
