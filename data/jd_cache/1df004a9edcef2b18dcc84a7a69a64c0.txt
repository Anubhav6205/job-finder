**Job Description AI Engineer (Digital Transformation)**

**Experience: 2–4 Years**  
 **Location: Bangalore**  
 **Domain: Digital Transformation / Digital Solutions**  
 **Employment Type: Full-Time**

**Role Overview**

We are looking for an AI Engineer with 2–4 years of experience in developing and implementing AI-driven solutions for digital transformation initiatives. The candidate should have hands-on experience in Python, Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Large Language Models (LLMs). The role involves building intelligent applications, automating business processes, and integrating AI capabilities into enterprise solutions.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Design, develop, and implement AI/ML solutions to address business challenges and improve operational efficiency.
* Develop and integrate Generative AI applications using Large Language Models (LLMs).
* Build AI-powered applications such as chatbots, intelligent assistants, document processing, and automation solutions.
* Work with LLM frameworks and orchestration tools such as LangChain and LangGraph.
* Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using vector databases and embedding models.
* Develop REST APIs and integrate AI models with enterprise applications and digital platforms.
* Perform data preprocessing, model evaluation, testing, and performance optimization.
* Work with cloud-based AI services and platforms to deploy scalable AI solutions.
* Collaborate with business analysts, architects, developers, and stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver AI-driven solutions.
* Stay updated with emerging AI technologies, frameworks, and industry best practices.

**Required Skills & Qualifications**

* 2–4 years of hands-on experience in AI/ML engineering or software development with AI implementations.
* Strong programming skills in Python.
* Good understanding of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Natural Language Processing (NLP).
* Hands-on experience with Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs).
* Experience with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, or LlamaIndex.
* Understanding of RAG architecture, embeddings, semantic search, and vector databases.
* Experience working with APIs, REST services, and application integrations.
* Knowledge of ML libraries such as Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
* Familiarity with Git, Docker, and basic CI/CD practices.
* Good analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills.

**Good to Have**

* Exposure to cloud AI services such as Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or Google Vertex AI.
* Experience with vector databases such as FAISS, Pinecone, ChromaDB, or Weaviate.
* Knowledge of prompt engineering, fine-tuning, and model evaluation techniques.
* Exposure to AI agents, agentic workflows, and multi-agent systems.
* Understanding of MLOps and model deployment practices.
* Experience in developing enterprise-grade AI solutions and workflow automation

**Preferred Candidate Profile**

* Hands-on experience in building and deploying AI-based applications.
* Ability to translate business requirements into practical AI solutions.
* Understanding of how AI and Generative AI can drive digital transformation.
* Strong communication and collaboration skills.
* Willingness to learn and work with emerging AI technologies.
