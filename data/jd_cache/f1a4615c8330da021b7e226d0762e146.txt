**About us**
------------

Administrative work eats 15 to 25 percent of US healthcare spending. Much of it still runs through phone calls, faxes and payer portals that never had an API. Akomi builds digital staff that does this work the way a person would, inside the systems a practice already uses.

Some of the tasks Akomi already takes on:

* **The Front desk.** Akomi plugs into a practice's telephony system and handles incoming and outgoing phone calls. Akomi autonomously completes complex tasks around patient scheduling, medication refills and patient recalls without a human involved.
* **Referrals.** Practices use Akomi to handle patient scheduling and documentation follow ups for incoming referrals. For outgoing, Akomi takes care of provider communication and referral authorizations with the payer.
* **Benefits.** The day a visit is booked, Akomi checks eligibility and benefits with the payer. Coverage, copay and deductible go on the chart before the patient arrives.
* **Prior Auths.** Akomi checks whether a procedure needs authorization and reads the chart against the payer's medical-necessity criteria. It submits through the payer's portal, fax or phone, follows up until the decision lands and appeals denials.

More than 500 healthcare providers use Akomi. We are growing fast and have $5 million in seed funding.
