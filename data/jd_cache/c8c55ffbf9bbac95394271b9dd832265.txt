We are looking for an experienced and commercially minded **AI Product Owner** to help shape, build and scale Sandhata's AI products and solutions.

  

This role sits at the intersection of **customer needs, business strategy, AI technology and engineering delivery**. You will take ownership of the product lifecycle, from identifying market and customer problems through product definition, prioritisation, development, launch, adoption and continuous improvement.

You will work closely with the **Principal Enterprise AI Architect and AI engineering teams** to translate product vision and customer requirements into clear, prioritised and achievable product capabilities.

This is not simply a backlog-management role. We are looking for someone who can **understand customers, challenge requirements, define compelling AI propositions, make prioritisation decisions and take accountability for product outcomes**.

  

**What You'll Do**

* **Own product vision and roadmap**, translating Sandhata's AI strategy into clear product priorities, capabilities, releases and measurable outcomes.
* **Understand customer and market problems** through discovery sessions, stakeholder engagement, competitor analysis and continuous customer feedback.
* **Define product requirements** including epics, features, user stories, acceptance criteria, workflows and non-functional requirements.
* **Own and prioritise the product backlog**, balancing customer value, strategic objectives, technical dependencies, delivery effort and commercial opportunity.
* **Work closely with AI Architecture and Engineering**, ensuring business requirements are translated into viable, scalable and secure product capabilities.
* **Lead product discovery and validation**, turning ideas into prototypes, MVPs, PoCs/PoVs and ultimately production-ready products.
* **Define product success measures**, including adoption, customer value, usage, quality, commercial impact and other relevant KPIs/OKRs.
* **Own release priorities and product readiness**, ensuring capabilities meet agreed acceptance criteria and deliver the intended customer outcome.
* **Drive product adoption**, working with Sales/GTM, Marketing, Delivery and customer teams on positioning, demonstrations, onboarding and feedback.
* **Engage directly with customers and senior stakeholders**, presenting product capabilities, capturing requirements and helping shape strategic product direction.
* **Monitor the AI and competitive landscape**, identifying opportunities created by Generative AI, Agentic AI and emerging enterprise technologies.
* **Balance innovation with practicality**, making informed decisions across customer value, technical complexity, cost, risk and time-to-market.
* **Maintain strong product governance**, ensuring decisions, priorities, requirements, dependencies and outcomes are clearly documented and communicated.
* **Champion continuous improvement**, using customer feedback, usage data and delivery learnings to evolve the product roadmap.

  

**Core Experience & Expertise**

* **7+ years of overall technology/product experience**, including at least **3+ years as a Product Owner, Product Manager or equivalent**.
* Demonstrable experience owning **B2B enterprise software, SaaS, digital platforms or technology products** from concept through delivery and adoption.
* Strong understanding of **Agile product development**, including epics, features, user stories, acceptance criteria, backlog prioritisation, sprint planning and release management.
* Experience working closely with **software engineering, architecture, UX/UI and delivery teams**.
* Strong capability in **product discovery, requirements analysis, stakeholder management and prioritisation**.
* Ability to translate complex customer and business requirements into **clear and actionable product requirements**.
* Working knowledge of **Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI and enterprise AI use cases**. Deep AI engineering expertise is not required, but the Product Owner must be sufficiently technically fluent to work credibly with AI architects and engineers.
* Experience using tools such as **Jira, Confluence and/or Azure DevOps** for product planning and delivery.
* Strong analytical ability with experience defining and monitoring **product KPIs, adoption metrics and business outcomes**.
* Excellent communication and presentation skills with the ability to engage **customers, engineers, senior stakeholders and commercial teams**.
* Commercial & Product Mindset
