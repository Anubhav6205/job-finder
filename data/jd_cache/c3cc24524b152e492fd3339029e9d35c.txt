AI/ML Engineers code and develop software that deploys ML models and algorithms in production. They communicate and present complex analytics results and concepts to leadership and internal stakeholders.

Employees in this role work with AI and/or ML technologies that may include:

* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* Natural Language Understanding (NLU)
* Semantic Understanding
* Intent Classification
* Computer Vision
* Deep Learning
* Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)

They apply deep learning technologies to give computers the capability to visualize, learn, and respond to complex situations. They may adapt machine learning to areas such as artificial intelligence, robotics, and other products that allow users to have an interactive experience.

They work with large-scale computing frameworks and data analysis systems. Employees in this role are expected to apply knowledge of:

* Experimental methodologies
* Statistics
* Optimization
* Probability theory
* Machine learning
* Coding for tool building and statistical analysis
* General-purpose software and statistical languages
