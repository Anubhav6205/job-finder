We are looking for a highly skilled and self-driven professional to join our Product Support team specializing in Oracle AIDP, Oracle Analytics Products, Oracle’s Big Data Service (BDS) , DFS, DIS, Data Catalog offerings. In this role, you will work directly with customers, engineering, and cross-functional teams to troubleshoot complex issues, drive resolution, and deliver exceptional support for both on-premise and cloud-based big data platforms. You will also play a critical role in supporting advanced data workflows, including AI/ML integrations, agentic framework adoption, and intelligent retrieval architectures.

As a Sr. Support Engineer, you will be the technical interface to customers, Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Value-Added Resellers (VARs) for resolution of problems related to the installation, recommended maintenance and use of Oracle products. Have an understanding of all Oracle products in their competencies and in-depth knowledge of several products and/or platforms. Also, you should be highly experienced in multiple platforms and be able to complete assigned duties with minimal direction from management. In this position, you will routinely act independently while researching and developing solutions to customer issues.

Experience with data integration tools (ODI/Informatica) and cloud data sources (FusionApps/BICC, Snowflake).
