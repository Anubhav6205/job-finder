**Job Description**
-------------------

**Business Analyst**

**Data & Analytics**

**LOCATION: Hyderabad, India**

**EXPERIENCE: 5–8 years (preferred)**

  

**ROLE OVERVIEW**

Translate business requirements into precise, testable data specifications for enterprise pipelines, KPI reporting and reusable data products.

  

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

* Gather functional requirements and document KPI calculations and expected outputs.
* Map source data fields to target structures and transformation rules.
* Perform SQL-based data profiling and investigate quality or completeness gaps.
* Document data dictionaries, acceptance criteria, PII classifications and access needs.
* Work with engineers on data models, semantic layers and data-product designs.
Prepare validation scenarios and support defects, business reviews and UAT.  

  
  

### **Requirements**

**REQUIRED SKILLS**

* Strong SQL and hands-on data analysis
* Requirements gathering and source-to-target mapping
* Data profiling, transformation rules and data-quality analysis
* ETL/ELT, data warehousing and analytics fundamentals
* Agile collaboration, clear documentation and stakeholder communication

**PREFERRED EXPERIENCE**

AWS Glue, Databricks, Delta Lake, Redshift, and data governance exposure.
