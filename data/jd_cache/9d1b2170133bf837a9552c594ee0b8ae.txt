**Project Execution Professionals (Engineer to Manager)**  
We are expanding our project engineering team, looking for result-oriented Mechanical/Chemical Engineering professionals to lead **Site Execution** for upcoming **Greenfield and Brownfield projects**.   
**Industry:** Fine Chemicals / Pharmaceuticals (API)   
**Qualification:** B.Tech / BE. - Mechanical or Chemical Engineering  
**Experience:** 5 to 12 Years  
**Positions Available:** Engineer / Senior Engineer / Assistant Manager / Deputy Manager/Manager for Site Execution.  
**Key Responsibilities:**

* **Site Execution & Infrastructure:** Drive end-to-end mechanical execution from concept to commissioning. Equipment involved are Reactors (SS, MSG, Hastelloy), storage tanks (SS, CS, MSGL), heat exchangers, ANFDs, RVPDs, Centrifuges and other process equipment

* **Vendor & Commissioning Management:** Coordinate with external consultants and contractors, manage technical bid evaluations and witness Factory/Site Acceptance Tests (**FAT/SAT**).
* **Quality & Regulatory Compliance:** Maintain audit-ready operations by strictly adhering to plant safety procedures, work permits, and electronic systems for **Change Control, Deviations, and CAPA**.
