position Description  
We are seeking a highly experienced and innovative Senior Generative AI & Agentic AI Engineer with 5+ years of hands-on experience in building, deploying, and scaling enterprise AI solutions. This role will be responsible for designing, developing, and operationalizing secure, production-grade Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Agentic AI solutions across enterprise environments. The ideal candidate will have deep expertise in the Microsoft Azure AI ecosystem, including Azure OpenAI, Microsoft Foundry, Azure AI Search, and modern AI orchestration frameworks. The position involves close collaboration with architects, engineers, security teams, data specialists, and business stakeholders to drive the adoption of scalable, secure, and responsible AI capabilities.

**Your future duties and responsibilities**

* Design, develop, and deploy enterprise-scale Generative AI solutions using Large Language Models (LLMs), Azure OpenAI, and Microsoft Foundry.
* Build and optimize prompt engineering, context engineering, structured outputs, and AI-driven workflows.
* Design and implement Agentic AI solutions including tool/function calling, state management, context retention, human-in-the-loop controls, and multi-agent orchestration.
* Develop secure Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures using embeddings, chunking strategies, vector search, hybrid search, semantic retrieval, reranking, and Azure AI Search.
* Integrate AI solutions with enterprise applications, APIs, databases, Microsoft services, and third-party systems using secure authentication and authorization mechanisms.
* Implement enterprise-grade security controls addressing prompt injection, data leakage, unauthorized agent actions, privacy regulations, auditability, and Responsible AI requirements.
* Build AI evaluation, observability, and monitoring frameworks covering hallucination detection, groundedness, retrieval quality, relevance, latency, accuracy, cost monitoring, and agent performance.
* Develop and maintain clean, scalable, production-ready code using Python and/or C#/.NET while following software engineering best practices.
* Establish CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, containerization, and cloud-native deployment strategies for AI applications.
* Research emerging AI technologies, evaluate new tools and frameworks, rapidly prototype innovative solutions, and contribute to enterprise AI strategy.
* Collaborate effectively with architecture, engineering, security, platform, data, and business teams throughout the solution lifecycle.
