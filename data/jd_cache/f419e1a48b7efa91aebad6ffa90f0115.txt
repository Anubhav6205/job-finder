**Job Summary**
---------------

NetApp's Chief Platform and Technology Office (CPTO) is hiring AI/ML Full Stack Engineers to design and build AI/ML-powered incubation projects that drive NetApp's technology strategy and business transformation. You'll own solutions end to end: the AI/ML core, scalable backend services in Python and Go, and React frontends that put AI capabilities in users' hands.The ideal candidate has hands-on experience deploying AI/ML solutions in production, with deep expertise in AI agents, LLM integration and optimization, RAG architectures, and modern ML frameworks. You should be proficient in Python and Go for backend development, and in React (with TypeScript) for the frontend. Strong experience with vector databases, MLOps practices, and containerized deployment (Docker/Kubernetes) is essential, as is familiarity with cloud AI/ML services (AWS SageMaker, Azure ML, GCP Vertex AI). A solid foundation in classical ML/DL, distributed systems, and software engineering best practices is also required. Experience in the storage or cloud domain is a strong plus. We value strong communicators who care about responsible AI and quality, and who deliver on deadlines.

**Job Requirements**
--------------------

* Design and build backend services and APIs (REST, gRPC, WebSocket/streaming) in Python and Go to serve AI/ML capabilities.
* Build large-scale, fault-tolerant, low-latency distributed systems and microservices for real-time AI inference.
* Build responsive React and TypeScript applications, including conversational interfaces, agent workflow dashboards, and data visualizations.
* Design and build intelligent AI agents that can reason, plan, use tools, and execute multi-step tasks.
* Architect and optimize LLM-powered systems, including token optimization, cost-efficient inference, prompt engineering frameworks, and caching mechanisms.
* Build and scale advanced RAG pipelines with hybrid search, re-ranking, agentic retrieval, and dynamic knowledge base integration.
* Develop and maintain MLOps pipelines for model training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring at scale.
* Fine-tune and customize foundation models for domain-specific use cases in storage, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise operations.
* Integrate frontends with backend and LLM services, supporting streaming responses, real-time updates, and complex application state.
* Own features end to end, from problem definition and architecture through development, testing, CI/CD, deployment, and early customer support.
* Contribute to POCs and incubation projects that shape NetApp's AI/ML roadmap and long-term product vision.
* Explore unconventional AI architectures, including multi-modal AI, edge inference, and distributed training.Collaborate cross-functionally with engineers, data scientists, designers, and product teams from problem definition through deployment.
* Stay current with the evolving AI/ML and full-stack landscape and drive adoption of emerging models, frameworks, and techniques that deliver business value.
