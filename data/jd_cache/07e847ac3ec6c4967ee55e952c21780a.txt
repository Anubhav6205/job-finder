SBS builds the 3D content library behind the Substation Design Suite, the physical modeling tools that substation designers use to build large BIM assemblies (3,000+ parts, 50,000+ instances). As a Senior Electrical Engineer, you will model and review high-value substation equipment and set the quality standard for the modeling team. You will also mentor junior modelers.

**Performance Objectives**

* Translate 2D manufacturer drawings (PDF, DWG, TIFF) into accurate 3D Inventor models for major substation equipment: **power transformers, circuit breakers, disconnect switches**, and related components such as bushings, arresters, and potential transformers.
* Produce **BIM-ready models** at a modified LOD 350, balancing detail with file size. Interfaces and connections are the priority.
* Apply SBS modeling standards.
* Keep models within file-size targets (e.g., transformers 100 MB, circuit breakers 40 MB, disconnect switches 20 MB).
* Create simplified multibody parts using Inventor’s BIM Simplify tool, and make sure models export cleanly to Revit with no surface bodies.

**Competitive Candidate Profile**

* BS in Electrical Engineering (or equivalent experience), with a specialty or significant background in **power systems and/or substations**
* 5+ years of 3D modeling experience, with demonstrated work on substation or utility equipment
* Advanced proficiency in **Autodesk Inventor** (assemblies, parts, work planes, iProperties, simplification)
* Strong skill in reading and interpreting 2D manufacturing and equipment drawings
* Working knowledge of substation equipment, NEMA pad and hole arrangements, grounding, and conduit entry conventions
* Experience building lightweight, performance-conscious models
* Strong attention to detail and the ability to work independently

Preferred Qualifications

* Experience with Revit and BIM workflows for utility or substation projects
* Familiarity with AutoCAD
* Experience mentoring or leading technical staff
* Prior work with substation design software or content libraries

What Success Looks Like

* Models meet standards on first review, with minimal rework
* Equipment models place and rotate correctly (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°) in SDS and Revit
* Junior modelers grow in quality and speed under your guidance

* Apply required metadata (manufacturer, model number, part numbers) and file naming conventions.
* Source and adapt vendor models or SBS Utility Content to meet standards, including reducing model size and fidelity.
* Review models from junior team members, perform QA/QC using the 3D Modeler checklist, and give constructive feedback.
* Interpret engineering drawings and resolve ambiguities with manufacturers and internal teams.
* Help refine templates, standards, and modeling documentation.
