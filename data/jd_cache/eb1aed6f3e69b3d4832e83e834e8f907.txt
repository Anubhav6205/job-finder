**About Position:**

  

We are seeking an experienced Senior Full Stack Developer – Agentic AI with 8 to 12 years of experience to design, develop, and deploy next-generation AI-powered enterprise applications. The ideal candidate will possess strong full-stack engineering expertise combined with hands-on experience in Generative AI, Agentic AI frameworks, and cloud-native technologies. You will play a key role in building intelligent applications, integrating Large Language Models (LLMs), implementing advanced RAG solutions, and delivering scalable AI-driven business platforms.

  

* **Role: Senior Full Stack Developer – Agentic AI**
* **Location: Pune**
* **Experience: 8 to 12 Years**
* **Job Type: Full-Time Employment**
  

**What You'll Do:**

  

* Design, develop, and maintain enterprise-grade full-stack applications using Java, Spring Boot, React, or Angular.
* Build and integrate Agentic AI solutions into business applications and workflows.
* Develop scalable APIs and microservices for AI-enabled products and platforms.
* Implement LLM-powered features, prompt engineering techniques, and intelligent automation capabilities.
* Design and optimize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using vector databases and embeddings.
* Develop secure, scalable, and resilient cloud-native applications on AWS.
* Build and deploy containerized applications using Docker and Kubernetes.
* Create automated testing frameworks across unit, integration, and API testing layers.
* Collaborate with product, architecture, and business teams to translate requirements into technical solutions.
* Implement CI/CD pipelines and engineering best practices for continuous delivery.
* Monitor application performance, reliability, security, and cost optimization.
* Contribute to AI governance, responsible AI practices, and enterprise compliance standards.
  

**Expertise You'll Bring:**

  

* 8 to 12 years of software development experience in enterprise application development.
* Strong proficiency in Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, and REST API development.
* Extensive experience with React or Angular and TypeScript for modern frontend development.
* Hands-on experience with relational and NoSQL databases.
* Strong expertise in Agentic AI architectures and intelligent workflow automation.
* Experience integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) into enterprise applications.
* Deep understanding of prompt engineering, context management, and AI orchestration frameworks.
* Experience with LangChain, LangGraph, Spring AI, Strands, or similar AI agent frameworks.
* Strong knowledge of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases, embeddings, and retrieval optimization techniques.
* Experience implementing LLM evaluation frameworks, guardrails, monitoring, and governance controls.
* Knowledge of MLOps/LLMOps practices including model versioning, prompt management, evaluation automation, and observability.
* Strong hands-on experience with AWS cloud services.
* Expertise in Docker, Kubernetes, microservices architecture, and cloud-native deployments.
* Experience with CI/CD pipelines, DevSecOps practices, and secure software delivery.
* Understanding of observability, resilience engineering, and performance optimization.
* Experience with event-driven architectures and enterprise integration patterns.
* Excellent problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
* Ability to mentor junior engineers and lead technical initiatives.
