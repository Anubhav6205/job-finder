**Job Title**

Workflow Engineer, 1

**Job Description**

**AI Workflow & Automation Engineering**

* Prototype and productionize new AI-driven automations.
* Design and implement Slack features including slash commands, message actions, interactive modals, triage flows, and user-friendly bot behaviors.
* Develop frameworks, templates, and reusable automation components to accelerate future workflow builds — both for code-first builds and for our low-code stack (n8n, Zapier, Google Workspace Studio).
* Raise concerns when a proposed approach has technical risk, and help shape a better path forward.

**Retrieval, Data Pipelines & AI Quality**

* Build and refine retrieval pipelines, vector stores, data-sync workflows, hashing/diffing systems, and content preprocessing pipelines.
* Implement structured outputs, evaluation loops, hallucination prevention methods, and AI quality assurance systems.
* Maintain and improve RAG pipelines using content from Confluence, Google Sheets, Jira, Slack logs, and other internal knowledge sources.

**Backend, Reliability & Production Readiness**

* Build and maintain backend services, REST APIs, and serverless orchestrators powering our internal automation platforms.
* Implement logging, monitoring, retry logic, structured error handling, and observability to keep our automations production-grade.
* Contribute to CI/CD workflows, testing practices, and version-controlled deployment patterns.

**Cross-Team Enablement & Expansion**

* Partner with teams across People Inc. to identify processes that can benefit from intelligent automation or AI augmentation.
* Translate business needs into reliable automated workflows and internal tools.
* Contribute to documentation, change management procedures, and the overall evolution of People Inc.'s internal AI platform.
* Take initiative to propose new AI-enabled enhancements, systems, or assistants the organization has not yet defined.

**What You'll Likely Work On in Your First 6 Months**

* **Dottie as a service**: help extract Dottie (our L1 tech support triage agent) from the existing in-process #it-techsupport implementation into a standalone API, define quality metrics, and onboard at least one new consuming surface.
* **Reusable build patterns**: turn our "Slack workflow + AI matcher + Apps Script" pattern into proper templates with example code, intake forms, and stakeholder-facing docs so future builds ship faster.
* **Stack-level upgrades**: identify where our current Sheets-based audit logs, Apps Script glue, and ad-hoc retrieval should graduate to more durable infrastructure, and help drive the migration where it makes sense.

**Required Skills**

**Background**

* 3+ years of professional software engineering experience, with meaningful exposure to AI/LLM-based systems.
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field — or equivalent practical experience.

**Core Engineering**

* Solid experience with Python and JavaScript; comfort working in Apps Script, or willingness to become fluent.
* Comfort with REST APIs, webhooks, and event-driven integrations.
* Experience building and shipping services that have run in production.

**AI & LLM**

* Hands-on experience with LLMs, OpenAI APIs, embeddings, retrieval pipelines (RAG), and prompt engineering.
* Familiarity with data normalization, indexing, content segmentation, and retrieval basics.

**Cloud & Systems**

* Experience with Google Cloud, particularly Cloud Functions (Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, IAM a plus).
* Experience building on Slack via the SDK, Bolt, or Events API, including interactive UI components.
* Experience integrating with Jira and similar APIs.

**Preferred Skills (Not Required)**

* Experience with low-code or iPaaS tools (n8n, Zapier, Make) used alongside code.
* Background in agentic workflows or LangChain.
* Exposure to observability tools and CI/CD pipelines.
* Experience designing systems with reliability and a real user-experience focus.
* Experience operating internal tools — running them, watching usage, iterating — not just building them.
* Knowledge of Confluence APIs, content hashing, or knowledge retrieval.
* Experience containerizing AI workloads (Docker) or working with vector databases.

**Who Thrives in This Role**

* Engineers who think in systems, not isolated tasks.
* Builders who enjoy being a strong technical contributor on a small team and want their work to ship and get used.
* Developers who care about reliability, uptime, and high-quality user experience.
* Technologists who stay curious about emerging AI advancements and adopt them responsibly.
* Problem-solvers energized by complex, cross-functional technical challenges.
