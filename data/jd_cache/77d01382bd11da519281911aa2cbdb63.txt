**About Position:**

  

We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Generative AI Engineer – Agentic AI with 8 to 12 years of experience in designing, developing, and deploying enterprise-grade AI solutions. The ideal candidate will have strong expertise in Python, Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI frameworks, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Azure OpenAI Services. In this role, you will build intelligent AI agents, automate complex business workflows, and develop scalable AI-powered solutions that enhance operational efficiency, accuracy, and business outcomes.

  

* **Role: Generative AI Engineer**
* **Location: All Persistent Locations**
* **Experience: 8 to 12 Years**
* **Job Type: Full-Time Employment**
  

**What You'll Do:**

  

* Design, develop, and deploy enterprise-grade Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions.
* Build intelligent AI agents capable of executing multi-step workflows and business processes.
* Integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) into enterprise applications and business platforms.
* Design and implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using vector databases and embeddings.
* Develop scalable backend services and APIs using Python and FastAPI.
* Create and optimize prompts to improve model accuracy, relevance, and response quality.
* Conduct LLM evaluation, benchmarking, and performance testing to ensure reliable AI outputs.
* Build agent orchestration workflows using frameworks such as LangChain and similar AI orchestration platforms.
* Deploy, monitor, and maintain AI applications on Microsoft Azure and Azure OpenAI environments.
* Implement observability, logging, monitoring, and governance frameworks for AI services.
* Containerize and deploy applications using Docker and Kubernetes.
* Collaborate with business stakeholders, product teams, and engineering teams to identify AI-driven opportunities.
* Optimize AI system performance, scalability, security, and cost efficiency.
* Ensure compliance with responsible AI practices and enterprise governance standards.
* Stay updated with emerging trends in Generative AI, LLMs, Agentic AI, and cloud-native AI technologies.
  

**Expertise You'll Bring:**

  

* 8 to 12 years of experience in software engineering, AI solution development, or intelligent automation.
* Strong hands-on programming expertise in Python.
* Experience developing and deploying enterprise-grade Generative AI applications.
* Deep understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering, and prompt optimization techniques.
* Hands-on experience with Agentic AI architectures, multi-agent systems, and workflow orchestration.
* Experience building AI applications using LangChain, LangGraph, or similar GenAI frameworks.
* Strong knowledge of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), embeddings, vector databases, and semantic search.
* Experience performing LLM evaluation, testing, validation, and performance optimization.
* Expertise in integrating Azure OpenAI and Azure AI services into enterprise applications.
* Hands-on experience with FastAPI and API-driven application development.
* Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and containerized application deployments.
* Knowledge of AI observability, monitoring, governance, and responsible AI practices.
* Understanding of cloud-native architectures and distributed systems on Microsoft Azure.
* Experience implementing security, reliability, and scalability best practices for AI applications.
* Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices for AI workloads.
* Good understanding of Machine Learning concepts and model lifecycle management.
* Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
* Ability to work with cross-functional teams and translate business requirements into AI-driven solutions.
