**About Us**
We are a global climate technologies company engineered for sustainability. We create sustainable and efficient residential, commercial and industrial spaces through HVACR technologies. We protect temperature-sensitive goods throughout the cold chain. And we bring comfort to people globally. Best-in-class engineering, design and manufacturing combined with category-leading brands in compression, controls, software and monitoring solutions result in next-generation climate technology that is built for the needs of the world ahead.
  
  
Whether you are a professional looking for a career change, an undergraduate student exploring your first opportunity, or recent graduate with an advanced degree, we have opportunities that will allow you to innovate, be challenged and make an impact. Join our team and start your journey today!
  
  
Key Responsibilities
  
  
AI, Generative AI & Agentic Solutions:-
  
  

* Design, develop, and deploy enterprise AI solutions using Azure AI, Azure OpenAI, and Generative AI technologies.
* Build multimodal AI applications leveraging text, images, video, audio, telemetry, and IoT data.
* Develop AI-powered troubleshooting, diagnostics, workflow automation, and intelligent copilots.
* Design and implement AI Agents, MCP Servers, and agentic workflows to drive business productivity and automation.

RAG & Knowledge Intelligence:-
  
  

* Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions using Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Search.
* Develop enterprise knowledge systems using embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, and document retrieval.
* Integrate product, engineering, and support documentation into AI-powered experiences to improve accuracy and user outcomes.

Intelligent Diagnostics & Insights:-
  
  

* Develop AI solutions for product diagnostics, health scoring, anomaly detection, and predictive insights.
* Build summarization and recommendation capabilities for service logs, incidents, alarms, technical documentation, and operational workflows.
* Deliver actionable insights that improve troubleshooting, maintenance, and decision-making.

Application Development & Cloud Engineering:-
  
  

* Develop AI-enabled applications and APIs using Python, FastAPI, Azure AI Services, .NET, Angular, and React.
* Design scalable microservices, agent endpoints, and enterprise integrations.
* Deploy and manage AI solutions across Azure services including Azure OpenAI, AI Search, AI Foundry, AKS, APIM, and Container Apps.

AI Quality, Security & Optimization:-
  
  

* Establish AI evaluation frameworks to measure accuracy, relevance, latency, groundedness, and user satisfaction.
* Optimize AI solutions for performance, scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency.
* Implement secure-by-design practices, AI governance controls, identity management, and compliance standards.

Cross-Functional Collaboration:-
  
  

* Partner with Product, Architecture, Security, Data, and Engineering teams to deliver enterprise AI solutions.
* Contribute to technical design, innovation initiatives, architecture reviews, and AI best practices.
* Stay current with emerging trends in Generative AI, Agentic AI, multimodal AI, and cloud technologies.

What Success Looks Like:-
  
  
Deliver secure, scalable, and business-impacting AI solutions that enhance troubleshooting, automate workflows, generate operational insights, and accelerate innovation across the organization. Ideal candidates combine deep expertise in
**Azure AI, RAG, Prompt Engineering, FastAPI, and Agentic AI**
