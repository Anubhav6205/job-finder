**About Technova Global**

Technova Global is building an AI dubbing studio in Hyderabad. We dub films and series into new languages using ComposerX, our in-house AI dubbing tool. Our team includes dubbing adapters, QC reviewers, audio engineers and operations staff, and it is growing.

**About the role**

We are looking for an HR Executive to run day-to-day HR for the studio – from hiring and joining to attendance, payroll inputs and exits. You will work directly with management and be the first point of contact for every team member.

**What you will do**

* Run hiring end to end: post jobs, screen CVs, schedule phone and in-person rounds, follow up, issue offers and manage joining
* Hire for creative and technical roles – dubbing adapters, QC reviewers, audio engineers, operations staff
* Onboarding: collect documents, prepare joining paperwork, set up ID and induction
* Maintain attendance, leave and shift records
* Prepare monthly payroll inputs; handle PF, ESI and PT basics with our accountant / consultant
* Keep employee files and HR records complete and up to date
* Handle staff queries, engagement activities and exit formalities
* Share simple HR reports (hiring status, headcount, attendance) with management

**You need**

* 2–4 years of experience as an HR Executive or HR Generalist
* Experience hiring for media, studio, creative or content roles (strongly preferred)
* Working knowledge of payroll, PF and ESI
* Good spoken and written English
* Comfort with Excel / Google Sheets, email and job portals (Indeed, Naukri etc.)
* Graduate; MBA / PGDM in HR preferred
* Organised, discreet with personal data, and good with people

**Selection process**

* Apply with your CV (CV is a must)
* Shortlisted candidates get a telephonic interview
* Final round: in-person interview at our Hyderabad office
