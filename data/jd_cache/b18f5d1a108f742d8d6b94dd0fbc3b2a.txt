About the Role

We are looking for a highly skilled engineer to build the core of our AI products. You will design and ship production-grade AI agents, multi-agent systems, RAG pipelines and automated workflows that solve real business problems. This is a hands-on, high-ownership role for someone who has already built agentic systems and wants to push them further.

Key Responsibilities

AI Agents & Chatbots

Design, build and deploy AI agents and conversational chatbots for WhatsApp, web and voice channels

Architect multi-agent systems where specialised agents collaborate, hand off tasks, use tools and make decisions

Build agents with tool/function calling, memory, planning and reasoning, and reliable fallback and human-handoff logic

Handle multilingual conversations (English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil and more)

RAG Systems

Build and optimise Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines: document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, re-ranking and grounded answers

Work with vector databases and improve retrieval accuracy, latency and cost

Reduce hallucinations and evaluate answer quality with structured testing and evaluation frameworks

Workflow Automation

Design and build end-to-end automated workflows covering lead capture, follow-ups, scoring, appointment booking, invoicing and CRM updates

Integrate the WhatsApp Business API, Google Calendar/Workspace, payment gateways, telephony and CRM tools

Build with orchestration and automation tools (n8n, Make, Zapier) as well as custom code where needed

Core Engineering

Develop scalable backend services, APIs, webhooks and web applications

Design databases, queues and event-driven systems that stay reliable under production load

Own deployment, monitoring, logging, security and cost optimisation of AI systems

Write clean, tested, documented code, conduct code reviews and mentor junior developers
