Job Description – Full Stack Developer – MEAN/MERN, Mobile & Agentic AI**About the Company**

**TechVibrant Innovative Solutions Pvt Ltd** is a Bangalore-based technology company focused on building **enterprise software solutions, digital products, mobile applications, and Industry 4.0 solutions** for businesses across multiple industries.

We work closely with our customers to understand complex business problems and build scalable, practical technology solutions that improve productivity, customer experience, and business outcomes.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a **Full Stack Developer with 3–4 years of experience** who is comfortable working across multiple layers of the technology stack.

You will be responsible for building and enhancing **web and mobile applications**, developing APIs and backend services, working with databases and integrations, and contributing to end-to-end product development.

This is not a role limited to frontend or backend development. You will have opportunities to work across the **frontend, backend, mobile, database, and cloud layers**.

**Forward-Deployed Engineering & AI Focus**  
**This is a forward-deployed engineering role. You will work closely with customers, business/product stakeholders and internal teams to understand real-world problems, translate them into specifications, build and deploy working solutions, and iterate based on feedback. The role requires strong hands-on engineering skills together with an ability to use AI coding agents and agentic AI capabilities to accelerate the software development lifecycle.**

**What Will You Work On?**

As part of the engineering team, you will:

· Develop and maintain modern **web applications using React.js / Angular**

· Build scalable backend services and **REST APIs using Node.js and Express.js**

· Develop and integrate **Android and iOS mobile applications**

· Design and work with databases such as **MongoDB, PostgreSQL and MySQL**

· Build integrations with third-party services and external APIs

· Develop new features and enhancements for existing platforms

· Understand existing codebases and independently implement new functionality

· Debug, troubleshoot and optimize applications across different technology layers

· Work with cloud infrastructure, containers and CI/CD pipelines

· Collaborate with product, design and engineering teams to deliver complete features

**AI-Native & Agentic Engineering**

We are building an **AI-native engineering culture**, where AI is an integral part of the software development lifecycle.

Our engineering processes increasingly use AI-assisted and agentic workflows for:

· Requirement analysis and clarification

· Specification and **Spec-Driven Development (SDD)**

· Architecture and technical design

· Code generation and implementation

· Automated testing

· Debugging and issue resolution

· Code reviews and refactoring

· Documentation

· Development workflow automation

You will be expected to learn and work with **AI coding agents and AI-assisted development tools** as part of your daily engineering workflow.

We are looking for engineers who are curious about how AI can make software development **faster, more reliable, and more scalable**, while maintaining strong engineering practices.
