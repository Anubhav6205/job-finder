**GenAI Engineer III - Pursuits**

As a GenAI Engineer, you will actively engage in your engineering craft, taking a hands-on approach to multiple high-visibility projects. Your expertise will be pivotal in delivering solutions that delight customers and users, while also driving tangible value for Deloitte's business investments. You will leverage your extensive GenAI & engineering craftmanship across multiple programming languages and modern frameworks, consistently demonstrating your strong track record in delivering high-quality, outcome-focused solutions. The ideal candidate will be a dependable team player, collaborating with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and deploy advanced software solutions.

This individual will support the Pursuits team in identifying areas where AI solutions can be provided and will work with technical teams to develop and deploy these solutions.

**Work you'll do**

As a Gen AI Engineer on the Pursuits team, you will be responsible for:

* Supporting the Pursuits team in identifying business needs and opportunities for artificial intelligence (AI) and generative AI solutions; partner with technical teams to develop, deploy, and support those solutions.
* Lead requirements analysis, component design, development, unit testing, integrations, and production support for software products.
* Translate business and user needs, architectures, and user-experience and user-interface designs into technical specifications and scalable code.
* Use rapid experimentation and iterative delivery to develop lean, maintainable solutions that address customer and business outcomes.
* Collaborate with product management, experience, delivery, and engineering teams to evaluate technical trade-offs, improve quality, and reduce technical debt.

**The team**

The Pursuit Management team is a full-service group of pursuit managers, graphic designers, editors, project managers/coordinators, multimedia specialists, and researchers who directly support client service teams in differentiating Deloitte and winning more business. We deliver support throughout the proposal process, from scoping meetings and written proposal submission through orals preparation and debriefing. This includes developing strategic win themes and written, oral, and visual messaging used consistently in all prospect interactions and pursuit deliverables, as well as facilitating a smooth, rigorous pursuit process.

**Location**: Hyderabad

**Shift Timings:** 2 PM to 11 PM

**Qualifications**

**Required:**

* A bachelor’s degree in computer science, software engineering, or a related discipline.
* 4+ years of experience with artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), with the last 2 years focused on generative artificial intelligence (GenAI), including OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, LangChain, AI agents, vector databases, prompt engineering, and fine-tuning.
* 7 – 9 years’ experience with object-oriented programming, data structures, algorithms, code instrumentation, Python, R, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Julia, machine learning libraries, natural language processing (NLP), big-data technologies, Angular, React, Node.js, C#, .NET Core, Java, Go, and SQL/NoSQL databases.
* Experience developing cloud-native applications using function-as-a-service (FaaS), platform-as-a-service (PaaS), or microservices on Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS), or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
* Experience with Extreme Programming (XP), Lean, Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe), DevSecOps, site reliability engineering (SRE), Azure DevOps (ADO), GitHub, and SonarQube.
* You must reside within a commutable distance of your assigned office with the ability to commute daily, if required, without the need for overnight accommodation.
* You can expect to co-locate on average 2 times a week with variations based on types of work/projects and client locations.

**Preferred:**

An advanced degree, such as a Master of Science (MS)
