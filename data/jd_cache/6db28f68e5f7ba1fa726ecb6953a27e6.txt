**About the Role**
------------------

We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated AI Engineer to join our growing team. In this role, you will design, develop, and deploy AI driven solutions that solve real-world problems at scale. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to transition models from research to production, ensuring performance, fairness, and explainability.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

* **AI Solution Development:** Design and develop AI models and algorithms from scratch, utilizing techniques like machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing (NLP), and computer vision.
* **Model Management:** Train, test, fine-tune, and optimize AI and machine learning models for performance, accuracy, and scalability.
* **Deployment and Integration:** Deploy AI solutions into production environments using APIs, microservices, Docker, and Kubernetes. Integrate these AI capabilities with existing software systems and applications
* **Data Handling:** Collect, analyze, and interpret large, complex datasets to derive insights and ensure data integrity for model training.
* **Collaboration:** Work closely with cross-functional teams, including data scientists, software engineers, product managers, and stakeholders, to define project goals and align AI strategies with business objectives.
* **Monitoring and Maintenance:** Manage the AI development process (MLOps), monitor model performance in real-time, troubleshoot issues, and implement continuous improvements.
