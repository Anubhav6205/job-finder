We are currently seeking a GenAI Sr. Developer to join our team in Bangalore, Karnātaka (IN-KA), India (IN).
  
  
Key Responsibilities:
  
  

* Develop, and implement AI/ML and Generative AI solutions across various domains, including prediction systems, recommendation engines, text analytics, computer vision, chatbots, and content intelligence platforms.
* Build and deploy advanced ML models using frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Spacy, and scikit-learn.
* Apply deep knowledge of statistics, machine learning, NLP, and generative models including GANs and transformers.
* Work with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) techniques.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and implement end-to-end AI pipelines, from data ingestion to model deployment and monitoring.
* Support the development of GenAI use cases, prototypes, and POCs tailored to specific industry requirements.
* Communicate technical concepts clearly and effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
* Engage with clients to understand business needs and deliver customized AI/ML solutions that drive value.
* Stay current with trends and advancements in AI, GenAI, LLMs, and related fields.
* Contribute to the development of frameworks, capabilities, and features for NTT DATA’s global GenAI platform and TechHub.

Minimum Qualifications:
  
  

* Masters in AI from a reputed College / University
* Demonstrated using Python and relevant packages for ML and data science in multiple real projects that is not limited to their own personal development environment.
* Strong fundamentals in building GenAI solutions, preferably using Agentic frameworks.
* Deep knowledge in all of the following areas:
* Predictive Analytics
* Machine Learning and Deep Learning
* Generative AI
* Agentic systems
* ML Ops / AI Infrastructure
* Strong understanding of LLM technologies, embedding models, cloud-based AI services, and AI/ML operations.
* Hands-on experience with cloud-based AI platforms, including Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex Gen AI, and AWS AI services.

Soft Skills:
  
  

* Strong problem-solving skills and critical thinking.
* Effective communicator capable of translating complex AI topics to stakeholders.
* Self-driven, collaborative, and adaptable to fast-paced environments.
