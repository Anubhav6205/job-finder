**Job Summary:**

As a Data Engineering Consultant specializing in GCP Architecture, you will be responsible for **designing, building, and optimizing cloud-native data solutions on Google Cloud Platform**. Your typical day will involve **architecting scalable data pipelines using BigQuery, Dataflow, and Pub/Sub, integrating generative AI capabilities via the Gemini API**, and deploying ML workflows through Vertex AI Pipelines. You will work closely with data architects and engineering teams to translate business requirements into production-grade GCP data architectures and deliver robust GCP solutions.

**Roles & Responsibilities:**

- Design and implement end-to-end data architectures on GCP leveraging BigQuery, Dataflow, Pub/Sub, Vertex AI Pipelines, and Gemini API.

- Build scalable, serverless streaming and batch data pipelines using Apache Beam and Cloud Dataflow.

- Integrate Gemini API for generative AI use cases within data products and analytical workflows.

- Orchestrate ML training and inference pipelines on Vertex AI, ensuring reliability and performance at scale.

- Configure real-time event-driven architectures using Pub/Sub for data ingestion and messaging.

- Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, and client stakeholders to define data platform requirements.

- Conduct performance tuning, cost optimization, and governance reviews for GCP environments.

- Stay current with GCP product releases and AI/ML advancements, recommending adoption where impactful.
