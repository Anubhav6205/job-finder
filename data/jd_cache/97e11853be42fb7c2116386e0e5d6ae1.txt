**What you’ll do:**
-------------------

Eaton is seeking a hands-on AI Agent & Automation Lead to join the AI Enablement, Literacy & Adoption team within AI & Data Governance, part of Eaton's AI & Data Management organization, based in Pune, India. This is primarily an new engineering role, with front-line enablement and training coordination as a secondary focus. The Lead will design, build and run the agents, workflow automations and standard services that let Eaton's AI adoption program scale.

The role has a dual focus. First, it owns day-to-day AI adoption service delivery: license provisioning, reclaim, transfers, waitlist and consumption credit management for enterprise AI assistants, one intake for leadership and business requests, adoption and consumption reporting, and in-region (IST) office hours and training operations. Second, it owns and delivers the enablement teams automation roadmap, so manual effort goes down measurably every quarter. The Lead will also be the first owner of the enterprise skills and agents library, collecting, reviewing, tagging and publishing reusable skills, prompts and agents into Eaton's AI governance technologies every sprint, and will coach employees, AI Ambassadors and AI focal points on agent development.

Essential responsibilities include:

Run - AI adoption service delivery  

* Own day-to-day AI adoption operations for Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Cowork and other enterprise AI assistants, including license provisioning, reclaim, transfers, waitlist and consumption credit-tier management, so routine licensing and service operations no longer depend on leadership, the specialists or front-line training coordination.
* Run the ServiceNow request service end to end (catalog, approval routing to managers, AI focal points and Finance, fulfillment, notifications and status) and make it the single front door, so requests stop arriving by email, chat and direct message.
* Build and run one intake, triage and prioritization process for leadership and business requests, with clear service levels, escalation paths and reporting. Point requesters to existing skills, agents and assets before anything new is built.

Copilot effectiveness - agents and automation  

* Design, build, test, deploy and support agents and workflow automations that answer and fulfill requests arriving through Teams, email and ServiceNow, using Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Automate, Microsoft 365 Copilot extensibility and Azure AI Foundry.
* Build and tune support agents grounded in help articles, policies and training content, so most routine questions about AI tools are answered without a person and the rest hand off cleanly to second-line support.
* Build agents and automations for the team's own operations, such as status reporting generated from Jira, meeting preparation and recaps, and release-note drafts from vendor product roadmaps.

Skills and agents library  

* Act as the first owner of the enterprise skills and agents library: set up standard work to collect, review, tag and publish reusable skills, prompts and agents into Eaton's AI governance technologies every sprint.
* Build agent-assisted intake and tagging so submissions from AI Ambassadors, business units and IT teams arrive described, classified and ready for review.
* Define reusable patterns and templates so one-off leadership builds become repeatable, shareable solutions, such as executive briefing skills, function playbooks and report-generation skills.
