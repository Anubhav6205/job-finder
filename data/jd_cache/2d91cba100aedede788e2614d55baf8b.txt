**About Northern Trust**

As a global leader in innovative wealth management, asset servicing, asset management and banking services, Northern Trust (Nasdaq: NTRS) is proud to guide the world’s most successful individuals, families, corporations and institutions.

  

Since 1889, we have aligned our efforts with our three guiding Principles That Endure: Service, Expertise, and Integrity. Together, they reflect the three cornerstones of business conduct which we strive to instill in our employees, whom we call partners, and to provide to our clients and the communities we serve worldwide.

  

With more than 135 years of financial experience and over 24,000 partners, we serve the world’s most sophisticated clients using leading technology and exceptional service.

  
  

**Role Overview**

We are looking for a Lead Software Engineer with 10+ years of software development experience and strong hands-on expertise in full-stack development, AI/ML, and Azure cloud technologies. The role requires a hands-on technical engineer who can design scalable solutions, contribute to development, and guide engineering teams through successful delivery.

**Required Skills**

* 10+ years of software development experience.

* Strong hands-on experience in Full Stack using React JS using Micro Front End framework, Node JS, TypeScript, Python, and SQL Server.
* Practical experience developing and integrating AI/ML and Generative AI solutions.
* Hands-on experience with RAG, vector databases/vector search, prompt engineering, AI agents, and MCP servers using Lang Graph / Lang Chain.
* Experience with Microsoft Azure and cloud-native application development.
* Experience with REST APIs, microservices, and distributed application architectures.
* Strong understanding of CI/CD, Git, automated testing, and DevOps practices.
* Strong problem-solving, technical leadership, communication, and mentoring skills.

**Preferred Skills**

* Experience with Azure OpenAI and Azure AI services.
* Experience with Docker, Kubernetes/AKS, and cloud-native deployment.
* Knowledge of AI solution evaluation, observability, security, and responsible AI practices.
