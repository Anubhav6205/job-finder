**About this role:**

As a Customer Success Engineer, you'll own technical product support for Refold's customers. When an integration fails, a workflow behaves unexpectedly, or a customer is blocked, you're the one who finds out why and gets them running again.

This is a hands-on support role. Most of your time goes into triaging tickets, debugging integrations across customer systems, and working with Engineering on root-cause fixes. You'll also turn what you learn into docs, tooling and product feedback, so the same issue doesn't come back.

**What you'll do:**

**Own the support queue**

* Be the first technical responder for customer issues raised through [ticketing tool], email and shared Slack channels.
* Triage by severity and business impact, and meet response and resolution SLAs.
* Keep customers updated with clear, honest status until every issue is closed.

**Debug integrations end to end**

* Investigate failed workflow runs, auth errors, rate limits, field-mapping and schema mismatches, webhook failures, and data-sync gaps between systems like SAP, Oracle, Workday, NetSuite and Salesforce.
* Read logs, API traces, and run history to isolate root causes, and reproduce issues in test environments.
* Fix configuration and workflow issues yourself; escalate product bugs to Engineering with clear repro steps, impact and evidence.

**Handle incidents**

* Act as first responder on high-severity incidents and coordinate between Engineering and the customer.
* Write post-incident summaries and root-cause analyses that customers can trust.
* Join an on-call rotation [with partial overlap with US business hours].

**Make support scale**

* Write and maintain troubleshooting guides, runbooks, FAQs and help-centre articles.
* Spot repeat issues and push for product fixes, clearer error messages or self-serve tooling.
* Use AI agents and automation, including Refold itself, to speed up triage, diagnosis and routine support work.

**Be the voice of the customer**

* Tag and trend tickets, and share top issues and feature requests with Product every sprint/month.
* Help customer teams adopt good support practices and self-serve resources after go-live.

**What we're looking for:**

* 2–5 years in technical support, product support, support engineering or integration engineering at a B2B SaaS company.
* A strong grasp of REST APIs, webhooks, JSON and HTTP, plus authentication methods like OAuth 2.0 and API keys.
* Comfort debugging with Postman, cURL and browser dev tools, and reading application logs.
* Working knowledge of SQL and a scripting language such as Python or JavaScript; you can read code to follow what a workflow is doing.
* An understanding of data mapping and transformation between systems with different data models.
* Experience with a support or ticketing tool such as Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk or Jira Service Management.
* Clear, calm written communication, especially when a customer's production workflow is down.
* A bias for ownership: you follow an issue through to resolution instead of handing it off.

**Nice to have:**

* Hands-on experience with enterprise systems such as SAP, Oracle, Workday, NetSuite, Salesforce or HubSpot.
* Experience with iPaaS or embedded integration platforms such as Workato, MuleSoft, Boomi, Paragon or Merge.
* Familiarity with LLM-based or agentic workflows and how to debug them.
* Exposure to cloud and observability tools: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Datadog, Grafana or Sentry.
* Experience at an early-stage startup where you helped build support processes from scratch.
