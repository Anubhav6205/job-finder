**Job Description:**

Role overview:

AI Engineer Apprentice learns to support AI products, data agents, copilots, chatbots, RAG, prompt evaluation & responsible AI.

Apprenticeship Duration: 12 Months

Learning: Python, SQL, APIs, GenAI, LLMs, embeddings, vector search, RAG, agents, privacy, safety & governance.

Responsibilities: Assist with AI prototypes, document ingestion, embeddings, retrieval & prompts; support integrations, response validation, testing & documentation.

Deliverables: AI prototype component, prompt test set, RAG evaluation notes & responsible AI checklist.

Eligibility: Must meet NATS registration & organization-specific apprenticeship eligibility requirements.

Guardrails: Supervised developmental role; production-critical ownership only after competency and mentor approval. Conversion depend on business need, policy & performance
