**Role Overview**

Aptlogica is seeking an AI and machine learning data scientist with experience in time-series forecasting, distributed data processing, statistical modeling, and Generative AI. You will build forecasting solutions on large datasets and combine predictive models with Generative AI reasoning to produce business insights.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Build scalable data-processing pipelines with PySpark on Google Cloud Dataproc.
* Perform feature engineering, time-window aggregation, and KPI calculation in PySpark.
* Conduct feature selection and feature-importance analysis.
* Develop and optimize time-series forecasting models using ARIMA, SARIMA, SARIMAX, and XGBoost.
* Work with BigQuery and SQL-based datasets.
* Integrate Gemini into analytics and AI workflows.
* Develop large language model applications with LangChain and apply prompt engineering to produce contextual business insights.
* Combine forecasting outputs with Generative AI reasoning.
* Collaborate with business and engineering teams to move AI solutions into production.

  

**Required Qualifications**

* 3-7 years of overall professional experience, including 1-3 years of directly relevant hands-on experience.
* Strong Python, PySpark, and PySpark ML skills, including experience with Google Cloud Dataproc.
* Experience with scikit-learn, statistical modeling, and time-series forecasting.
* Hands-on knowledge of ARIMA, SARIMA, SARIMAX, and XGBoost.
* Experience with BigQuery, SQL, and GCP data and AI services.
* Understanding of large language models, LangChain, and prompt engineering.

  

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience building production machine learning pipelines.
* Experience with model monitoring and evaluation.
* Experience combining Generative AI with predictive analytics.
* Experience working with large-scale enterprise datasets.

  

**Interview Process**

The selection process includes one interview round with Aptlogica, followed by one or two client interview rounds.
