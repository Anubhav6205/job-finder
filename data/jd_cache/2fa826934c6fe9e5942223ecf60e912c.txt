**Role & responsibilities**

Job Summary  
We are seeking a skilled Python / Shell Scripting Engineer to design, develop, maintain, and automate business processes, system administration tasks, and application support activities. The ideal candidate should have strong expertise in Python development, Unix/Linux environments, Shell scripting, and automation tools to improve operational efficiency and reliability.

Key Responsibilities  
Python Development & Automation

. Design, develop, test, and maintain Python based applications and automation solutions.  
. Create reusable scripts and tools for operational and business process automation.  
. Develop APIs, ETL processes, and data integration solutions using Python.  
. Analyze business requirements and translate them into technical solutions.  
. Optimize existing Python code for performance, scalability, and maintainability.  
. Troubleshoot and resolve application and automation related issues.

Shell Scripting & Unix/Linux Administration

. Develop and maintain Shell scripts (Bash, Korn Shell, or Unix Shell) for automation and system administration.  
. Automate routine operational tasks, monitoring activities, and maintenance jobs.  
. Manage files, cron jobs, batch processing, and job scheduling activities.  
. Troubleshoot Unix/Linux system issues and perform root cause analysis.  
. Monitor server health, system resources, and application performance.

Production Support & Operations

. Provide L2/L3 support for production environments.  
. Investigate incidents, resolve application issues, and ensure minimal business impact.  
. Coordinate with infrastructure, database, and application teams for issue resolution.  
. Support deployment, release, and change management activities.  
. Maintain operational documentation, runbooks, and SOPs.
