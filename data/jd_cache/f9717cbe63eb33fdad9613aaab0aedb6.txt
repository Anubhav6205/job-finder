**About the job**
-----------------

Google's software engineers develop the next-generation technologies that change how billions of users connect, explore, and interact with information and one another. Our products need to handle information at massive scale, and extend well beyond web search. We're looking for engineers who bring fresh ideas from all areas, including information retrieval, distributed computing, large-scale system design, networking and data storage, security, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, UI design and mobile; the list goes on and is growing every day. As a software engineer, you will work on a specific project critical to Google’s needs with opportunities to switch teams and projects as you and our fast-paced business grow and evolve. We need our engineers to be versatile, display leadership qualities and be enthusiastic to take on new problems across the full-stack as we continue to push technology forward.

Join Google Search AI and work at the intersection of industry leading
  
global scale. We apply breakthrough research, leveraging models like Gemini, to launch high-impact products that help billions ask any question and get things done. If you want to drive the future of AI in Search and work with world-class experts to deliver tangible, global impact and continuous innovation, this is the place.

**Responsibilities**
--------------------

* Make models fast and cheap by picking speculative decoding, prefix caching, sharding, and quantization bets, proving them with load tests and live experiments, and landing them.
* Get to know every new Gemini revision better than its release notes do, analyze what changed in its architecture, behaviour, and cost to turn that into a serving topology that gets the most quality out of every TPU chip.
* Build the guardrails that let us move fast without breaking Search: configure checks, pre-submits, monitor and fast rollback, fix the root cause, not just the alert.
* Qualify new checkpoints, shape evaluation and autorater strategy, and explain why the new revision is suddenly slower, and what steps needs to be taken to improve it.
* Write the design documentation people read, and raise the bar in design and code reviews.
