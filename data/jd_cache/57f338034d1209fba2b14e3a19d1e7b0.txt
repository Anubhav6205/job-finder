**Software Engineer II**

**Aero Site Reliability Engineering**

As a Software Engineer II here at Honeywell Aerospace Technologies, you will design, develop, and support reliable software and automation for the Aero Site Reliability Engineering team. You will apply full-stack development, Agentic AI, and DevOps practices to improve service availability, engineering productivity, and operational excellence.

You will report directly to Dhanunjaya Kumar and you’ll work out of our Hyderabad, India location on a Hybrid work schedule as part of a global engineering team.

In this role, you will build user-facing applications, APIs, intelligent automation, CI/CD capabilities, and production reliability tooling. Your work will reduce operational toil, accelerate software delivery, strengthen incident response, and improve the reliability of aerospace digital services.
