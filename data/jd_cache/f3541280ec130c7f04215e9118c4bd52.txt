**About the Role:**
===================

**Grade Level (for internal use):**

09

S&P Global Commodity Insights is looking for an experienced **Machine Learning Engineer** to join our Data Science & Modelling team.

In this role, you will design, build and productionize machine learning and AI solutions using S&P Global's extensive data and domain expertise. You will work at the intersection of **machine learning, software engineering, data engineering and domain modelling** , partnering with data scientists, modelers, engineers, product teams and subject-matter experts.

Your work will support a range of business outcomes, including commercial products, advanced forecasting and modelling capabilities, intelligent automation, internal decision support and client-facing solutions.

  
  

**Responsibilities**

* Design, develop and productionize **scalable machine learning and AI solutions** for complex business and modelling problems.
* Build robust data and ML pipelines that support the complete lifecycle from **data preparation and feature engineering through training, deployment, evaluation and monitoring** .
* Develop high-quality, reusable Python software and services that integrate ML capabilities into production applications and analytical platforms.
* Work with large and complex structured and unstructured datasets to improve the quality, accuracy and intelligence of our products and modelling capabilities.
* Apply modern ML techniques, and where appropriate **Generative AI, LLMs and agent-based approaches** , to solve business and domain problems.
* Establish and apply strong **MLOps and software engineering practices** , including automated testing, CI/CD, versioning, observability and reproducibility.
* Build solutions using modern cloud and distributed-computing platforms.
* Evaluate model quality and system performance using appropriate quantitative metrics, validation frameworks and monitoring.
* Collaborate closely with data scientists, modelers, data engineers, product teams and energy-domain experts to translate business and modelling requirements into robust technical solutions.
* Investigate emerging ML and AI technologies and identify practical opportunities to apply them within Data Science & Modelling.
* Contribute to engineering standards, architecture decisions, code reviews and technical mentoring across the team.
  

  

**Requirements**

* 3+ years of professional software development or engineering experience, with strong proficiency in **Python** .
* Demonstrated experience designing and implementing **production-grade machine learning or advanced analytical systems** .
* Strong knowledge of software engineering fundamentals, including **object-oriented design, modular architecture, testing, clean-code practices and design patterns** .
* Experience working with **SQL and large-scale data platforms** .
* Experience building scalable data processing and ML pipelines using technologies such as Apache Big data processing engines **and Databricks** or comparable platforms.
* Experience deploying and operating solutions in a **cloud environment such as AWS** .
* Understanding of the ML lifecycle, including training, validation, deployment, monitoring and model/version management.
* Experience with modern engineering and DevOps practices including **Git, automated testing, CI/CD and containerization** .
* Ability to design reliable, performant and maintainable systems handling large or complex datasets.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
* Ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders and work successfully in cross-functional Agile teams.
* Bachelor's or advanced degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Data Science or a related quantitative discipline, or equivalent practical experience.

**Nice to Have**

* Experience working with **energy, oil & gas, commodities or financial/market data** .
* Experience with **Databricks and modern lakehouse architectures** .
* Experience building or integrating **Generative AI, LLM, RAG or agentic AI solutions** .
* Experience with ML lifecycle and orchestration technologies such as **MLflow, Airflow or equivalent platforms** .
* Experience with model evaluation, observability and production monitoring.
* Experience developing APIs or services for integrating ML capabilities into production applications.
* Familiarity with deep-learning frameworks such as **PyTorch or TensorFlow** .
* Experience working with time-series forecasting, optimization, simulation or other advanced modelling techniques.
