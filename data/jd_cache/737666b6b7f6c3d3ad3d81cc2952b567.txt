**Position Summary**  
  
This role owns agent reliability, observability, and trust controls. Monitors system health, trace logs, response quality, and hallucination signals; establishes detection thresholds and alerts. Works closely with the AI platform team to diagnose issues, improve guardrails, and ensure stable, compliant operation in production

AI Engineer will:

  

* Monitor data quality and changes across operational platforms to maintain reliability of AI solutions.
* Govern data access and documentation to ensure secure and well-controlled use of information.
* Collaborate with technology and business stakeholders on proposed changes to processes and solutions.
* Design, develop, and optimize data pipelines, AI agents, and interfaces to support evolving use cases.
* Develop, test, and refine prompts; train users on effective prompt usage and embed best practices.
* Establish a closed-loop learning cycle by using user feedback, performance metrics, and outcomes to continuously improve AI behavior.
* Ensure AI solutions operate within defined governance and control frameworks, with clear traceability to approved rules and data sources and appropriate escalation where human judgment is required.
* Translate business needs and user feedback into prioritized enhancements, aligned with architecture, security, and platform standards, partnering with technology teams to improve usability and user experience while preserving approved business logic.

**Key responsibilities:**

  

* Design, build, and deploy machine learning and generative AI solutions that meet business needs, including lightweight automation, data pipelines, and AI agents in partnership with technology and data teams.
* Maintain and enhance AI agents using large language models and prompt design, ensuring alignment with agreed business rules, governance standards, and control frameworks.
* Monitor data quality, system changes, and access to key data sources to ensure reliable, secure, and uninterrupted AI operations.
* Translate user feedback and evolving business requirements into prioritized enhancements, acting as a first-line tester for new features and improvements.
* Establish a closed-loop learning cycle by systematically using user feedback, performance metrics, and production outcomes to refine prompts and AI agent behaviour.
* Maintain and share clear documentation, training materials, and an up-to-date understanding of the service landscape, platforms, and tools used by the AI solutions.
* Ensure AI solutions rely on trusted data sources and enforce appropriate refusal or escalation when queries fall outside approved scope or require human judgment.
