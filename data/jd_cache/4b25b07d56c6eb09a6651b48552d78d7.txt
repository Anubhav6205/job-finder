# Senior Generative AI Engineer
  
  

* Experience:\*\* 3–10 Years
* Location:\*\* Pune
* Employment Type:\*\* Full-Time(915)

## Role Overview
  
  
We are looking for a \*\*Senior Generative AI Engineer\*\* to build and deploy enterprise-grade AI solutions using modern Generative AI technologies. The role will involve working across \*\*LLMs, RAG, AI agents, Azure AI services and Python\*\*, with a strong focus on developing reliable solutions that can be integrated into real-world business processes.
  
  
You will work closely with technical teams and business stakeholders to understand requirements, design suitable AI solutions, take ownership of implementation, and support solutions through production.
  
  
## Key Responsibilities
  
  
### AI Solution Development
  
  

* Design and develop Generative AI solutions using \*\*LLMs, RAG and AI agent technologies\*\*.
* Build production-ready AI applications using \*\*Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search and Azure AI Foundry\*\*.
* Develop backend components and AI workflows using \*\*Python\*\*.
* Implement solutions involving prompt engineering, retrieval, vector databases and LLM evaluation.
* Develop AI agents that can interact with business applications, data and enterprise systems.

### Solution Integration
  
  

* Connect AI applications with \*\*REST APIs, SAP, Microsoft 365, SharePoint and other enterprise platforms\*\*.
* Work with Microsoft technologies such as \*\*Power Platform, Power Automate and Copilot-related solutions\*\* where required.
* Translate business requirements into practical and scalable AI solutions.
* Ensure smooth integration between AI capabilities and existing business processes.

### Cloud, Deployment & Operations
  
  

* Deploy and manage AI applications on \*\*Microsoft Azure\*\*.
* Work with \*\*Azure Functions, Azure DevOps and CI/CD pipelines\*\*.
* Perform testing, deployment, monitoring and continuous improvement of AI solutions.
* Troubleshoot production issues and improve application reliability, scalability and performance.
* Follow secure and maintainable coding practices throughout the development lifecycle.

### Architecture & Governance
  
  

* Contribute to AI solution architecture, technical standards and reusable frameworks.
* Consider \*\*security, data protection, Responsible AI and governance\*\* while designing AI solutions.
* Evaluate technical options based on scalability, performance, security and cost.
* Document architecture decisions and technical approaches clearly.

### Collaboration & Ownership
  
  

* Work with cross-functional and globally distributed teams.
* Communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
* Take ownership of projects from requirement gathering through production implementation.
* Provide technical guidance and mentoring to other engineers.
* Stay updated with emerging developments in Generative AI and evaluate their practical use in enterprise applications.
