**Senior GenAI Engineer Agentic AI (Aviation Domain)**

  

**Role Overview:**

  

We are looking for a highly experienced **Senior GenAI Engineer** to lead the design, development, and delivery of a significant AI use case within the aviation domain. The candidate will take end-to-end ownership of solution delivery, ensuring high standards of architecture, code quality, performance, scalability, and reliability.

  

**Key Responsibilities:**

  

* Own end-to-end technical delivery of a strategic AI use case in the aviation domain, from solution design to production deployment.
* Design, develop, and implement scalable GenAI and Agentic AI solutions using LLMs and agent-based architectures.
* Establish and drive engineering standards for code quality, solution reliability, scalability, performance, and maintainability.
* Implement agentic patterns, multi-step reasoning, tool calling, workflow orchestration, and RAG-based solutions.
* Build robust AI applications with appropriate evaluation frameworks, guardrails, monitoring, and error-handling mechanisms.
* Drive technical problem-solving, architecture decisions, and continuous improvement throughout the development lifecycle.
* Mentor engineers on GenAI engineering practices, Agentic AI design patterns, and production-ready implementation approaches.
* Collaborate with architects, product owners, and business stakeholders to translate aviation business requirements into effective AI solutions.
* Ensure solutions meet enterprise standards for security, governance, compliance, and operational reliability.

  

**Required Skills & Experience:**

  

* 7+ years of overall software engineering experience.
* 3+ years of hands-on experience in Generative AI and LLM-based application development.
* Strong programming expertise in Python and experience building scalable applications and APIs.
* Practical experience developing Agentic AI solutions and working with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, or equivalent.
* Strong understanding of LLMs, Prompt Engineering, RAG, embeddings, vector databases, and LLM orchestration.
* Experience building production-grade AI applications with a focus on reliability, performance, scalability, and quality.
* Experience implementing AI evaluation, observability, monitoring, and guardrails.
* Strong software engineering fundamentals, including system design, API development, testing, debugging, and CI/CD.
* Demonstrated ability to mentor engineers and provide technical guidance to development teams.
