**Role Overview**  
 We are looking for a hands-on **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** who can work directly with clients/business teams to identify AI opportunities and rapidly build, prototype and deploy **production-ready GenAI and Agentic AI solutions**.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Work closely with clients to understand business problems and translate them into **AI/ML solutions**.
* Build **AI Agents, RAG applications and multi-agent workflows**.
* Develop LLM-powered applications using **Python and modern AI frameworks**.
* Design RAG pipelines covering **document processing, embeddings, vector search, retrieval and generation**.
* Build **LLM evaluation (Evals)** frameworks to measure accuracy, relevance, hallucination, latency and cost.
* Develop **tool calling, API integrations and agent workflows**.
* Rapidly prototype PoCs and convert successful solutions into **production-grade applications**.
* Implement **prompt/context engineering, guardrails, observability and responsible AI**.
* Deploy solutions on **Azure/AWS/GCP** and work with enterprise security and engineering teams.
* Troubleshoot and continuously improve AI solutions based on real-world production feedback.

  

**Required Skills**

* Strong hands-on **Python and AI/ML** experience.
* Practical experience with **LLMs, RAG, AI Agents and Vector Databases**.
* Experience with **LangGraph, LangChain, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel** or similar frameworks.
* Understanding of **LLM Evals, prompt engineering and AI observability**.
* Experience with REST APIs, FastAPI, Git and cloud platforms.
* Strong problem-solving and client-facing communication skills.
* Ability to work in an **ambiguous, fast-paced environment and deliver working solutions quickly**.
* Experience with **MCP, A2A and multi-agent architectures**.
* Experience with Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock or Google Vertex AI.
* Experience with enterprise GenAI implementations.
* Knowledge of **Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD**.
* Experience in Life Sciences, Healthcare or other regulated industries.
