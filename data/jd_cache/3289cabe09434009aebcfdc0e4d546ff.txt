**Overview:**

* We are looking for an Engineer, Applied AI who is excited to work at the intersection of emerging AI technologies and real-world business impact across Collins Aerospace. In this role, you will contribute to transforming innovations in AI—such as Generative AI and agentic workflows—into practical applications that deliver measurable value.
* You will work across the development cycle—from experimenting with AI techniques to supporting deployment and participating in stakeholder demos. This is a hands-on role where you will design and build components of AI systems, working closely with senior engineers and stakeholders to deliver robust and scalable solutions.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Applied AI Development:Design and develop components of applications leveraging AI across modalities such as text, vision, and structured data, using modern frameworks and tools.
* Prototyping & Iteration:Build and iterate on prototypes to validate AI concepts, support demos, and incorporate feedback to improve solutions.
* Contribution to End-to-End Delivery:Contribute to different stages of the AI development lifecycle—from experimentation to deployment—under guidance, ensuring quality and reliability.
* System Integration:Assist in developing and integrating backend services and APIs, ensuring compatibility with enterprise systems and compliance requirements.
* Automation & MLOps Support:Support implementation of experiment tracking, model versioning, and CI/CD workflows to help transition solutions from development to production.
* Collaboration:Work closely with product teams, architects, and domain experts to understand use cases, refine solutions, and contribute to delivering business value.

**Required Qualifications:**

* **Experience:** 3-4 years of experience in software engineering or ML engineering, with at least 2 years of exposure to applied AI or data-driven application development.
* **Programming:**Proficiency in at least one object-oriented programming language, along with strong Python skills for developing AI applications, data pipelines, and backend services using frameworks such as PyTorch, FastAPI, LangChain, etc.
* **Generative AI (LLMs):**Exposure to working with LLMs (e.g., GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, or Mistral) for building simple workflows, experimentation, or prototypes.
* **Infrastructure (Basic Exposure):**Familiarity with tools such as Docker, Git-based workflows, and CI/CD pipelines.
* **Data Handling:**Basic understanding of relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL), data preprocessing, and working with structured/unstructured data.
* **Problem-Solving Mindset:**Ability to work in ambiguous environments, learn quickly, and contribute to solving real-world problems using AI.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Exposure to building or experimenting with AI applications such as chatbots, copilots, or RAG-based solutions
* Familiarity with emerging frameworks like LangGraph or Semantic Kernel
* Basic understanding of LLM evaluation or prompt optimization
* Exposure to Computer Vision or NLP fundamentals
* Internship/project experience in applied AI use cases
* Domain exposure to Aerospace, Engineering, or Analytics (nice to have)
