**Role & Responsibilities**

  

**1. Deliver Training Sessions**

* Conduct live and recorded sessions on **Excel, SQL, Python, Tableau, and Power BI**.
* Teach data analytics concepts like data cleaning, EDA, visualization, and reporting.
* Use real-world business examples and practical projects to make learning interactive.

  

**2. Curriculum Development**

* Create and update Data Analytics course content as per industry requirements.
* Develop modules, assignments, case studies, and hands-on projects using real datasets.

  

**3. Student Mentorship**

* Guide learners in building analytics projects and portfolios.
* Support students with data interpretation, dashboard creation, and problem-solving.

  

**4. Industry Integration**

* Teach industry-relevant analytics practices and business use cases.
* Prepare students for analytics interviews and real-world challenges.

  

**5. Continuous Learning**

* Stay updated with the latest trends, tools, and technologies in Data Analytics.
* Implement new practices to improve learning outcomes.

  

**6. Performance Evaluation**

* Assess student progress through assignments, quizzes, and projects.
* Collaborate with teams to improve course quality and learner experience.

**Topics the Faculty Must Be Comfortable Teaching**

* Excel for Data Analysis
* SQL for Data Analysis
* Python for Data Analysis
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Data Visualization
* Tableau / Power BI Dashboards
* Business Analytics Case Studies
* Data Storytelling and Reporting
* Generative AI Applications
