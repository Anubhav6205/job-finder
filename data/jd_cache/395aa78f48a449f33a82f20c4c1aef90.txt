## Role Overview

We are seeking a versatile engineer to evaluate and validate identity and security technologies for AI-agent use cases. The role involves configuring vendor products, building and executing proofs of concept (POCs), testing real-world scenarios, and documenting findings and recommendations.

The immediate focus areas are Ping, gateway technologies, and SPIFFE/SPIRE.

## Must-Have Skills

- Strong hands-on engineering skills and ability to work independently.

- Identity and Access Management (IAM) background with a solid understanding of identity flows.

- Token issuance, token exchange, claims, and scoping.

- Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) knowledge.

- Experience with Ping and/or API or LLM/AI gateway technologies.

- Hands-on experience with SPIFFE/SPIRE.

- Experience working in GCP and/or AWS environments.

## Key Responsibilities

- Independently configure vendor products and build and execute POCs.

- Test AI-agent and security use cases and document findings and recommendations.

- Configure and test SPIFFE/SPIRE for AI-agent identity.

- Work with Ping and LLM/AI gateways to enforce identity and access controls.

- Implement and validate token issuance, token exchange, claims, and scoping.

- Collaborate with security, IAM, and engineering teams to share results and guide next steps.

## Good-to-Have Skills
