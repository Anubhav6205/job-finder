**Position Overview:**

  

As a Sr. Analytics Engineer, you transform business and data requirements into efficient data pipelines and analytics solutions that deliver high-quality data assets across all medallion layers. You design, develop, and optimize data flows using Azure Databricks and Power BI, collaborate with Data Architects and cross-functional teams on technical solutions, ensure performance, scalability, and reliability, and promote reuse through documentation, collaboration, and knowledge sharing.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* **ELT Implementation:** Translate business and data requirements into scalable Extract-Load-Transform (ELT) pipelines across Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers using Azure Databricks and related data engineering technologies.

* **Design Collaboration:** Work closely with Data Architects and cross-functional teams to design scalable, maintainable, and high-performing data and analytics solutions.
* **Adoption Collaboration:** Collaborate with Analytics Engineers, AI Engineers, Data Analysts, and business stakeholders to ensure data assets and analytics solutions meet business and technical requirements.

* **Performance Optimization:** Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize data pipelines and analytics solutions for performance, reliability, scalability, and cost efficiency.

* **Knowledge Sharing:** Promote reusable data assets, engineering best practices, and standards through documentation, collaboration, code reviews, and technical knowledge sharing.

* **Deploy & Support:** Deploy, monitor, troubleshoot, and provide third-level support for data assets and analytics solutions, following established CI/CD and DevOps practices.

* **Analytics & Visualization:** Develop and optimize Power BI semantic models, datasets, and reports to deliver reliable, scalable, and business-focused analytics solutions.

  

**Requirements:**

  

* Bachelor’s or Master's degree in IT, Computer Science, or a related field.

* **Experience:** 8+ years of relevant experience in Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, or related data and analytics roles.

* **Agile Experience:** Experience working in agile product teams and collaborating effectively with cross-functional stakeholders.

* **Databricks:** Proven hands-on expertise with Databricks and Lakehouse architecture, including experience designing and optimizing data pipelines across Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers.

* **Azure:** Hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure and related data and analytics services.

* **DevOps:** Familiarity with DevOps practices, including CI/CD pipelines and Infrastructure as Code (IaC).

* **Power BI (Good to have):** Proven hands-on expertise with Power BI, including semantic models, DAX, datasets, reports, and dashboards, with a strong understanding of delivering scalable and business-focused analytics solutions.

This position reports to **: Engineering Manager - Data & Analytics**
