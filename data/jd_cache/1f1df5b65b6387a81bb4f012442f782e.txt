**Role Name: AI Engineer**

  

**Location: Gurgaon/Bangalore**

**Job Summary**

We are seeking an AI Engineer to support a large-scale BI Modernization initiative focused on transforming legacy reporting platforms into a modern cloud-based analytics ecosystem. The ideal candidate will possess strong expertise in Databricks, Python, SQL, and Power BI and have experience implementing AI-enabled analytics capabilities.

The engineer will work closely with business stakeholders, data engineers, analytics teams, and product owners to design scalable data solutions, develop AI-powered insights, and support the migration of reporting assets into a modern enterprise analytics platform.

**Responsibilities**

* Design and develop scalable data pipelines using Python, SQL, and Databricks.
* Build and optimize data models for analytics and reporting.
* Develop Power BI semantic models, datasets, dashboards, and executive reporting solutions.
* Implement AI and machine learning solutions to enhance business insights and decision-making.
* Work with Azure AI/OpenAI services to build intelligent analytics capabilities.
* Support migration from legacy BI platforms to Power BI.
* Collaborate with business teams to define KPIs, metrics, and reporting requirements.
* Ensure data quality, governance, lineage, and compliance standards are met.
* Optimize performance of large-scale reporting and analytical workloads.
* Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to solution architecture discussions.

**Required Qualifications**

* 8+years of experience in Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, or AI Engineering.
* Strong hands-on experience with Python and SQL.
* Experience developing solutions in Databricks using PySpark.
* Strong experience with Power BI, DAX, and semantic modeling.
* Experience building ETL/ELT pipelines and cloud data platforms.
* Understanding of Machine Learning and AI concepts.
* Experience with Azure cloud services.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Banking, Cards, Retail Banking, or Financial Services experience.
* Experience with Azure OpenAI, Microsoft Copilot, or Generative AI solutions.
* Experience with Tableau-to-Power BI migration initiatives.
* Familiarity with data governance tools such as Collibra or Microsoft Purview.
* Experience implementing metric lineage and business KPI frameworks.
