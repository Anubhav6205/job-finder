**Job Description: QA Automation Engineer**

Location: Hyderabad, India

Employment Type: Full-Time

Experience Level: 2 to 4 Years

**Position Overview**

We are seeking a skilled **QA Automation Engineer** to join our Enterprise Digital Solutions team in **Hyderabad, India**. In this role, you will design, build, and maintain reliable automated tests across UI and API layers, embed quality checks into CI/CD, and partner with Engineering and Product to prevent defects early. You’ll contribute to scalable test architecture using **Playwright** with **TypeScript/JavaScript** and help improve test stability, coverage, and release confidence.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Test Automation Development**

* Develop and maintain automated test suites for critical user journeys (smoke, regression, end-to-end).

* Create maintainable UI automation using **Playwright** and **TypeScript/JavaScript** (reusable fixtures, selectors, and page abstractions).

* Build and enhance API automation (request validation, data setup/teardown, and basic contract checks where applicable).

* Analyze failures, triage defects, and collaborate with engineers to identify root causes and prevent regressions.

**Framework & CI/CD Integration**

* Contribute to and continuously improve the Playwright automation framework (structure, utilities, reporting, and test patterns).

* Implement scalable execution capabilities such as parallelization, environment orchestration, and reliable test data management.

* Integrate automation into CI/CD pipelines (PR validation and nightly runs), with clear reporting and quality gates to support fast, reliable releases.

* Leverage AI/LLM tooling (where approved) to accelerate test case authoring, refactoring, and failure triage (log/trace summarization).

* Follow responsible AI and data-handling guidelines; collaborate with Security/Platform teams to ensure compliant use of AI-enabled testing.

* Contribute ideas and small proofs-of-concept to improve test intelligence (failure clustering, flaky detection), as needed.

**Collaboration**

* Partner with engineers, product owners, and DevOps to clarify acceptance criteria and ensure testability of requirements.

* Participate in code reviews for automation and share best practices for stable, maintainable tests.

* Promote shift-left quality by adding automation early in the lifecycle and providing fast feedback to the team.

**Quality Engineering & Governance**

* Define automation scope for critical business journeys and ensure coverage for new/changed features.

* Track and report quality metrics (pass rate, stability/flakiness, defect leakage) and propose improvements.

* Communicate test status, risks, and release readiness to stakeholders with clear, actionable information.
  

**Required Skills & Experience**

* 5-7 years of hands-on experience in test automation for web (and/or mobile) applications, including designing and maintaining automation frameworks.

* Strong proficiency in **TypeScript/JavaScript**.

* Expertise in architecting and scaling cross-platform test execution using **BrowserStack SDK/Automate** in CI/CD environments

* Deep experience integrating automation into **CI/CD pipelines**.

* Practical experience using or operationalizing **AI/LLM-based testing tools**.

* Familiarity with agent-based testing concepts and orchestration patterns (e.g., MCP or similar) is a plus.

* Ability to influence quality outcomes through strong collaboration, clear communication, and pragmatic decision-making.

* Experience making architecture-level automation decisions in complex environments.

* Strong system-thinking mindset with the ability to balance speed, quality, and risk.

* Strong background in Agile, DevOps, and continuous testing practices.

* Experience supporting cloud-native, microservices, and API-driven systems.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience in enterprise-scale or regulated environments.

* Exposure to CIAM, security testing, or identity-centric platforms.

* Prior experience driving quality transformation or modernization initiatives.

* Familiarity with observability, analytics, and test intelligence platforms.
