**Automotive Domain + AI Subject Matter Expert (SME)**

**Role Purpose**

We are looking for an **Automotive Domain + AI SME** who combines deep automotive business and business-process expertise with a strong applied understanding of **AI, Generative AI and Agentic AI**.

The role will work closely with automotive business and technology teams to understand existing processes, IT landscapes and value flows, challenge existing assumptions and improvement opportunities, map applications and data supporting those processes, and identify how AI and agents can enable fundamentally better ways of working and measurable business value.

The SME will lead opportunities from **process discovery future-state reimagination AI opportunity identification prioritization business-value validation implementation roadmap**, working together with consulting, architecture and engineering teams.

**Key Responsibilities**

**1. Automotive Domain Expertise & Process Reimagination**

* Conduct workshops and engage on eye level with automotive business, engineering and IT stakeholders.
* Understand and document **as-is business processes, value flows and pain points** to challenge and contribute to client discussions.
* Identify where current process structures, controls or decision points can be simplified, augmented or eliminated through AI and automation.
* Understand process dependencies across business functions, applications, data and organizational boundaries.
* Reimagine **to-be processes enabled by AI and automation**.
* Bring automotive industry benchmarks, best practices and solid domain knowledge into discussions.

**2. AI Opportunity Identification**

* Evaluate processes for applicability of **GenAI, Agentic AI, ML and intelligent automation**.
* Translate domain problems and future-state process ideas into clearly defined AI-enabled use cases.
* Identify opportunities for:
  + Knowledge assistants and enterprise search
  + AI agents and autonomous workflows
  + Engineering productivity
  + Predictive analytics
  + Intelligent document processing
  + Service/support automation
  + Process re-imagionation and optimization
  + AI-enabled decision support
* Assess feasibility considering **data, applications, integration, security and business readiness**.

**3. Application & Process Mapping**

* Undertand and map how business processes are supported by underlying applications, systems and data.
* Understand interactions between legacy, enterprise and custom applications.
* Identify where AI can augment or orchestrate existing applications rather than requiring major replacement, and where process redesign applies.
* Work with architects and engineers to translate requirements into APIs, integrations and data sources.

**4. AI Use-Case Prioritization & Business Case**

* Develop an **AI opportunity backlog** through discovery and reimagationation workshops.
* Prioritize opportunities based on:
  + Business value
  + Productivity improvement
  + Technical feasibility
  + Data availability
  + Implementation complexity
  + Time-to-value
* Define measurable KPIs and expected outcomes for selected use cases.
* Develop high-level business cases and implementation roadmaps.

**5. PoC / Prototype Leadership**

* Convert shortlisted opportunities into structured PoC proposals.
* Define PoC scope, success criteria, data requirements and expected outcomes.
* Work hands-on or closely with AI engineers to shape and develop prototypes.
* Validate and demonstrate solutions to business stakeholders.
* Assess PoC results against agreed business KPIs.
* Recommend **Scale / Refine / Stop** decisions based on results.

**6. Automotive AI Advisory**

The candidate should have deep knowledge in **at least 2 automotive domains**, such as:

* Vehicle Engineering / R&D
* Software-Defined Vehicle
* Manufacturing & Plant Operations
* Quality Management
* Supply Chain & Logistics
* Procurement
* Sales & Marketing
* Aftersales / Customer Service
* Connected Vehicle
* Finance / Leasing
* Dealer Management

The SME should continuously assess the impact of emerging AI capabilities on these automotive processes.

**AI & Technology Expectations**

Strong conceptual and practical understanding of and how to apply:

* AI and Large Language Models
* Agentic AI / AI Agents, including humain-in-the-loop patterns
* RAG and enterprise knowledge solutions
* AI-assisted software engineering
* Machine Learning fundamentals
* Process automation and orchestration
* AI APIs and integration concepts
* Cloud-based AI platforms and enterprise AI architectures
* Responsible AI, security and data privacy

Exposure to major enterprise AI platforms and agent frameworks. Experience with technologies such as **Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, OpenAI, Microsoft Copilot, LangChain/LangGraph** would be advantageous.

The person **does not need to be a full-time AI developer**, but must be technically strong enough to discuss AI architecture with engineering teams and guide a PoC from business problem to working solution.
