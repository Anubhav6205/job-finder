Position: AI Engineer

Location: Bangalore

Working Days: Monday to Friday

Experience Required: 3-5 years

Our Client is building an AI-native Social Content & Campaign Intelligence OS for global consumer brands , helping them monitor, audit, reason over, and activate intelligence across social content, influencer campaigns, UGC, and commerce. We are working with Fortune 500 global enterprise brands.

**The role**

You'd build the agents in our AI-native social content operating system, and the deterministic pipelines they run inside , conceiving those workflows as well as writing them.

You'd sit in the core build team, working with the Backend engineers on the services around the pipelines.

What you'll do

* Build and operate multi-step AI pipelines as real workflows , idempotent, retryable, observable, resumable after failure
* Design the evaluation before you ship: datasets, metrics, and gates that can block a release
* Make model behaviour reproducible through versioning, caching and replay, so a decision made months ago can be reconstructed
* Harden pipelines against adversarial and injected input, structurally rather than by prompt instruction
* Keep cost and latency per operation measured, and treat regressions as bugs
* Work with the Site Reliability Engineers on production visibility into provider latency, failure rates and cost
* Keep the model layer swappable, so changing provider is a configuration change rather than a rewrite

**What you'll need**

* Around 3 or more years in software engineering, including shipping AI or LLM-backed features to production
* Stayed responsible for those features after launch, including their failures
* Built evaluation harnesses and used them to make real ship or no-ship decisions
* Strong general software engineering , most of this job is systems work
* Can reason clearly about the failure modes of a component that doesn't return the same answer twice

**Nice to have**

* Multi-modal pipelines , vision, audio, or document understanding
* Retrieval systems and the evaluation of retrieval quality
* Adversarial input, prompt injection, or abuse-resistance work
* Fine-tuning or distillation, and the infrastructure around it
* Experience with workflow orchestration at scale.
