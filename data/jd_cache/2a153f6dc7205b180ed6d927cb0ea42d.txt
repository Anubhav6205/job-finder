**The Impact You’ll Make in this Role**

As the EHS AI Specialist, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and implementing AI-driven tools and solutions that improve the efficiency, quality, and consistency of Environmental, Health and Safety (EHS) work at 3M. Partnering closely with EHS subject-matter experts, auditors, and IT/digital teams, you will identify high-value opportunities for automation and intelligent assistance, build solutions that integrate with existing EHS systems, and drive adoption across the organization. This role offers the opportunity to tap into your curiosity, collaborate with innovative people around the world, and help modernize how EHS teams work while ensuring AI is applied responsibly, securely, and in compliance with 3M policies. Here, you will make an impact by:

* Partner with EHS, audit, and compliance teams to identify tasks where AI can improve efficiency, quality, and consistency.
* Design, build, and deploy AI tools such as assistants, document review tools, data extraction, classification, summarization, analytics, and dashboards.
* Integrate AI solutions with EHS systems and enterprise platforms such as Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams, Power Platform, and related workflows.
* Pilot solutions with users, gather feedback, measure success metrics, report business value, and continuously improve performance.
* Ensure responsible, secure, and compliant AI use while creating documentation, training materials, and supporting adoption across global EHS teams.

**Your Skills and Expertise**

To set you up for success in this role from day one, 3M requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications:

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Environmental/Safety Science, or a related field.
* Minimum 3 years of experience developing and implementing AI, machine learning, automation, or data analytics solutions in a business environment.
* Hands-on experience with generative AI/LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, AI agents, platforms such as Azure OpenAI, AWS, or Microsoft Copilot, plus Python, low-code tools, APIs, and data integration.
* Ability to translate business needs into technical solutions, communicate with non-technical stakeholders, and use Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Teams, and SharePoint effectively.

**Additional qualifications that could help you succeed even further in this role include:**

* Experience in EHS, compliance, quality, or audit functions within manufacturing, industrial, or chemical environments.
* Familiarity with EHS management systems such as Enablon, Intelex, Cority, or Sphera, and EHS data including incidents, audits, permits, and chemical management.
* Experience with data visualization and analytics tools such as Power BI, Tableau, and SQL.
* Knowledge of responsible AI, data governance, change management, cross-functional/global collaboration, and relevant certifications such as Azure AI Engineer, AWS Machine Learning, or Power Platform.
