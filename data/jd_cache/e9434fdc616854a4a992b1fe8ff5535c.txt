**Description**
Job Description: Junior GenAI & Data Engineer
  
  
Role: Junior GenAI & Data Engineer
  
  
Experience Level: Entry Level / Fresher (0-2 Years)
  
  
Location: Pune ( Compulsory Work From Office (WFO))
  
  
Employment Type: Full-time
  
  
**About The Role**
We are looking for a passionate and technically agile Junior GenAI & Data Engineer to join our innovation team. This is not a typical software engineering role; it is a hybrid position that sits at the intersection of Big Data, Generative AI, Cloud Computing, and Business Intelligence.
  
  
We are looking for a "Vibe Coder" — someone who codes with rhythm and intuition, leverages modern AI tools to move fast, and isn't afraid to experiment with the latest in Large Language Models (LLMs) and distributed systems.
  
  
Key Responsibilities
  
  
GenAI Development: Build and deploy applications using RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architectures. Implement agentic workflows using LangChain and LangGraph.
  
  
Research & Innovation: Conduct deep-dive research on emerging GenAI trends, model architectures, and optimization techniques. Synthesize findings into technical blogs, whitepapers, or research summaries to guide the team's technical direction.
  
  
Big Data Processing: Design and implement scalable data pipelines using Apache Spark and Hadoop to handle large datasets effectively.
  
  
Business Intelligence & Reporting: Design and maintain interactive dashboards using PowerBI or MicroStrategy to visualize model outputs, track KPIs, and drive data-driven decision-making.
  
  
Prompt & Context Engineering: Optimize system prompts and context windows to ensure high-accuracy model responses.
  
  
Data Engineering & ETL: Design data pipelines with an understanding of core distributed computing concepts. Assist in ETL workflows using tools like DataStage, Postgres, or custom scripts.
  
  
Model Selection: Analyze business problems and identify the correct Machine Learning algorithm or GenAI model for the task.
  
  
Cloud Deployment: Deploy and maintain services on AWS (specifically EC2 for compute and RDS for storage).
  
  
Agile Collaboration: Participate in daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and use Jira for task tracking and Git for version control.

Python (Must-Have): Strong proficiency for AI/ML and PySpark workflows.
  
  
Java: Solid understanding of OOPs concepts and willingness to work on Big Data integrations.

Hands-on experience (academic projects or internships) with Generative AI.

Core Concepts: Conceptual clarity on Partitioning, Sharding, Distributed Computing, and Data Modeling.

Analytics Architecture: Understanding of OLAP Cubes and multi-dimensional data modeling.
  
  
ETL Tools: Awareness of ETL frameworks and tools like IBM DataStage or similar data integration platforms.

AWS: Basic awareness of EC2 and RDS.
  
  
DevOps Basics: Git (branching/merging) and Jira.
