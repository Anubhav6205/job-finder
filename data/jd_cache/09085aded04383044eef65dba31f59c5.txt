 **Responsibilities**

Engage with customer business process owners, developers & engineers to understand the roles and recurring workflows where agents create value, and build secure AI & Agentic solutions that drive business value for their organizations.  

* Help customers navigate Copilot Cowork and agent onboarding, environment setup, licensing and consumption questions, capacity and governance requirements, and common operational blockers using approved playbooks and knowledge sources.
* Work on technical engagements across envisioning, architecture design, Proof of Concepts (POCs), and Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) through to production deployment to accelerate adoption of Copilot, Copilot Cowork, Copilot Studio, Foundry, Fabric, Azure AI, GitHub, and data platforms.
* Support adoption and value realization by delivering persona-based scenario workshops, helping customers agree a target user cohort and measure of value, and tracking repeat usage, realized value, and consumption.
* Leverage AI to automate and deliver repeatable processes that help customers achieve faster outcomes and deliver repeatable IP aligned with AI and Agentic Solutions
* Engage in relevant agent and AI communities to share expertise, contribute to IP creation, and learn from others to help accelerate your customers’ AI transformation journey.
* Act as the Voice of the Customer by sharing insights and feedback with AI Business Solutions Leadership to identify opportunities and remove adoption blockers.
* Maintain deep technical expertise and stay current with AI trends and technologies such as Copilot, Copilot Cowork, Copilot Studio, Foundry, Fabric, Azure, AI, GitHub, Security solutions, while contributing to internal and external technical communities.
* Capture and share repeatable implementation patterns with account teams, the solution engineering community, and Microsoft partners
* Actively participate in onboarding, technical training, and ongoing skill development programs.
* Incorporate coaching feedback to improve technical accuracy, efficiency, and customer interaction quality
* Demonstrate a growth mindset by continuously aligning your skills to current and future customer needs
