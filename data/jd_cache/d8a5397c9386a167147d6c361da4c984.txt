Primary Responsibilities

Design, implement, and maintain enterprise DNS infrastructure (Microsoft DNS, BIND, Infoblox, Route 53, Azure DNS, etc.).

Troubleshoot complex DNS resolution issues affecting applications, servers, and network services.

Manage DNS zones, records, delegations, conditional forwarders, and split-brain DNS configurations.

Perform DNS migrations, consolidations, and infrastructure upgrades with minimal business impact.

Monitor DNS performance, availability, and health using enterprise monitoring tools.

Implement DNS security controls including DNSSEC, Response Rate Limiting (RRL), and secure zone transfers.

Collaborate with network, cloud, security, and application teams to resolve service-impacting issues.

Support hybrid cloud DNS integrations across Azure, AWS, and on-premises environments.

Conduct root cause analysis (RCA) for major incidents and provide preventive recommendations.

Develop and maintain DNS operational procedures, architecture documentation, and knowledge articles.

Automate DNS administration tasks using PowerShell, Python, or Infrastructure-as-Code tools.

Participate in on-call support and incident management activities for critical infrastructure services.

Ensure compliance with organizational security, governance, and change management standards.

Perform capacity planning and recommend improvements to enhance DNS resilience and scalability.
