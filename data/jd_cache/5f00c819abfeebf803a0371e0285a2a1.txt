**Team Overview**

The team is responsible for building, enhancing, and operating scalable data engineering solutions and distributed data processing platforms. The team processes large volumes of data, including terabyte-scale datasets, through recurring batch workloads and production pipelines, with a strong focus on performance, scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency.
  
The role will contribute to developing and optimizing data-intensive applications using Apache Spark, Scala, Python, and cloud technologies, with a preference for Google Cloud Platform (GCP). The team works on identity resolution and data-driven solutions, where linking identities and analyzing relationships across devices, digital identifiers, and other data signals are important. Experience in identity resolution, AdTech, MarTech, or digital identity platforms will be a strong advantage.
  
The team also explores AI/ML concepts and AI-assisted development approaches to improve engineering productivity and enable innovative solutions.&#xa;&#xa;This is a hybrid position and involves regular performance of job responsibilities virtually as well as in-person at an assigned TU office location for a minimum of two days a week.  
  

**Role Overview And Core Responsibilities**

* Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable data processing applications using Apache Spark and Scala.
* Build and operate production-grade distributed data pipelines capable of processing terabytes of data per execution.
* Develop and maintain recurring batch processing workloads, including weekly and scheduled pipelines, ensuring timely completion, reliability, and data quality.
* Optimize large-scale Spark jobs for execution time, memory utilization, parallelism, resource consumption, and cloud infrastructure costs.
* Design robust data processing workflows with appropriate error handling, retries, checkpointing, recovery mechanisms, and idempotency.
* Monitor production jobs, troubleshoot failures and performance bottlenecks, and perform root-cause analysis to ensure operational stability.
* Develop cloud-based data engineering solutions, preferably on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), with AWS experience also considered.
* Work with technologies such as Google Dataproc, BigQuery, cloud object storage, Parquet, and equivalent cloud data services.
* Write clean, efficient, reusable, and maintainable code using Scala, Python, and shell scripting.
* Apply strong analytical and problem-solving skills to resolve complex data processing and distributed computing challenges.
* Contribute to platform enhancements, automation, workflow orchestration, and continuous improvement of data engineering systems.
* Leverage AI/ML concepts and AI-assisted development tools where appropriate to improve development productivity and engineering quality.
* Collaborate with engineers, architects, and cross-functional stakeholders to deliver scalable, reliable, and maintainable solutions.
* Follow software engineering best practices, including code reviews, automated testing, version control, CI/CD, monitoring, and production support.

  
  

**Required Knowledge And Experiences**

**Core technical requirements**  
  

* Strong hands-on experience with Apache Spark and Scala for distributed data processing.
* Demonstrated experience designing, developing, and operating production-grade data pipelines processing terabytes of data per run.
* Experience managing large-scale recurring batch workloads, preferably weekly or more frequently, with responsibility for successful execution, performance, reliability, and data quality.
* Strong understanding of Spark performance optimization, including partitioning, shuffles, joins, caching, memory management, parallelism, and resource tuning.
* Experience troubleshooting failed or slow-running production jobs and implementing robust recovery, retry, and fault-tolerance mechanisms.
* Working knowledge of cloud platforms, preferably Google Cloud Platform (GCP); AWS experience is also acceptable.
* Familiarity with GCP Dataproc, BigQuery, cloud object storage, and distributed data formats such as Parquet, or equivalent AWS technologies.
* Proficiency in Python and shell scripting for automation, data processing, and operational workflows.
* Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, debugging, and root-cause analysis skills.
* Good understanding of distributed computing, data structures, algorithms, and software engineering fundamentals.
* Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, automated testing, workflow orchestration, and production monitoring.

  
**Preferred qualifications**  
  

* Experience operating terabyte-scale Spark workloads in production with defined execution schedules and delivery SLAs.
* Experience processing billions of records, handling incremental data loads, and optimizing large-scale data transformations.
* Experience with cloud cost optimization, workload sizing, capacity planning, and resource utilization.
* Exposure to machine learning pipelines, identity resolution, entity resolution, or graph-based data processing.
* Basic understanding of AI/ML concepts and familiarity with LLMs, generative AI, or AI-assisted software development tools.
* Ability to independently own complex engineering problems from design and implementation through production deployment and ongoing operations.

* Experience in identity resolution, identity graphs, entity resolution, or digital identity platforms, including linking and reconciling identifiers across multiple data sources.
* Experience in MarTech (Marketing Technology) or AdTech (Advertising Technology), particularly with digital identifiers such as mobile advertising IDs, cookies, IP addresses, device signals, or email identifiers.
* Familiarity with audience segmentation, household or individual identity mapping, cross-device identity, or identity graph construction.
