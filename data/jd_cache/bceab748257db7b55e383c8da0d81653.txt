**Job Description:**  
Middleware Platform Engineer — Cloud(AWS/AZURE) and On-Premises.
  
  
**Summary:**  
The Middleware Platform Engineer design, build, deploy, automate, and support middleware platforms in cloud and on-premises. This role is responsible for platform provisioning, operations, security, maintenance, and lifecycle management for cloud and on-premises.
  
  
**Skills:**  
Ansible, Cloud, Jenkins, GIT, WebLogic, JBOSS, Wildfly, Tomcat, Apache, IIS, NGINIX, WebSphere, Python, Shell...
  
  
**Key Responsibilities:**  
1. Working in data center migration(DCM), support, middleware patching and new build ecosystems.
  
  
2. Experience in scripting and automation.
  
  
3. Experience in vulnerability remediation , certificate renewal , load balancer , SSO, JDK, troubleshooting issues related to JDK/memory/filesystem etc...
  
  
4. Troubleshoot middleware issues on Cloud and On-premises.
  
  
5. Well versed with support process like SOP, Handover/Takeover, following SLA
  
  
6. Modernize middleware platforms and migrate workloads to newer versions.
  
  
7. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  
  
8. Experience in Middleware installations/upgrade .
  
  
10. Develop infrastructure-as-code and Ansible automation for middleware provisioning and configuration.
  
  
11. Deploy middleware on infrastructure-as-a-service and cloud-native platforms.
  
  
12. Design and implement auto-scaling, load balancing, and reusable middleware reference patterns.
  
  
13. Integrate middleware deployments into cloud server builds and automated provisioning workflows.
  
  
14. Configure cloud networking and security groups required for middleware services.
  
  
15. Maintain compatibility across hybrid-cloud environments.
  
  
16. Monitor cloud-hosted middleware and maintain observability integrations.
  
  
17. Integrate middleware provisioning and deployment into CI/CD pipelines.
  
  
18. Good knowledge of ITIL processes
  
  
19. Follow change management processes and support audits and evidence requests.
