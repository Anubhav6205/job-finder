Responsibilities
  
  

* Apply SRE principles to boost the availability, resilience, performance, and recoverability of our services.
* Support the implementation and measurement of Service Level Indicators (SLIs), Service Level Objectives (SLOs), and other key service-health metrics.
* Analyze service behavior, identify failure patterns, and pinpoint reliability risks and improvement opportunities.
* Participate in resilience, failover, recovery, and production-readiness activities.
* Partner with application, platform, and infrastructure teams to implement reliability improvements.
* Use observability platforms to analyze metrics, logs, traces, events, and service topology.
* Build and enhance dashboards, alerts, and service-health views.
* Correlate telemetry across applications, infrastructure, cloud, network, and shared platforms to support end-to-end troubleshooting.
* Identify gaps in telemetry, monitoring, and alerting, and collaborate with engineering teams to enhance observability.
* Utilize modern telemetry and instrumentation approaches such as OpenTelemetry.
* Harness AI, Generative AI, and AIOps capabilities to support operational analysis, anomaly detection, event correlation, troubleshooting, and knowledge retrieval.
* Apply AI-assisted techniques to speed up incident investigation and identify potential causes and solutions.
* Develop AI-assisted operational workflows and automation to boost reliability and reduce manual effort.
* Validate AI-generated insights against telemetry and authoritative operational data before implementing recommendations.
* Ensure appropriate safeguards and human oversight when AI is used in production operations.
* Engage in technical investigation and recovery of production incidents.
* Use telemetry, dependency information, and engineering analysis to diagnose service failures.
* Contribute to root-cause analysis, post-incident reviews, and corrective and preventive actions.
* Identify recurring reliability issues and help implement engineering solutions to prevent recurrence.
* Validate that remediation actions have led to measurable reliability improvements.
* Create scripts, tools, and automated workflows that enhance operational efficiency and reliability.
* Automate repetitive detection, diagnostic, remediation, and recovery tasks where feasible.
* Integrate systems and operational platforms using APIs and automation frameworks.
* Identify operational toil and replace repeated manual activities with sustainable engineering solutions.
* Contribute reusable automation, runbooks, and engineering patterns that can be applied across services.

Qualifications

* Bachelors degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* Foundational experience in software engineering, cloud, infrastructure, systems engineering, production operations, or a related technical area.
* Proficiency in at least one programming or scripting language such as Python, PowerShell, Java, or JavaScript.
* Understanding of the fundamentals of application, infrastructure, cloud, and networking.
* Knowledgeable in monitoring, logging, alerting, and troubleshooting basics.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
* Effective collaboration skills across engineering and operational teams.
* Interest in deepening expertise in SRE, observability, automation, and AI-enabled operations.

Preferred
  
  

* Experience or exposure to observability technologies such as Dynatrace, Splunk, Grafana, Prometheus, or equivalent.
* Familiarity with OpenTelemetry and modern application instrumentation.
* Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Microsoft Azure.
* Knowledge of Kubernetes and container technologies.
* Experience with Infrastructure as Code and automation technologies such as Terraform or Ansible.
* Familiarity with ServiceNow or enterprise IT Service Management platforms.
* Understanding of CI/CD and modern software delivery practices.
* Knowledge of SRE concepts including SLIs, SLOs, error budgets, incident management, and post-incident reviews.
* Experience with AI/ML, Generative AI, AIOps, or AI-assisted operational tooling.
