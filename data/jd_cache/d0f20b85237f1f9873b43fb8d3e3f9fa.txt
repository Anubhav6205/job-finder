* Lead enterprise control framework modernization.
* Develop compliance methodologies and operational standards.
* Drive compliance process optimization initiatives.

* Lead enterprise framework harmonization.
* Establish enterprise compliance methodologies.
* Lead control rationalization initiatives.

Information Security Risk Management

* Configure Optro IT & Cyber Risk Management, RiskOversight, and Scenario Planning.
* Develops, implements, administers, and continuously improves information security risk management processes, procedures, standards, and governance activities supporting the Information Security Risk Management Program.
* Executes enterprise cybersecurity, technology, data, and artificial intelligence risk management activities in accordance with methodologies, governance standards, and program objectives.
* Maintains and administers cybersecurity, technology, information, data, and artificial intelligence risk assessment methodologies, risk taxonomies, scoring models, and supporting program documentation.
* Leads and facilitates risk assessments across cybersecurity, information technology, enterprise applications, cloud services, infrastructure, digital platforms, data environments, artificial intelligence initiatives, and other technology-enabled business processes to identify, evaluate, prioritize, and mitigate security threats and risks impacting organizational assets.
* Maintains the enterprise information security risk register, ensuring risks are appropriately identified, assessed, documented, prioritized, assigned, monitored, escalated, and reported throughout their lifecycle.
* Coordinates with risk owners to document mitigation plans, treatment activities, residual risk decisions, target completion dates, and status updates.
* Partners with business leaders, technology teams, security stakeholders, and risk owners to evaluate risk exposure and recommend treatment strategies, including mitigation, transfer, acceptance, and avoidance options.
* Provides independent challenge and oversight of risk assessments, treatment plans, and risk acceptance recommendations to ensure alignment with established methodologies, governance expectations, and organizational risk appetite.
* Assesses security threats and risks throughout the lifecycle of technology, cloud, data, artificial intelligence, and business initiatives and supports development of risk mitigation strategies.
* Evaluates security control effectiveness, governance processes, and risk treatment activities to identify opportunities for enhanced protection of organizational assets and reduction of enterprise risk exposure.

Information Assurance & Security Governance

* Supports identification, analysis, and validation of security requirements during technology implementations, digital transformation initiatives, emerging technology deployments, and significant business changes.
* Assesses cybersecurity and technology-related regulatory compliance requirements and supports integration of information security and risk management practices into organizational processes.
* Supports development and continuous improvement of enterprise information security risk management standards, methodologies, governance processes, and operational plans that influence cybersecurity strategy and program maturity.
* Supports information assurance activities designed to improve the protection, resilience, and governance of enterprise information assets.
* Evaluates governance processes and security requirements to support secure adoption of new technologies, digital capabilities, and emerging business initiatives.

Risk Metrics, Reporting & Program Administration

* Develops, maintains, and monitors key risk indicators (KRIs), key performance indicators (KPIs), risk metrics, dashboards, and reporting artifacts used to support risk monitoring, governance activities, and program effectiveness measurement.
* Develops, maintains, and enhances risk reporting data, metrics, dashboards, and supporting documentation used to support governance activities, operational decision-making, and program maturity measurement.
* Supports implementation, optimization, and ongoing administration of Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) platforms used to manage risk workflows, risk registers, reporting, metrics, and issue tracking.
* Maintains risk management procedures, standards, templates, workflows, and supporting documentation to ensure consistency and repeatability of program activities.
* Provides risk reporting and analysis to support governance processes, leadership decision-making, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Cyber, Data & Artificial Intelligence Risk Management

* Assesses risks associated with information protection, data classification, access management, data governance, data retention, data sharing, data residency, integrity, availability, and protection of sensitive information.
* Evaluates emerging technology and artificial intelligence risks, including governance, data protection, third-party dependencies, model integrity, transparency, explainability, human oversight, bias, and responsible-use considerations.
* Develops and maintains governance processes supporting artificial intelligence risk management activities and advises stakeholders on alignment with applicable AI governance frameworks, standards, and regulatory expectations.
* Supports integration of AI risk management practices into enterprise governance, risk, compliance, and security processes.
* Analyzes risk events, incidents, audit findings, assessments, threat intelligence, control deficiencies, and industry trends to identify risk themes, emerging concerns, and opportunities for continuous improvement.

Mentorship & Technical Leadership

* Mentor internal Risk and Compliance Leads.

Serve as senior technical advisor to the InfoSec GRC Director
