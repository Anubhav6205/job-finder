**About Position:**

  

We are seeking a skilled Senior Power BI Developer with 4 to 6 years of experience in business intelligence, data visualization, and analytics solutions. The ideal candidate will have hands-on expertise in Power BI report development, semantic modeling, SQL-based data extraction, and dimensional data modeling. You will work closely with business stakeholders and data engineering teams to transform complex data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and reports that support strategic decision-making.

  

* **Role: Power BI Developer**
* **Location: Pune**
* **Experience: 4 to 6 Years**
* **Job Type: Full-Time Employment**
  

**What You'll Do:**

  

* Design, develop, and maintain interactive Power BI dashboards and reports aligned with business requirements.
* Create and optimize Power BI Semantic Models to support scalable reporting and analytics solutions.
* Develop complex SQL queries for data extraction, transformation, and reporting purposes.
* Implement dimensional data models using Star Schema and other best-practice modeling techniques.
* Collaborate with business users to gather reporting requirements and translate them into effective dashboard solutions.
* Perform data analysis, validation, and quality checks to ensure reporting accuracy.
* Work closely with Data Engineers to integrate and optimize data pipelines for reporting and analytics.
* Develop and enhance ETL/ELT processes to support enterprise reporting needs.
* Leverage cloud-based data platforms such as Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, and Databricks for analytics solutions.
* Utilize Python and PySpark for advanced data preparation and transformation when required.
* Support Power Platform initiatives including Power Apps and Power Automate integrations.
* Identify opportunities to improve reporting performance, usability, and data accessibility.
* Troubleshoot report performance issues and optimize dashboard responsiveness.
* Document reporting solutions, data models, and development standards.
  

**Expertise You'll Bring:**

  

* 4 to 6 years of experience in Power BI development and business intelligence solutions.
* Strong hands-on experience creating Power BI dashboards, reports, and semantic models.
* Expertise in DAX, Power Query, and Power BI Service capabilities.
* Strong knowledge of dimensional modeling concepts, including Star Schema and Snowflake Schema.
* Proficiency in SQL, T-SQL, PL/SQL, and complex query development.
* Experience with ETL/ELT processes and enterprise data integration.
* Understanding of Data Warehousing concepts and modern analytics architectures.
* Hands-on experience with cloud data platforms such as Snowflake, Databricks, or Microsoft Fabric.
* Knowledge of Python and PySpark for data transformation and analytics.
* Experience working with large datasets and performance optimization techniques.
* Familiarity with Power Platform technologies including Power Apps and Power Automate.
* Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.
* Experience gathering business requirements and translating them into reporting solutions.
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
* Ability to explain complex data insights to technical and non-technical audiences.
* Experience working in Agile development environments.
