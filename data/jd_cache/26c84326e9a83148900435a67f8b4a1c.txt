**Role Summary:**

You will be part of the Sphera AI Center of Excellence team building and maturing the AI capabilities that Sphera and its customers depend on. You will design and ship AI agents and AI-powered features used across product families and internal functions, and act as the expert other teams come to when they want to build with AI themselves. We build on Claude and deploy on Azure AI Foundry, with Claude Code as our primary development environment. The pace is fast — prototype to production in weeks, not quarters — so you'll apply the skills you have while learning new ones constantly. If you have hands-on experience shipping LLM-based systems and enjoy unblocking other engineers, you'll do well here.

**Roles and Responsibilities:**

* Design, build, and maintain production AI agents and AI-powered features — new builds as well as enhancements to existing solutions.
* Build and sustain agentic systems end to end: task decomposition, tool design, context and memory strategy, orchestration, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, and fallback behavior.
* Build the retrieval and grounding layer — document processing, chunking, hybrid search, and citation — so agents answer from Sphera's own data.
* Deploy and operate AI workloads on Azure AI Foundry: model deployments, endpoints, content safety, private networking, and cost and quota management.
* Use Claude Code as a core engineering tool and set the patterns other teams follow when they do the same.
* Own evaluation: build eval suites and golden datasets, measure accuracy, latency, cost, and tool-call correctness, and gate changes on results.
* Implement AI guardrails, prompt-injection defenses, and PII handling, working with Platform Engineering on identity and network controls.
* Ship through CI/CD with versioned prompts and agent definitions, plus tracing and token/cost observability on every path.
* Support teams adopting shared AI services — review their designs, troubleshoot integrations, and document reusable patterns.
* Establish KPIs for adoption, quality, and cost-per-outcome, and use them to drive improvement and make the team's impact visible.
* Participate fully in the agile process — own your sprint tasks, log time worked, and keep details current.

**Experience and Qualifications:**

* 4–6 years of software engineering experience, including at least 2 years building with large language models in production.
* Hands-on experience shipping agentic systems: multi-step tool use, structured outputs, state and memory, error recovery — and the judgment to know when an agent isn't the right answer.
* Working command of Claude or a comparable frontier model platform, including APIs, tool use, and agent SDKs.
* Practical daily use of AI-assisted development tooling, Claude Code in particular.
* Retrieval engineering: embeddings, vector and hybrid search, reranking, and evaluating retrieval quality on its own terms.
* Evaluation and observability for non-deterministic systems — offline evals, human review loops, tracing, and cost instrumentation.
* Strong Python, plus one of TypeScript, C#, or Java.
* Azure AI Foundry or Azure OpenAI — model deployment, endpoint configuration, and content safety controls.
* Azure fundamentals: App Service or Function Apps, Key Vault, Storage, Entra ID managed identities, and Application Insights.
* CI/CD with Azure DevOps — YAML pipelines, multi-stage deployments, and secret management.
* REST and async API design, and integration with enterprise systems of record.
* Databases (SQL and NoSQL) and vector stores; solid Git and code review practice.
