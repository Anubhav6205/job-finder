**Software Engineer Backend (Engineering Applications)**

**Experience: 3-9 Years**

  

**Role Summary**

We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer with strong software development fundamentals and exposure to engineering applications. The ideal candidate should have a solid understanding of engineering domains, strong object-oriented programming skills, and experience developing backend software solutions. Candidates should be capable of understanding engineering business logic, simulation workflows, and translating technical requirements into scalable software solutions.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Design, develop, and maintain backend software components for engineering applications.
* Collaborate with product managers, engineering teams, and domain experts to understand business requirements and convert them into software solutions.
* Develop scalable, maintainable, and reusable code using object-oriented programming principles.
* Understand engineering workflows, simulation processes, and associated business logic.
* Build,enhance, and maintain APIs and backend services.
* Participate in architecture discussions, design reviews, and code reviews.
* Troubleshoot,debug, and resolve software issues.
* Work within Agile development methodologies and contribute to the complete software development lifecycle.
