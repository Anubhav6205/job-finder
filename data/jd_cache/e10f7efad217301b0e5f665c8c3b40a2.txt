**Your key responsibilities**

* As Scrum Master you support DAC cross functional agile teams working on implementing end-to-end functionalities.
* You facilitate Scrum ceremonies and closely collaborate with all Squad team members to ensure that a healthy prioritized product backlog is maintained, and Tribe and Squad goals are understood by everyone in the Squad.
* You help the Squad to continuously improve the effectiveness of their incremental delivery of value to clients and stakeholders. Furthermore, you identify inefficiencies in the Squad operation and remove impediments to the Squad's progress.
* You live and promote an agile mindset and culture within the Tribe. You act as a coach and facilitator within the team for self-organization and cross-functionality – and deliver agile training within your Squads on demand.
You create physiologically safe environments for the squads, enabling them to their own outcomes, surface dependencies & risk and escalate in a timely manner.  

  

**Your skills and experience**

* You have several years of experience working in agile environments and applying agile methodologies and frameworks in practice (Scrum, Kanban), ideally in a scaled agile environment.
* You have at least one year of experience working as a Scrum Master for one or several Scrum teams.
* You are used to proactively structure and manage your workload and have very good organizational, communication and conflict management skills.
* You have strong motivational skills and practical experience in coaching others to continuously improve in their job.
You are a very good team player and are used to collaborate with stakeholders of diverse backgrounds and seniority levels. Furthermore, you can communicate fluently in English
