**About the Role:**

  

We are looking for an experienced, technically strong and innovative **Senior Automation Engineer** to join our SAP Infrastructure Engineering team. The successful candidate will play a key role in transforming infrastructure operations through intelligent automation, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), DevOps practices and cloud-native technologies.

This role requires a hands-on engineer who can identify automation opportunities, design scalable solutions and drive end-to-end implementation to improve operational efficiency, infrastructure reliability, security and service delivery.

We are particularly interested in candidates who have experience integrating **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI or intelligent automation capabilities** into infrastructure operations.

  

**Key Responsibilities:**

  

**1. Infrastructure Automation & Engineering**

  

* Design, develop and maintain automation solutions for SAP infrastructure hosted on Microsoft Azure.
* Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration, patching, maintenance, monitoring and lifecycle management.
* Develop reusable automation frameworks using **Ansible, Python, PowerShell and Terraform/Bicep** .
* Identify manual and repetitive operational activities and replace them with reliable, scalable automation.
* Build self-service automation capabilities for infrastructure operations.
* Develop robust error handling, logging, auditing and automated recovery mechanisms.
Integrate automation with existing enterprise infrastructure platforms and operational processes.  

  

**2. DevOps & Infrastructure as Code**

  

* Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions.
* Develop and manage Infrastructure as Code frameworks using Terraform, Bicep or ARM templates.
* Implement Git-based version control, peer reviews, automated testing and deployment standards.
* Build reusable pipeline templates and modular infrastructure automation components.
Ensure automation follows enterprise security, compliance and governance requirements.  

  

**3. Azure Cloud & SAP Infrastructure**

  

* Work closely with SAP Basis, Cloud Infrastructure, Security and Platform Engineering teams.
* Develop automation for Azure virtual machines, storage, networking, backup and disaster recovery operations.
* Support automated deployment, configuration and maintenance of SAP infrastructure environments.
* Automate activities such as VM start/stop, OS patching, disk management, backup validation and infrastructure health checks.
* Improve consistency, resilience and operational efficiency across production and non-production environments.
Contribute to standardisation of cloud infrastructure engineering practices.  

  

**4. AI Integration & Intelligent Operations — Added Advantage**

  

* Explore opportunities to introduce **AI-powered automation and AIOps** into infrastructure engineering and operations.
* Integrate Generative AI capabilities using Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Foundry or equivalent enterprise AI platforms.
* Develop AI-assisted operational workflows, intelligent troubleshooting solutions and automated incident analysis.
* Explore integration of LLMs with enterprise tools, APIs, monitoring platforms and automation frameworks.
* Build AI-powered assistants or agents to support infrastructure teams with operational tasks, knowledge retrieval and diagnostics.
* Apply AI to log analysis, anomaly detection, incident correlation, root cause investigation and operational recommendations.
Ensure AI-based solutions comply with enterprise security, access controls and responsible AI principles.  

  

**5. Operational Excellence & Continuous Improvement**

  

* Drive improvements in infrastructure reliability, operational efficiency and engineering productivity.
* Identify opportunities to reduce manual effort, eliminate recurring incidents and improve service quality.
* Build monitoring, alerting and automated remediation capabilities.
* Create and maintain technical documentation, operational runbooks and automation standards.
* Collaborate with engineering teams to identify and prioritise high-value automation use cases.
Track and demonstrate automation outcomes through measurable improvements in efficiency, reliability and cost.  

  

**Required Technical Skills**

**Core Requirements**

  

* Strong hands-on experience in **Python and PowerShell scripting** .
* Proven experience implementing infrastructure automation using **Ansible** .
* Strong knowledge of **Microsoft Azure infrastructure services** , including compute, storage, networking and identity.
* Practical experience with **Terraform, Bicep or ARM templates** .
* Experience designing and implementing **CI/CD pipelines** using Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions.
* Strong understanding of REST APIs, JSON, YAML and system integrations.
* Experience with Git, software development best practices, debugging and automated testing.
* Working knowledge of Windows and Linux operating systems.
* Understanding of identity, access management, secrets management and security controls.
Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving capabilities.  

  

**Desirable Skills**

  

* Experience working with SAP infrastructure or other enterprise mission-critical platforms.
* Knowledge of SAP HANA infrastructure, high availability and disaster recovery.
* Experience with Azure Monitor, Log Analytics and KQL.
* Experience with ITSM integrations such as ServiceNow or Jira.
* Familiarity with containers, Docker and Kubernetes.
Knowledge of infrastructure observability, event-driven automation and auto-remediation.  

  

**AI & Emerging Technology Skills – Highly Desirable**

  

We would particularly welcome candidates with practical exposure to:

* Azure OpenAI Service and Azure AI Foundry.
* LLM integrations using Python, REST APIs or SDKs.

* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and enterprise knowledge assistants.
* Model Context Protocol (MCP) or equivalent tool-integration frameworks.
* AIOps and intelligent event correlation.
* AI-assisted incident management and root cause analysis.
Integration of AI capabilities with Ansible, Azure DevOps or infrastructure management systems.  

  

**Experience & Qualifications**

  

* Relevant experience in cloud infrastructure engineering, platform engineering, DevOps or infrastructure automation.
* Proven track record of independently designing and delivering production-grade automation solutions.
* Strong understanding of enterprise infrastructure operations and engineering practices.
* Experience collaborating with cross-functional engineering and operations teams.
* Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or equivalent practical experience.
Relevant Microsoft Azure, DevOps or automation certifications are advantageous.  

  

**What Success Looks Like:**

  

Within the first 6–12 months, the successful candidate will be expected to:

* Identify and deliver high-value automation use cases across SAP infrastructure operations.
* Reduce repetitive manual activities and improve infrastructure service delivery.
* Develop reusable, secure and maintainable automation frameworks.
* Improve integration between Azure infrastructure, operational processes and CI/CD pipelines.
* Demonstrate measurable improvements in operational efficiency, reliability and engineering productivity.
Identify and, where feasible, deliver AI-enabled operational automation use cases.  

  

**Ideal Candidate Profile:**

  

We are looking for an engineer who is:

  

* **Hands-on:** Comfortable writing, debugging and maintaining production-grade automation code.
* **Automation-first:** Consistently challenges manual processes and seeks scalable solutions.
* **Solution-oriented:** Capable of translating operational challenges into practical engineering solutions.
* **Independent:** Able to own technical deliverables from design through implementation.
* **Innovative:** Interested in applying AI and emerging technologies to real infrastructure challenges.
* **Collaborative:** Works effectively across infrastructure, SAP Basis, security and cloud engineering teams.
* **Outcome-driven:** Focuses on measurable improvements rather than automation for its own sake.

  

**Our goal is to move from traditional infrastructure operations towards a highly automated, intelligent and self-service engineering model.**
