**The Job**
-----------

The Analytics Engineer will build and maintain the data pipelines behind Omnidian's performance and financial reporting. Our platform is moving away from API-driven workflows toward set-based, SQL-first pipelines, and this role sits at the center of that shift.

This role is roughly 80% data and 20% backend engineering. Most of your time goes to SQL transformations and data modeling, done with software engineering habits: testing, versioning, code review, and clean deployments. The team is upstream of most of the company's data, so your work reaches Finance, Operations, Product, and leadership reporting.

### **What You'll Do**

At Omnidian we believe in trust and autonomy. How you create an impact is ultimately up to you. Here is an outline of some of the things you’ll be doing:

**Pipeline Development & Migration (50%)**

* Migrate existing pipelines and reports to our new reporting architecture with little or no noticeable impact to the people who rely on them.
* Build simple, scalable data pipelines in SQL and Python that the next engineer can maintain long after you've moved on.
* Write tests, version your work, and follow sound deployment practices.
* Use AI coding assistants as a core part of daily development to ship pipelines, tests, and documentation faster, while owning and understanding every line you commit. Help set the team's standards for AI-assisted development: review practices, guardrails, and reusable prompts and workflows.

**Platform Ownership & Optimization (30%)**

* Learn the wider data platform on your own: where data lives, who consumes it, and how other teams' changes affect yours, including reading their pull requests.
* Spot performance and cost problems in a design before they ship.
* Recommend the simplest tool that solves the problem, and help retire tools that no longer fit.

**Cross-Team Collaboration (20%)**

* Work directly with Product, Operations, Finance, and Legal to understand their needs and find new places the team can help.
* Communicate clearly in your own words, showing real understanding of the work.
* Adapt as priorities shift, and keep momentum when plans change.

### **Who You Are**

* A hybrid of analytics and engineering: strong in data, comfortable with backend development. Not only a report writer, and not only an application developer.
* Independent. You look for room to improve without being asked, keep up with what's happening across the department, and aren't afraid to suggest a simpler way of doing things.
* A communicator beyond engineering. You engage in good faith with non-technical teams, and you know those relationships are where the most valuable work comes from.
* Platform agnostic. You choose tools because they solve a real problem, not out of habit.
* AI-forward and disciplined. AI is central to how you build, and you hold its output to the same bar as your own work: small, readable, tested changes you can explain line by line. Your written communication reflects your own understanding, not pasted AI output.
* Calm under change. You stay productive and positive when plans shift.

### **Experience You’ll Need**

* 7+ years in a data engineering or analytics engineering role, building and supporting production data pipelines.
* 7+ years of advanced SQL (window functions, CTEs, incremental models, query tuning) on a cloud warehouse or lakehouse: Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, or equivalent.
* 5+ years of Python for data work, including PySpark, pandas, or equivalent.
* 3+ years of backend development in a typed language and framework, such as Java or Kotlin with Spring Boot, Python with type hints and FastAPI, TypeScript with Node, or equivalent.
* Hands-on testing experience with pytest, JUnit, dbt tests, or equivalent. You write tests as part of the work, not after it.
* Git-based development with pull request review and CI/CD (GitHub Actions, Bitbucket Pipelines, Jenkins, or equivalent), including versioned, repeatable deployments.
* 1+ year of AI-assisted development as a daily practice, using Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or equivalent. You can show concrete examples where AI sped up delivery without lowering code quality.
* Senior-level ownership: you've set technical direction for a pipeline or platform area, reviewed others' code, and mentored less experienced engineers.
* Led at least two platform migrations end to end (for example a warehouse, ETL tool, or BI platform move) with minimal disruption to downstream users.
* Practical platform administration: cluster and warehouse sizing, partitioning, query profiling, and cost monitoring. You can explain a time you prevented a performance or cost problem before it shipped.
* Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, statistics, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
