**JOB OVERVIEW**

  

About Arm
  
Arm Limited is the company behind the world's most widely used compute architecture. Headquartered
  
in Cambridge, UK, Arm designs the CPU architecture and processor IP that our partners build into
  
hundreds of billions of chips – from smartphones and IoT devices to vehicles, robots and data centres.
  
As compute moves into the physical world, Arm technology is at the heart of the automotive and
  
Physical AI systems where safety and security are non-negotiable.
  
About the team
  
The Arm CE Quality and Compliance team is responsible for how Arm products meet Functional Safety
  
(ISO 26262, IEC 61508), Automotive Cybersecurity (ISO/SAE 21434) and Quality requirements. We
  
work alongside Arm's CPU, SoC, software and ML engineering teams and with our automotive and
  
robotics partners, from early development through external assessment and certification. Our products
  
– including Arm processors, compute subsystems and software self-test libraries – are relied on by the
  
world's leading automotive and industrial companies.
  
Job Description
  
Arm is looking for a Functional Safety Engineer with a focus on tooling, automation and AI to join the
  
Arm CE Quality and Compliance team.
  
In this role you will support Functional Safety, Automotive Cybersecurity and Quality activities, working
  
closely with engineering and assurance teams to explore and implement process-improvement and
  
automation solutions. These will include data analytics and the use of AI to support – not replace –
  
human judgement on safety and security.
  
Youwill implement and maintain practical, scalable compliance solutions for ISO 26262, IEC 61508 and
  
ISO/SAE 21434.

**RESPONSIBILITIES**

* Identify and evaluate opportunities to improve productivity through automation, data analytics and AI.
* Design and implement AI-powered assistants and autonomous or semi-autonomous agents that

support engineering activities.
  

* Develop automated solutions for work-product quality checks, maturity assessments, consistency

reviews and engineering-process monitoring.
  

* Integrate automation solutions with existing engineering tools, databases and enterprise systems.
* Develop data-analytics solutions and dashboards to monitor engineering performance, quality and

process effectiveness.
  

* Establish appropriate validation, monitoring and human oversight for AI-supported engineering

decisions, ensuring compliance with the requirements of ISO 26262, IEC 61508, ISO/SAE 21434,
  
ISO/PAS8800 (Safety and AI) or similar standards.
  

* Collaborate with engineering teams, process owners and IT to translate business needs into scalable

technical solutions.
  

* Measure and continuously improve the effectiveness, reliability and adoption of the solutions you

deliver.

**REQUIRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE**

* Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Engineering or a related

technical discipline.
  

* Strong software development experience, including building automation solutions.
* Practical experience developing AI/LLM-based applications, assistants or agentic workflows.
* Knowledge of LLM integration, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), API

integration and evaluation of AI output.
  

* Experience with data analytics, data processing and visualisation.
* Understanding of modern software engineering practice, including version control, automated testing

and deployment.
  

* Ability to work across technical and organisational boundaries in complex engineering environments.
* Strong communication, stakeholder-management and problem-solving skills.

**NICE-TO-HAVE SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE**

* Practical knowledge of ISO 26262, ISO/SAE 21434, Automotive SPICE, IEC 61508 or similar

standards.
  

* Familiarity with ISO/PAS8800 (Road vehicles – Safety and artificial intelligence) and emerging

guidance on the use of AI in safety-related engineering.
  

* Experience in a safety-critical or regulated engineering environment.
* Experience with requirements/ALM tools (e.g. Jira, Polarion, DOORS) and their APIs.

**IN RETURN**

In this role you will support Functional Safety, Automotive Cybersecurity and Quality activities, working
