**Job Description**
**What you'll bring**
You have foundational programming skills in Go Lang / C# /Java, Python, JavaScript and a genuine curiosity about how AI-native applications work. A basic understanding of APIs, microservices architecture, CI/CD practices, and cloud fundamentals is expected.
  
  
Awareness of AI/LLM concepts, including prompt engineering, AI-assisted development, and basic evaluation techniques, is a plus. Familiarity with testing, debugging, logging, and structured development workflows will help you contribute from the start.
  
  
1 to 3 years of relevant experience in software development with master’s or bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering or equivalent.
  
  
**What You'll Build**
You'll contribute to AI-enabled application features in SAP Concur Travel through coding, testing, and debugging. LLM integration tasks, prompt testing, and AI output validation are part of the regular work. You'll help prepare evaluation datasets and participate in benchmarking workflows. System logs, metrics, and application behavior are things you'll monitor to spot issues and support observability improvements. CI/CD pipelines, deployment support, and basic automation workflows are areas where you'll contribute, along with maintaining clear documentation.
  
  
**Where you belong**
You will join the SAP Concur Travel Engineering AI & Core team in Labs India. SAP Concur offers cloud-based services that make it simple to manage travel and expenses. SAP Concur supports more than 54,000 businesses and 96 million users across 150+ countries, processing over one billion transactions annually.
  
  
You'll work in a collaborative, global engineering environment with access to mentorship and structured learning. Knowledge sharing and honest technical feedback are part of how this team operates. You'll gain hands-on exposure to AI-first systems and modern development practices, not just observe them. If you're early in your career and want to build real technical depth in AI-native application engineering, this is a good fit.
  
  
**Skills**
**We value the skills you bring and support you to build on them through our learning and development opportunities. These are the skills associated with this role:**
**Core Skills-**
Foundational skills in Golang/C#, cloud basics (AWS/GCP/Azure), RDBMS, (SQL Server or MySql etc)REST APIs, and awareness of AI/ML and data pipeline concepts.
  
  
*AI Adoption Capability, AI output quality assurance, AI-assisted development, context engineering, agentic orchestration, agentic engineering, application security, cloud architecture, observability, semantic retrieval, high availability engineering, troubleshooting, evaluations and benchmarking engineering, CI/CD.*
