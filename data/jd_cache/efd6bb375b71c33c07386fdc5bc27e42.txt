Our Finance team in Pune is looking for a Senior Payroll Analyst to own accurate, compliant, and on-time payroll for our growing India workforce, while helping scale payroll operations across APAC. You will bring strong statutory expertise and a data-first mindset, using the Snowflake AI Data Cloud to make payroll smarter and faster.

**AS A SENIOR PAYROLL ANALYST AT SNOWFLAKE, YOU WILL:**

* Own end-to-end monthly payroll processing for India, partnering with our payroll vendor to ensure every employee is paid accurately and on time.
* Ensure full compliance with Indian statutory requirements, including PF, ESI, Professional Tax, LWF, TDS, and Gratuity, and manage timely filings and remittances.
* Build payroll reconciliations, variance analyses, and reporting on Snowflake, turning HR and Finance data into trusted, audit-ready insights.
* Partner with HR, Total Rewards, Equity, and Accounting teams to process new hires, exits, bonuses, RSU taxation, and full and final settlements.
* Use Snowflake Cortex AI and automation to streamline manual payroll workflows, validations, and employee query handling.
* Support internal and external audits, SOX controls, and month-end close activities for payroll-related accounts.
* Serve as a trusted point of contact for employee payroll and tax questions, including investment declarations and Form 16.
* Contribute to payroll expansion and process standardization across APAC markets as Snowflake grows.

**OUR IDEAL SENIOR PAYROLL ANALYST WILL HAVE:**

* 5+ years of payroll experience, with hands-on ownership of India payroll in a multinational environment.
* Deep knowledge of Indian payroll legislation, labour laws, and income tax rules.
* Experience managing third-party payroll providers and working with an HRIS such as Workday.
* Strong analytical skills and advanced Excel proficiency, with a track record of building reconciliations and reports.
* High attention to detail and a strong sense of ownership over confidential data.
* Clear, empathetic communication skills with employees and cross-functional partners.
* Experience supporting audits and working within SOX or similar control frameworks.

**BONUS POINTS FOR THE FOLLOWING:**

* Working knowledge of SQL or experience querying data in Snowflake.
* Payroll experience in other APAC countries such as Japan, Singapore, or Australia.
* Experience with equity compensation taxation (RSUs, ESPP).
* A professional qualification such as CA Inter, CMA, or a payroll certification.
* Experience in a high-growth technology company.
