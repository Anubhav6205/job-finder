**Job Summary:**  
The Senior Electrical Design Engineer leads the design and development of electrical systems, ensuring compliance with standards and project requirements.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Design electrical systems, panels, and circuits
* Prepare and review technical drawings and schematics
* Lead design projects and mentor junior engineers
* Ensure compliance with electrical standards and safety norms
* Coordinate with cross-functional teams and clients
* Conduct design validation and troubleshooting

**Skills & Requirements:**

* Strong knowledge of electrical design principles
* Experience with tools like AutoCAD, EPLAN, or similar
* Understanding of LT/HT systems and control panels
* Leadership and problem-solving skills
