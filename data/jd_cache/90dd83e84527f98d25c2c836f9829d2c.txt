**THE ROLE**

You'll build and own core parts of Securly AI Chat, a production generative AI assistant used every school day by K–12 students, teachers and admins. The work is mostly backend in Go: real-time chat streaming, integrations with multiple LLM providers, privacy and access controls, and the AWS services that run it all. You'll own features end to end, from design through production support.

AI Chat has two sides. One is Securly's own AI assistant built for students. The other gives schools visibility and control over third-party AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini through our browser extension. Both are managed from a single policy editor and reviewed through transcripts and an AI transparency dashboard.

This is a senior, hands-on engineering role in Pune Engineering, reporting to the Engineering Manager.

**WHAT YOU'LL BUILD**

* **Multi-provider LLM engine** — primary and fallback providers, plus per-policy model selection, so a school can run different models for staff and students.
* **Real-time chat** — Go services that stream responses over WebSockets, handle reconnects cleanly, resume conversations, and stay fast on tablets.
* **Safety and privacy layers** — PII masking before data leaves our systems, safety and deflection logic, role-based access, and audit logging for sensitive data.
* **Third-party AI monitoring** — backend services that extend coverage of popular generative AI tools used in classrooms.
* **Knowledge and memory** — retrieval with PostgreSQL and pgvector, and real-time knowledge sources that go beyond Wikipedia.
* **Compliance-ready features** — capabilities that meet emerging standards for generative AI in education, including the UK Department for Education's product safety expectations.
* **Platform health** — observability through structured logging, tests, accurate API docs, and steady paydown of tech debt.
