**Who we are looking for**

The State Street Middleware Application Infrastructure Team supports multiple Web and Application server software suites across the Bank. We provide managed services for the packages like JBoss, WebSphere, IIS, Tomcat (RedHat JWS) and other Middleware technologies. The successful candidate will provide technical expertise and leadership to the Web Operations Team. The position requires effective communication and technical skills with a willingness to learn and implement best-in-class tools and strategies. The candidate must be willing to design and implement solutions, work on new technology projects and improve the current Web Application Stack.

The ideal candidate has a strong operational mindset, hands‑on scripting expertise, and a passion for reducing manual effort, improving reliability, and driving continuous improvement. Exposure to **AI‑assisted development or intelligent automation** is a strong plus.

**What you will be responsible for**

As Middleware Web SRE Engineer you will

· Provide Level 3+ technical support for Middleware Web Application Services for the State Street Corporation.

· Manage and train Operational personnel

· Effectively participate and lead ITIL processes including Incident, Problem, Change, Capacity management

· Serve as a client liaison and a point of escalation for internal and external customers

· Work with the engineering team on continuous service improvement focusing on automation and service standardization.

· Initiate and manage vulnerability remediation, patching, upgrades, and hardware life cycle to ensure we remain current with technology solutions and meet client needs.

  

**What we value**

These skills will help you succeed in this role

· Strong knowledge in web server and web application technologies - , JBoss and JBCS, IBM WebSphere, IBM HTTPD Server, Microsoft IIS, Tomcat, Cloud Technologies (AWS, Azure).

· Strong knowledge of SSL and SSH security, protocols, and key management.

· Proficient knowledge and hands-on experience with Shell Scripting, Python Programming, Ansible Core/Tower and Terraform, Cloud (AWS/Azure).

· Ability to multitask, manage high-priority initiatives and coordinate the activities with Service Delivery and Engineering teams to completion.

· Ability to work in a team setting with a diverse group: operations support resources, SSC client services representatives, application and service delivery teams.

  

**Education & Preferred Qualifications**

* 8+ year of IT experience in Middleware
* Bachelor in Computer Science or equivalent
  

**Work Schedule**

* No Hybrid Working. It is fully Working from Office
* Schedule – APAC (6 am to 3 pm) or EMEA (2 pm to 11 pm)
* Location – Hyderabad or Bangalore (Candidates not willing to relocate need not apply)
  

**Why this role is important to us**

The team you will be joining is a part of Platform Engineering Middleware, a function that is vital to the company as we execute over 2 million job runs per day in Autosys, support 3,500 web sites on over 3,000 servers (both internal and external facing), 5 Billion messages per day traverse Kafka and MQ across more than 150,000 queues, and we handle over 2 million file transfers per day. State Street is one of 6 banks designated by the federal reserve as GSIB (Global Systemically Important Bank) with nearly 4 trillion USD in AUM. Our Middleware platforms touch all areas of the company and are the vital plumbing serving all of the banks activities. As a GSIB we operate under a strict oversight program from the Federal Reserve and have to ensure our systems are operating at full capacity 24/7.
