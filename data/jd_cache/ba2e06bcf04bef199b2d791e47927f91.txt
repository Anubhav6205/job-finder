**In this role, you will:**

* Lead the architecture, design, development, testing, deployment, and production support of **enterprise-grade GenAI and Agentic AI solutions**.
* Design and build **AI agents and multi-agent systems** capable of planning, reasoning, tool utilization, workflow orchestration, human-in-the-loop execution, and controlled autonomous actions.
* Build solutions leveraging **LLMs, foundation models, RAG, embeddings, vector databases, knowledge retrieval, prompt engineering, context engineering, and structured tool/function calling**.
* Develop agentic solutions using frameworks such as **LangGraph, LangChain, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen, CrewAI or equivalent enterprise AI orchestration frameworks**. These frameworks are specifically represented in current Wells Fargo GenAI/Agentic AI engineering requirements.
* Develop reusable AI components, APIs, services, agent patterns, prompt libraries, evaluation frameworks, and reference implementations.
* Build robust **Python-based AI services, APIs, automation capabilities and data-processing pipelines**.
* Define technical patterns for integrating GenAI capabilities with enterprise applications, APIs, databases, knowledge stores, engineering platforms, and business workflows.
* Establish engineering approaches for **LLMOps/MLOps, AI observability, evaluation, benchmarking, monitoring and production support**.
* Implement appropriate AI guardrails addressing hallucination, data leakage, prompt injection, inappropriate tool invocation, access controls and unsafe agent behavior.
* Apply **Responsible AI, AI security, privacy, risk, compliance and governance** requirements throughout the solution lifecycle.
* Participate in architecture reviews, technical design reviews, code reviews and engineering governance.
* Partner closely with product owners, architects, engineers, cybersecurity, risk, data, platform and business teams to translate business outcomes into appropriate technical solutions.
* Drive engineering excellence through **CI/CD, automated testing, DevSecOps, observability, resiliency, performance engineering and operational readiness**.
* Diagnose and resolve complex technical problems involving AI models, applications, data, integrations, APIs and agent workflows.
* Mentor engineers and provide technical direction on **GenAI, Agentic AI and modern software engineering practices**.
* Evaluate emerging AI technologies through hands-on experimentation and convert successful approaches into reusable enterprise patterns.
* Contribute to standards, reference architectures and engineering best practices that enable safe adoption of AI at enterprise scale.

**Required Qualifications:**

* **5+ years of Specialty Software Engineering or Software Engineering experience**, or equivalent demonstrated through work experience, training, military experience, or education, consistent with current Wells Fargo Lead Specialty Software Engineer requirements.
* Strong hands-on software engineering experience using **Python** and modern application-development practices.
* Demonstrated experience building production-grade **Generative AI and/or Agentic AI solutions**.
* Strong understanding of **LLMs, foundation models, RAG, prompt engineering, embeddings, vector search and AI orchestration**.
* Hands-on experience with one or more frameworks such as **LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, AutoGen, CrewAI or equivalent**.
* Experience designing API-based, microservices-oriented and cloud-native architectures.
* Strong understanding of software engineering fundamentals including design patterns, automated testing, API design, CI/CD, security and observability.

**Desired Qualifications:**

* Experience building enterprise-scale GenAI platforms supporting multiple applications and use cases.

* Knowledge of **Responsible AI and AI governance**, including model risk, privacy, security, auditability and regulatory controls.
