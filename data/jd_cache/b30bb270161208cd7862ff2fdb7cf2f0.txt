**ML ENGINEER / DATA SCIENTIST**

  

**Role Overview**

Develop machine learning solutions by preparing and analysing data, building predictive models and evaluating model performance to solve business problems.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Collect, clean, transform and analyse structured datasets.
* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) and identify patterns, trends and useful features.
* Develop, train, validate and evaluate machine learning models.
* Apply appropriate algorithms for classification, regression, forecasting or related use cases.
* Use model evaluation metrics and validation techniques to assess performance.
* Collaborate with engineering teams to integrate models into applications and workflows.
* Document experiments, results and model improvements.

  

**Required Skills and Qualifications**

* Strong Python programming skills.
* Hands-on experience with Pandas and NumPy.
* Understanding of statistics, data analysis and exploratory data analysis.
* Practical knowledge of machine learning algorithms and model evaluation.
* Experience with scikit-learn.
* Exposure to XGBoost or LightGBM.
* Good problem-solving skills and familiarity with Git.

  

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience deploying models, working with real-world datasets, feature engineering or cloud-based ML workflows.

Candidates should be comfortable working from the Hyderabad office.
