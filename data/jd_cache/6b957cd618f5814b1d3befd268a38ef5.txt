AI Engineer
  
  
Role Summary
  
  
We are seeking a hands-on AI Engineer to build, deploy, and support production-grade Agentic AI and multi-agent solutions on AWS. This is an engineering-focused role requiring strong software development expertise, rapid prototyping capabilities, and the ability to transform successful AI concepts into secure, scalable, and production-ready solutions.
  
  
Key Responsibilities
  
  

* Build and deploy Agentic AI and multi-agent solutions using Python, LangGraph, and LangChain.
* Develop POCs and rapidly transition successful approaches into production.
* Implement orchestration, tool calling, state/memory management, checkpointing, HITL, MCP, and A2A capabilities.
* Develop RAG and LLM-based solutions integrated with enterprise applications, APIs, and data platforms.
* Build and operate AI services using AWS, Amazon Bedrock, and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
* Develop production APIs and integration services.
* Implement automated testing, CI/CD, AI evaluation, guardrails, observability, and security controls.
* Optimize production solutions for performance, reliability, scalability, latency, and cost.
* Collaborate with teams to share reusable patterns, best practices, and AI engineering innovations.
* Provide AI engineering consulting and technical guidance on Agentic AI implementation and adoption.

Qualifications
  
  

* 5+ years of experience in Software Engineering, AI/ML Engineering, or related development roles.
* Strong Python software engineering skills.
* Hands-on experience with LangGraph, LangChain, Agentic AI, and multi-agent systems.
* Strong experience with AWS, Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, MCP, and A2A.
* Strong understanding of RAG, LLMs, embeddings, vector search, tool calling, state/memory management, and HITL.
* Experience with FastAPI, APIs, Docker, Git, CI/CD, automated testing, AI evaluation, guardrails, observability, and production support.
* Ability to independently deliver high-quality production solutions from established designs.

Mandatory Experience
  
  

* Proven experience building, deploying, and supporting at least two production-grade multi-agent AI solutions.
* Production experience is essential; certification, tutorial, hackathon, or POC-only experience is not sufficient.

Preferred Skills
  
  

* Terraform or CloudFormation.
* Kubernetes/EKS.
* OpenTelemetry or LangSmith.
* SageMaker or MLflow.
* Azure and/or GCP exposure.
* Experience integrating AI solutions with SAP environments.

Preferred Certifications
  
  

* AWS Certified Generative AI Developer – Professional.
* AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer – Associate.
