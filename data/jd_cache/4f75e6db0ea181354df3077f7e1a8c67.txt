Cloud Sales Executive - Startup (Inside sales representative)  
**Location:** Bangalore  
**Experience:** 1-2 years  
**Department:** Startup & Cloud Sales  
**Reporting To:** Head of Startup Business  
About the Role  
We are looking for a driven Startup Sales Executive to build and grow our startup business in Bangalore.  
The role will focus on prospecting, building the sales funnel, generating qualified opportunities and converting them into business, while developing strong relationships with startup founders, technology leaders and the AWS ecosystem.  
Key Responsibilities

* Build and develop the startup market by identifying and engaging potential customers through cold calling, LinkedIn, email, networking, referrals, and ecosystem partnerships pan india.
* Work alongside the regional startup teams on identifying new logos and also progressing all existing cases.
* Own the sales funnel from prospect identification and initial engagement through qualification, opportunity development, proposal, negotiation and closure.
* Engage with startup founders, CTOs and technology decision-makers to understand their business priorities, technology landscape, challenges and growth plans.
* Identify and develop business opportunities across cloud infrastructure, cloud migration, managed services, GenAI, GPU infrastructure and other technology solutions.
* Proactively engage with AWS Account Managers, Solution Architects and relevant partner teams to identify opportunities, build joint customer engagements and accelerate deal progression.
* Work closely with internal sales, presales, delivery and alliance teams to develop suitable solutions, prepare proposals, coordinate proof of concepts (POCs), develop commercials and progress opportunities to closure.
* Drive new customer acquisition and revenue generation by converting qualified opportunities into closed business and developing long-term customer relationships.
* Maintain a healthy sales pipeline with clear opportunity stages, expected deal values, closure timelines, next steps and forecast accuracy.
* Identify expansion opportunities within existing startup accounts to grow customer relationships and generate additional revenue.
* Maintain accurate CRM records and provide regular updates on prospecting activity, pipeline development, deal progression, revenue performance and risks.
* Stay informed about startup ecosystem developments, emerging technology needs, cloud adoption trends and relevant AWS programs to identify new business opportunities.

What We're Looking For

* 1-2 years of experience in sales, business development or inside sales. Experience in B2B technology, SaaS, cloud or IT services is preferred.
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage confidently with startup founders and technology decision-makers.
* Comfortable with cold calling, outbound prospecting, lead generation and persistent follow-ups.
* Demonstrated ability to identify prospects, qualify opportunities, build relationships and progress sales conversations.
* Strong ownership, execution discipline and a target-oriented mindset.
* Curious about cloud computing, GenAI, GPU infrastructure and emerging technology solutions, with a willingness to learn and communicate their business value.
* Ability to work collaboratively with sales, presales, delivery, alliances and external technology partners.
* Basic understanding of startups, SaaS, cloud technology or the IT services industry is an advantage.
* Proactive, resilient and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with clear performance expectations.

Key Success Metrics  
Performance will be evaluated across the following areas:

* **New customer acquisition:** Relevant startup accounts engaged and new customers acquired.
* **Qualified meetings:** Customer meetings generated and completed with relevant decision-makers.
* **Pipeline generation:** Qualified opportunities created, with measurable pipeline value.
* **Pipeline conversion:** Progression of opportunities across sales stages and conversion into wins.
* **Revenue achievement:** New business closed against assigned targets, including recurring revenue where applicable.
* **AWS ecosystem engagement:** Qualified opportunities identified and progressed through AWS relationships and joint customer engagements.
* **Account growth:** Expansion opportunities identified and additional business generated from existing customers.
* **Sales execution:** CRM accuracy, timely follow-ups, forecast reliability, and adherence to sales processes.
* **Internal and external stakeholder coordination**
