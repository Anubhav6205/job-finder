**Job Description**
**About the Role**
We are looking for a
**Senior AI Engineer**
with deep expertise in Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Systems, MCP (Model Context Protocol), and LLM-powered applications.
  
  
This role requires an engineer who can independently drive AI initiatives from idea to production. You will be responsible for identifying opportunities, building proofs of concept, validating business value, designing scalable architectures, and delivering production-grade AI solutions.
  
  
The ideal candidate has hands-on experience building intelligent agents that can reason, plan, use tools, collaborate with other agents, and solve real business problems.
  
  
**Responsibilities**

* Design, build, and deploy Agentic AI solutions using modern LLMs and AI frameworks.
* Architect and implement multi-agent systems with agent orchestration, planning, memory, and tool usage capabilities.
* Design and develop MCP-based integrations to connect agents with enterprise systems, APIs, data sources, and external tools.
* Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems using enterprise knowledge sources and vector databases.
* Define agent workflows, reasoning strategies, evaluation frameworks, and guardrails for reliable AI behavior.
* Drive AI initiatives from proof-of-concept through production deployment and ongoing optimization.
* Collaborate with product and engineering teams to translate business requirements into AI-powered solutions.
* Build reusable AI components, frameworks, and platform capabilities that accelerate future AI development.
* Establish best practices for prompt engineering, agent design, evaluation, observability, and governance.
* Mentor other engineers and provide technical leadership for AI initiatives.

**Qualifications**
**Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, or a related field.
* 6+ years of software engineering and AI application development experience.
* Proven experience building and deploying production-grade Agentic AI solutions.
* Strong understanding of LLM capabilities, limitations, reasoning patterns, and agent architectures.
* Hands-on experience with multi-agent orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel, OpenAI Agents SDK, or similar platforms.
* Experience building MCP servers, MCP clients, tools, and integrations.
* Strong experience designing and implementing RAG systems and enterprise knowledge retrieval solutions.
* Strong proficiency in Python and modern backend development practices.
* Experience integrating LLM providers such as OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or open-source models.
* Experience building scalable APIs and AI-powered services.
* Excellent system design, debugging, and problem-solving skills.
* Strong communication skills and the ability to work independently with end-to-end ownership.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience with AI evaluation frameworks such as LangSmith, DeepEval, RAGAS, TruLens, or similar tools.
* Experience with vector databases such as Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, Chroma, or Azure AI Search.
* Experience deploying AI solutions on Azure, AWS, or GCP.
* Experience leading technical initiatives and mentoring engineers.

**What We Are Looking For**
We are specifically looking for engineers who have built and shipped production AI agents and multi-agent systems.
  
  
Candidates whose experience is primarily limited to prompt engineering, chatbot development, or traditional machine learning without hands-on ownership of agentic workflows, agent orchestration, MCP integrations, and production AI systems may not be a fit for this role.
