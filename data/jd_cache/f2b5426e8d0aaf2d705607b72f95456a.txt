**Role**
--------

As a Lead - Connected & Digital Engineering, you will be a part of the Emerging Mobility Buisness Unit and will report to the Head - Connected and Digital Platforms.
  
In this role, we are seeking a visionary and execution-focused Engineering Manager to lead the design, scalability, reliability and end-to-end operations of our next-generation Connected Platform built on AWS Cloud. This platform is responsible for ingesting high-volume telemetry from millions of connected devices and vehicles, processing real-time data streams, and exposing robust APIs for our expanding partner ecosystems.
  
You will transition from a traditional siloed engineering focus to leading a comprehensive, cross-disciplinary engineering organization. You will have full ownership over a multi-layered platform spanning everything from on-device firmware up to consumer-facing interfaces and cloud infrastructure.

**A purpose driven role for you**
---------------------------------

Your key responsibilities will include, but not limited to:
  
1. Engineering Leadership & People Management -
  
Lead, mentor, and scale a multi-disciplinary engineering team across SDK, Backend, Frontend, web developers , Data, DevOps and Firmware.
  
Cultivate a culture of engineering excellence, continuous learning, accountability, and psychological safety.
  
Conduct performance evaluations, map out career growth paths, and define success metrics for individual engineering leads.
  
2. Architecture & Technical Strategy -
  
Propose, define, and implement the optimum, consolidated, and scalable Solution Architecture across the cloud and edge ecosystems.
  
Act as the internal Subject Matter Expert (SME) for all things related to Telematics-Cloud connectivity, hardware constraints, and cellular protocols.
  
Participate in high-level hardware platform architecture evaluations to ensure seamless alignment with cloud ingestion capabilities.
  
Architect the Control Tower framework to aggregate data from disparate sources (IoT, Supply Chain, ERP) into a unified, actionable interface.
  
Enforce modern design patterns, code reviews, technical documentation, and rigorous testing methodologies across all sub-teams.
  
3. Delivery, Roadmap & Governance -
  
Collaborate with cross-functional teams (CFT) and business users to construct the platform roadmap and prioritize features optimized for both internal combustion engine (ICE) and electric vehicles (EV).
  
Establish strict project governance protocols across development cycles for both EV and ICE product lines.
  
Build data-driven business cases for architectural upgrades and present technical milestones clearly to executive stakeholders.
  
Lead the evaluation, benchmarking, and shortlisting of third-party Solution Integrators (SIs), establishing clear success criteria for proof-of-concepts (PoCs).
  
Establish real-time KPIs that inform the business cases for future architectural upgrades.
