**Position:** Senior Security Specialist

**Experience:** 5-8 years in Application Security, Offensive Security, or Penetration Testing  
 **Location:** India (Noida, Chennai, Hyderabad, And Bengaluru)

  

**About the Role**

Straive is looking for an experienced, hands-on **Senior Security Specialist** to support its Generative AI security and evaluation services.

The role involves conducting adversarial security assessments of Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications, AI-powered chatbots, and agentic AI systems.

The selected candidate will work closely with Straive's AI engineering and delivery teams, contributing specialized offensive-security expertise, conducting vulnerability assessments, and supporting the development of standardized red-teaming methodologies.

The position requires strong technical capabilities, an investigative mindset, and the ability to translate security findings into actionable recommendations for enterprise clients.

This is primarily a **hands-on technical role** involving security assessments, automation, vulnerability investigation, and collaboration with cross-functional teams.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

**1. Security Testing and Evaluation**

* Conduct hands-on adversarial testing of LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and AI agents.
* Identify and validate vulnerabilities involving prompt injection, jailbreaking, sensitive information disclosure, insecure tool usage, and unauthorized access.
* Evaluate application-level security risks involving APIs, authentication, authorization, and external integrations.
* Investigate complex attack scenarios, reproduce vulnerabilities, and document technical findings.
* Recommend remediation measures and perform validation testing.

**2. Red-Teaming Tools and Automation**

* Work with AI red-teaming frameworks such as Microsoft PyRIT, Promptfoo, NVIDIA garak, and Giskard.
* Develop and customize Python-based scripts for adversarial testing and security automation.
* Combine automated vulnerability scanning with manual security investigations.
* Contribute to reusable test cases, attack scenarios, evaluation datasets, and reporting templates.
* Support improvements in testing processes, automation, and assessment coverage.

**3. Technical Collaboration**

* Collaborate with AI engineers, evaluation specialists, and delivery teams to understand application architectures and potential security risks.
* Participate in technical discussions, security reviews, and vulnerability investigations.
* Provide technical guidance and share relevant expertise with colleagues during assessments.
* Review technical findings, where required, to ensure accuracy and reproducibility.
* Contribute to consistent testing practices and assessment quality.
* Support proof-of-concept demonstrations and technical evaluations when required.
* Execute security assessments within agreed testing boundaries.
* Prepare technical reports covering identified vulnerabilities, supporting evidence, severity, business impact, and remediation recommendations.
