**What You'll Do**
==================

* Configure and customise Amperity, including data sources, identity stitching, segments and destinations.
* Configure and support Airship push notifications, in-app messages, audience targeting and automations.
* Configure and support Mailgun sending domains, templates, webhooks, suppression lists and delivery settings.
* Build and maintain marketing technology SnapLogic flows and API and webhook integrations between marketing platforms, Azure services and other systems.
* Troubleshoot audience, messaging and delivery issues, including email deliverability (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, bounces and suppressions) and push opt-in issues.
* Validate technical campaign readiness, including audience counts, data feeds, templates, test sends and tracking.
* Apply data privacy and consent requirements when handling customer data.
* Coordinate vendor support tickets and escalations, and document permissions, platform procedures and runbooks.
* Monitor platform health and scheduled jobs, and take part in rotational weekend and holiday support coverage for campaign-critical periods.

**Required Skills & Experience**
================================

**Marketing Platforms**
-----------------------

* 4+ years of experience in marketing technology engineering, platform administration or integration engineering.
* Hands-on experience with Amperity, Airship and Mailgun, or demonstrable depth in comparable platforms with the ability to ramp up quickly (for example, Segment, mParticle or Tealium; Braze or OneSignal; SendGrid or Amazon SES).
* SaaS administration experience, including users, roles, permissions and configuration management.

**Integration & Data**
----------------------

* Hands-on experience with SnapLogic or a comparable integration platform (MuleSoft, Boomi, Workato).
* Working knowledge of REST APIs, JSON and webhooks, using tools such as Postman.
* Working SQL skills for audience and data validation, ideally with Azure SQL.
* Understanding of audience data concepts such as identity resolution, segmentation, consent and preference management.
* Email delivery diagnostics, including DNS authentication, bounce handling and reputation basics.

**Delivery & Collaboration**
----------------------------

* Ability to work with marketing operations, engineering and vendor teams to resolve issues.
* Awareness of data privacy regulations such as GDPR and CCPA.
* Clear documentation habits and written and verbal communication with client stakeholders.
* Familiarity with Agile delivery practices and ticketing tools such as Jira.
