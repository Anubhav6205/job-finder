We are looking for a **Pricing Audit Specialist** to lead deep-dive investigations into systemic pricing defects, root-cause analyses, and cross-functional process breakdowns. Serving as a domain expert, this role collaborates with Product, Engineering, and Machine Learning Engineering (MLE) teams to track defect remediation until fixes are permanently deployed in production. This role serves as a subject matter expert within the audit organization and frequently handles the most complex, ambiguous, and business-critical audit matters.

  

**Responsibilities**

* Lead investigations involving significant pricing issues, customer-impacting defects, operational failures, automation risks, and audit escalations
* Perform deep-dive analysis to identify underlying drivers of recurring defects and determine whether controls are functioning as intended
* Collaborate with Operations, Product, Engineering, Analytics, MLE, and Business teams to validate findings and understand root causes
* Provide guidance to junior auditors and help standardise audit methodologies across multiple workflows
* Contribute to the development of defect taxonomies, investigation frameworks, and escalation standards
* Present findings to senior stakeholders and help translate complex observations into actionable recommendations

**Requirements**

* 1-4 years of experience in audit, operational excellence, risk management, or complex analytics
* Strong investigative and analytical skills, with proven experience conducting root-cause analysis and handling complex operational issues
* Background in operational quality auditing, including quality audits, process audits, or quality assurance
* Experience in e-commerce operations or related operational processes
* Understanding of operational risk controls and identifying process gaps
* Capability to independently evaluate process quality and compliance
* Proficiency in analysing operational performance data, metrics, and KPIs

**Nice to have**

* Skills in SQL
* Familiarity with Power BI and Tableau
* Flexibility to use other reporting or BI tools
