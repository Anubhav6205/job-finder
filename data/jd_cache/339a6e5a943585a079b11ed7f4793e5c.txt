We need someone who enjoys solving real-world problems, building reliable technology, and taking responsibility from development to deployment and maintenance.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

**1. Full-Stack Development**

* Build and maintain backend services and REST APIs using Go (Golang).
* Develop responsive web applications using React.js.
* Design and manage MySQL databases and optimize queries.
* Integrate third-party services and APIs.

**2. Cloud, Performance & AI**

* Deploy and maintain applications using AWS.
* Implement caching, performance optimization, and security practices.
* Explore and integrate AI/LLM capabilities, including RAG and AI APIs.
* Work with Git, Docker, and CI/CD processes.

**3. Office IT Support & Maintenance**

* Maintain office computers, laptops, software, and operating systems.
* Troubleshoot hardware, software, printer, Wi-Fi, and network issues.
* Manage IT assets, system security, backups, and software updates.
* Coordinate with vendors for IT repairs and maintenance.

**4. Ownership & Collaboration**

* Take ownership of assigned projects from development to deployment.
* Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code.
* Identify and resolve technical issues independently.
* Collaborate with team members and translate requirements into technical solutions.

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering, or a related field. Strong practical projects may be considered in place of a degree.
* Minimum 1 year of software development experience or relevant internship experience.
* Working knowledge of Go (Golang), React.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, and MySQL.
