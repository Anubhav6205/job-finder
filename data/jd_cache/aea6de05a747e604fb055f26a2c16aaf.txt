**Roles and Responsibilities :**

* Design and develop high-pressure die castings, molds, and tools using Creo, Unigraphics NX, and UG NX software.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure effective communication and project delivery.
* Conduct quality control checks on designed components to meet customer requirements.
* Troubleshoot design issues and optimize designs for improved performance.

**Job Requirements :**

* 2-5 years of experience in aluminum die casting, casting design, die design, or tool design.
* Proficiency in Creo, Unigraphics NX, and UG NX software.
* Strong understanding of high pressure die casting processes and techniques.
* Experience working with 2D drafting tools such as AutoCAD or similar software.
