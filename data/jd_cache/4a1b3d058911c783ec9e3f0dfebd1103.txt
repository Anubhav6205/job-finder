Dodge Construction Network (Dodge) is looking for a seasoned, hands-on python and database developer with systems design and architecture exposure to work in our highly technical and intense development team.

This is a full-time position and reports directly to the VP, Engineering.

***Preferred Location***

Qualified candidates should be based in or near Kochi and able to work from our Kochi office as part of a hybrid schedule.

***Essential Functions***

* **Design and develop backend systems** — Build scalable, reliable, and maintainable server-side applications, APIs, and services
* **Develop and maintain APIs** — Design, implement, document, and optimize RESTful, GraphQL, or other service interfaces
* **Manage databases and data systems** — Design schemas, write efficient queries, manage data integrity, and optimize database performance
* **Ensure system scalability and reliability** — Identify bottlenecks and implement solutions for performance, availability, fault tolerance, and high traffic
* **Troubleshoot and resolve production issues** — Diagnose complex bugs, outages, and performance problems and implement durable fixes
* **Drive continuous technical improvement** — Identify opportunities to improve architecture, development processes, automation, security, observability, and engineering efficiency
