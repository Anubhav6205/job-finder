Meet the Team
  
The Data Center Networking Group at Cisco provides next generation data center and cloud management solutions with its Application Centric Infrastructure products. Be part of a team transforming next gen data center architecture to build products which are uniquely positioned to transform the datacenter and cloud deployment landscape by adopting an application centric policy driven approach whereby the compute, storage and networking needs of applications drive the underlying infrastructure. You will be working on cutting edge products that secure, analyze, monitor application workloads across the globe in public and private clouds.
  
  
You are a driven engineer who thrives on solving complex challenges, embraces new technologies, and is eager to expand your technical expertise. You will join a collaborative team that encourages innovation, supports professional growth, and empowers you to take your skills to the next level.
  
  

* Problem-Solving: Troubleshoot complex software and OS issues, identify root causes, and develop effective solutions.
* Continuous Learning: Explore emerging technologies, testing tools, and automation frameworks to expand your technical capabilities.
* Team Collaboration: Partner with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, high-quality software and achieve shared goals.
* Innovation: Enhance test automation frameworks, optimize testing strategies, and improve software quality and testing efficiency.
* Ownership and Adaptability: Take initiative, adapt to evolving technical challenges, and drive continuous improvement in a dynamic engineering environment.

Minimum Qualifications
  
  

* You have deep understanding of Layer 2 and Layer 3 technologies with strong knowledge on networking protocols.
* You have the ability to proficiently code in any one of the scripting languages such as python, TCL or Perl and very strong in manual testing.
* Problem solving skills in the realm of Layer 2 to Layer 7.
* AI/GenAI knowledge - Strong understanding of AI concepts, tools and applications relevant to the role
* A background with one or more of following areas is a plus: Virtualization, Open flow, Cloud technologies, Intelligent network Services and Large data center

Preferred Qualifications
  
Bachelor's/master’s degree in computer science or related field with 3 years of experience as a software/os test or automation experience.
  

* Strong Analytical and Problem-Solving Skills: Ability to troubleshoot complex issues, identify root causes, and develop effective testing solutions.
