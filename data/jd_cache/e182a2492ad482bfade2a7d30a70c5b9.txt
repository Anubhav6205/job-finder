**Job Summary**  

As a Lean Telco Manager / Senior Manager, you will lead the reinvention of telco operating models for global Communications & Media clients, helping them move from high-CAPEX/OPEX, low-return positions to AI-first, lean and agile operating models. You will shape Lean Telco strategy and value realization roadmaps, drive enterprise-wide cost, productivity and operating model reinvention, lead telco restructuring and separation initiatives, and apply AI-first process reinvention across customer, commercial and technology domains to deliver measurable cost, productivity and cash-flow impact
