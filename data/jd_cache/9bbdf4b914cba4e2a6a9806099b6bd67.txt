**POSITION SUMMARY:**

Responsible for maintaining the integrity of Garmin's production environment and that all moderately complex releases into the environment are well-organized, communicated, and managed.

  

**ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:**

* Partner on process improvements to the whole project lifecycle and release process
* Participate in design/definition activities for moderate-complexity systems, features, and/or process
* Champion the shift-left culture of reliability and delivery performance within software development teams
* Monitor and support moderate-complexity software releases
* Implement improvements to the software lifecycle and production pipeline through automated tools/systems that align with industry best practices
* Implement observability improvements to the software applications and infrastructure
* Coordinate and improve monitoring practices across software applications and infrastructure
* Build and/or maintain tools to generate reports
* Maintain accurate data to facilitate reporting on key reliability SLOs for multiple products/systems
* Improve the teams incident response by nurturing incident playbooks
* Facilitate a healthy culture of post-incident activities throughout the organization
* Through post-incident activities, proactively identify and/or implement reliability improvements and automated mitigations of recurrence
* Participate in in the SRE community to nurture standards, best practices, and training across product owners, software engineers, and other SREs
* Participate in capacity planning to ensure software can scale sufficiently at peak times
* Manage the error budget for one or more products/systems

  

**EDUCATION EXPERIENCE AND SKILLS REQUIRED:**

* Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Software Engineering or Computer Engineering or another technical field plus 4 years experience **OR** equivalent combination of education and relevant experience
* Excellent academics results
* Configuration of complex multi-tiered server applications
* Effective judgment, discretion, and decision-making abilities
* Strong and effective verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills
* Team-oriented, possessing a positive attitude and working well with others

  

**DESIRABLE QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Familiarity with the Agile Manifesto and various Agile practices and frameworks
* Experience applying Lean principles to individual, team, and organizational process
* Proficiency in application languages/frameworks such as Java, SpringBoot, C#, JavaScript, React, Angular
* Experience with scaling cloud native applications in large, high-availability environments
* Experience with moderately complex build and deployment automation
* Experience with DevOps-style tools such as Jenkins, Maven, GitLab, Nexus, RunDeck
* Experience with Infrastructure as Code such as Ansible, Terraform, Salt, Chef, Puppet
* Experience with CDN Providers such as Akamai/Cloudflare
* Experience with APM monitoring tools such as Zabbix, AppDynamics, New Relic, Dynatrace
* Experience with observability tools such as Uptrends, Splunk, Kibana
* Experience with messaging technologies such as RabbitMQ, Kafka
* Experience with scripting languages such as Python, Groovy
* Experience with automated testing tools and testing procedures
* Experience with data technologies such as RDBMS, No-SQL
* Experience with Docker/ Kubernetes
* Experience with Linux Administration
