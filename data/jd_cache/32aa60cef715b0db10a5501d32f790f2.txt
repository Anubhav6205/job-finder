Job Description

**Your Role**

We are looking for a dynamic & self-motivated person who can work in 'Spare Parts Manual’ used by Service Engineers & Atlas Copco Group Customers to find the right parts.

**What you will do:**

* Collect, evaluate, and validate information required for the development of Spare Part Manual while coordinating effectively with internal teams and business partners.
* Create, maintain, and update spare parts books and other technical documentation using various authoring tools and platforms.
* Explore and apply AI and automation to improve existing documentation workflows and productivity.
* Plan, prioritize, and manage projects to ensure alignment with customer requirements, quality, timelines, and business objectives.
* Develop strong product knowledge and collaborate with global customers and stakeholders to support documentation needs.
* Demonstrate a strong commitment to established processes and best practices, ensuring consistent adherence while encouraging others to do the same.
* Create and maintain high-quality documentation, ensuring accuracy and timely updates in response to changing requirements or scope.
* Build and sustain effective working relationships with colleagues, external vendors, and cross-functional teams across the organization through strong interpersonal and collaboration skills.

**To succeed, you will need**

**Skills & Experience:**

* Minimum 2+ years of experience as an engineer & worked on the different type of technical documentation - Spare parts book.
* Experience using illustration creation tools (Isodraw CADprocess would be beneficial)
* Expert in creating/structuring Bill of material (BOM) based on production & service BOM.
* Must have exposure to AI, automation tools & digital documentation solutions.
* Should have basic knowledge of engineering, service, aftermarket processes.
* Should be familiar with different Authoring tools for creating Spare parts book.
* Basic knowledge of PDM & PLM systems
* Strong technical knowledge of compressors and generators.
* Proven ability to deliver within defined timeline with ownership & accountability skills
* Excellent planning, organizing & prioritization skills.
* Customer focused professional with the ability to build strong relations & collaborate effectively across global teams, stakeholders & customers

**Personality Requirements:**

* Collaborative team player with strong communication and interpersonal skills, thriving in multicultural environments.
* Self-driven and adaptable, with a structured and organized approach to work.
* Detail-oriented and execution-focused, delivering high-quality results in fast-paced environments.
* Innovative problem solver with the ability to think creatively and provide practical solutions.
* Customer-centric and business-minded, building strong relationships and driving value for stakeholders.

**Educational Requirements:**

Bachelor of engineering in mechanical engineering with 2-3 years of experience
