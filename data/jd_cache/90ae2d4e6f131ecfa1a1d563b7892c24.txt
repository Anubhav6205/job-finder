**The Role**

**Your role**

Bring your specialist knowledge and experience to a company managing 50% of the world’s MIPS and play a critical role in our modernization mission. You’ll be in good company among our global community of experts leading the future of mainframe modernization. Experts who share knowledge openly and collaborate freely.

In this role, you’ll design, build and maintain the high-volume business logic that runs on the mainframe. At the same time you’ll play a key role in refactoring legacy code to enable modern cloud and mobile applications to seamlessly interact with the mainframe’s core data.

**What you will do**

* **End-to-End Environment Setup:** Deploy containerized \*Agent Builder platform components and cloud infrastructure across sandbox, staging, and production environments using Docker, Kubernetes, and IaC templates.
* **Enterprise Integration:** Build secure API adapters, connectors, and webhooks to integrate AI agents with enterprise platforms like ServiceNow, SharePoint, Confluence, GitHub, and CMDB.
* **Agent Workflow Configuration:** Operationalize \*Agent Builder catalog workflows, tuning prompts, tools, memory, and retrieval mechanisms for production readiness.
* **Security & Observability:** Implement enterprise guardrails, access permissions, logging, distributed tracing, metrics, retry policies, and audit trails for reliable agent execution.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partner directly with architects, engineers, AMS SMEs, and customer stakeholders to resolve blockers and ensure platform alignment.
* **Technical Discovery & Delivery:** Lead workshops, capture requirements, validate data access, and support environment readiness reviews through go-live.
* **Handover & Supportability:** Resolve integration and network defects, and produce deployment runbooks and documentation for operational handover.

### **Required Skills and Experience**

  

* **Experience & Domain:** 6+ years in software, DevOps, platform, cloud, or integration engineering, including 2+ years deploying AI/ML, GenAI, automation, or data-driven platforms in enterprise environments.
* **Enterprise Execution:** Experience leading customer-facing implementations, production support, and security-constrained enterprise deployments.
* **Platform Hands-on:** Hands-on deployment and configuration of Agent Builder or equivalent enterprise agent platforms.
* **Agent Building & Customization:** Proven track record with low-code/no-code workflows, agent catalogs, tool registration, prompt configuration, and template customization.
* **Lifecycle & Release Management:** Practical execution of agent workflow testing, release packaging, environment promotion, monitoring, and rollback planning.
* **Workflow Mechanics:** Strong understanding of workflow state management, durable execution, retries, timeouts, and long-running workflows.
* **AI Architecture & Agent Patterns:** Practical understanding of tool-using AI agents, multi-step workflows, RAG architectures, and specialized agents (knowledge, ticket analysis, operations).
* **LLM Tech Stack & Quality:** Working knowledge of core LLMs (Azure OpenAI/OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Llama, Mistral), model endpoint integrations, prompt/context configuration, grounding, guardrails, agent evaluation, output validation, and expert proficiency in Python, FastAPI, REST APIs, JSON, YAML, and shell scripting.

**Preferred Skills and Experience**

  

* Prior background in AMS, ITSM, ServiceNow administration/development, SRE, cloud migration, or automation.
* Hands-on exposure to agentic frameworks including Semantic Kernel, LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, or MCP-style tool connectivity.
* Deep familiarity with ServiceNow APIs, data models, CMDB integrations, incident/request/change workflows, and knowledge management.
* Integration experience with enterprise systems including SharePoint, Confluence, GitHub/GitLab, Jira, monitoring/event tools, enterprise search, and cloud databases.
* Practical working exposure to JavaScript or TypeScript alongside strong hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, OpenShift, and Terraform.
* Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines via GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Azure DevOps.
* Proven skill in observability and monitoring using logs, metrics, traces, operational dashboards, alerts, and distributed troubleshooting.
Solid grasp of security fundamentals: OAuth, OIDC, SAML, certificates, secrets management, API gateways, RBAC, and private networking.
