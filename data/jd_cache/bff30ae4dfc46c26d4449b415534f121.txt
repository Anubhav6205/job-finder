**About the job**
-----------------

The Google Cloud Consulting Professional Services team guides customers through the moments that matter most in their cloud journey to help businesses thrive. We help customers transform and evolve their business through the use of Google’s global network, web-scale data centers, and software infrastructure. As part of an innovative team in this rapidly growing business, you will help shape the future of businesses of all sizes and use technology to connect with customers, employees, and partners.  
  
As a Data Engineer, you will guide customers on how to ingest, store, process, analyze, explore, and visualize data on Google Cloud Platform. You will lead data migrations and transformations, partner with clients to architect scalable data processing systems, build efficient data pipelines, and resolve platform challenges. In this role, you'll collaborate with Google's strategic cloud customers and our team to successfully implement Google Cloud products.

**Responsibilities**
--------------------

* Contribute to the design, and independently develop, test, and maintain scalable and reliable data pipelines for acquiring and processing petabyte-scale satellite and aerial imagery. Analyze existing overhead imagery operational workflows to identify bottlenecks and opportunities for automation and efficiency improvements.
* Define requirements for develop, and deploy tooling solutions, potentially leveraging Generative AI (GenAI), to enhance data quality checks and automate manual tasks in collaboration with Engineering and Operations teams. Implement, monitor, and refine robust data quality checks and validation systems to ensure the accuracy, completeness, and integrity of imagery data.
* Identify and implement optimizations within the data infrastructure to enhance performance, cost-efficiency, and scalability.
* Develop and maintain dashboards and reports to provide actionable insights into data quality, pipeline status, and key operational metrics for the Overhead Imagery Operations and partner teams.
