**What Exactly Should You Look For?**

**Core Expertise:** Python, Machine Learning, Time-Series Modelling, and Numerical Optimisation.

**Preferred Industry Background:** Manufacturing, Metals, Mining, Cement, Chemicals, Oil & Gas, and other Process Industries.

**A. Strong Python & Machine Learning Expertise**

**Must-have skills:**

* Strong proficiency in Python programming.
* Experience in Machine Learning model development.
* Hands-on experience with LightGBM.
* Knowledge of GAM (Generalized Additive Models).
* Feature engineering.
* Model validation and diagnostics.
* Model explainability.

**What to look for:**

The candidate should be capable of reviewing existing ML models, identifying areas for improvement, and optimising their performance rather than building everything from scratch.

**B. Time-Series & Industrial Process Modelling**

This is one of the most important aspects of the requirement.

**Must-have expertise:**

* Time-series forecasting and modelling.
* Industrial process data analysis.
* Equipment performance modelling.
* Experience working with historian data and laboratory data.
* Understanding of lagged features and sensor data.
* Knowledge of process variables and operational parameters.
* Predictive modelling for manufacturing operations.

**What does this mean practically?**

The candidate should understand how different industrial variables interact and develop models that can predict final outcomes based on historical and real-time operational data.

**C. Constrained Numerical Optimisation**

**Relevant skills:**

* Numerical optimisation.
* Constrained optimisation.
* Metaheuristic algorithms.
* Grid Search.
* Optimisation of operating parameters.
* Multi-objective optimisation.
* Throughput, recovery, and cost optimisation.
* Sensitivity and scenario analysis.

**What to look for:**

Candidates should have experience developing optimisation approaches that identify the best operating parameters while considering industrial constraints, production targets, recovery rates, and operational costs.

**D. Industrial Domain Experience**

**Preferred industry backgrounds:**

* Manufacturing.
* Mining and Metals.
* Cement.
* Chemicals and Petrochemicals.
* Oil & Gas.
* Power and Energy.
* Industrial Automation.

**Relevant experience:**

* Working with process engineers, plant operators, operational SMEs, and industrial data.
* Understanding industrial processes and operational parameters.
* Experience in manufacturing optimisation or process optimisation.

Candidates with direct experience in industrial or process optimisation would be particularly relevant.

**E. Model Pipeline & Live Testing**

Look for candidates who have hands-on experience with:

* Machine Learning model pipelines.
* Model deployment and inference workflows.
* Kedro (QuantumBlack) desirable, but not mandatory.
* API integration and data exchange.
* Live model validation.
* Shadow testing.
* Model monitoring and feedback loops.

**What to look for:**

The candidate should be comfortable running predictions on live plant data, comparing predicted outcomes with actual results, validating model performance, and improving models through continuous monitoring and feedback.

**Key Hiring Priority**

The ideal candidate should combine three major capabilities:

1. **Machine Learning:** Strong Python, LightGBM, GAM, feature engineering, and model diagnostics.
2. **Industrial Modelling & Optimisation:** Time-series modelling, process data understanding, and constrained numerical optimisation.
3. **Practical Implementation:** Model pipelines, deployment, live plant data validation, and shadow testing.

**Important:** Prioritise candidates who have applied Machine Learning and optimisation to real industrial processes rather than candidates with only theoretical ML or general data science experience.
