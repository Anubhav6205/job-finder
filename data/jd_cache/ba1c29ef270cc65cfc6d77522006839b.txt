**A. Regulatory Circulars & Compliance Implementation**

* Monitor, review and assess regulatory circulars, notifications, guidelines and directives issued by SEBI, stock exchanges and other applicable regulatory authorities.
* Drive effective implementation of regulatory requirements by coordinating with relevant business and support functions and ensuring timely communication of applicable regulatory changes.
* Assess the impact of regulatory amendments on existing business processes, policies and operational practices and facilitate necessary modifications to ensure compliance.
* Track implementation progress, monitor adherence to prescribed timelines and ensure appropriate documentation and closure of regulatory requirements.
* Provide regulatory guidance and interpretation to internal stakeholders to facilitate consistent understanding and implementation of compliance obligations.

**B. Regulatory Reporting & Statutory Submissions**

* Oversee the preparation, validation and timely submission of periodic regulatory reports and returns, including monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual submissions.
* Ensure accuracy, completeness and consistency of regulatory data and disclosures before submission to the relevant authorities.
* Manage FATCA-related reporting and submissions to the Income Tax Department, ensuring compliance with applicable reporting requirements and prescribed timelines.
* Establish effective tracking and monitoring mechanisms to ensure timely completion of all regulatory reporting obligations.
* Coordinate with relevant internal teams for data collation, reconciliation and resolution of discrepancies, wherever required.

**C. Compliance Advisory & Stakeholder Management**

* Act as a key point of contact for addressing regulatory and compliance-related queries, concerns and clarifications raised by internal stakeholders.
* Provide timely guidance on regulatory requirements, internal compliance processes, policies and applicable statutory obligations.
* Partner with business and support functions to facilitate resolution of compliance matters and ensure consistent interpretation of regulatory guidelines.
* Identify recurring compliance issues, assess potential risks and recommend appropriate corrective and preventive measures.

**D.** **Advertisement Review & Regulatory Approvals**

* Oversee the review and approval process for advertisements, marketing communications and promotional materials to ensure adherence to applicable regulatory guidelines.
* Review advertisement creatives, disclosures and communication content for regulatory accuracy, completeness and compliance before release or publication.
* Coordinate with regulatory authorities for review, clarification and approval of advertisement creatives wherever required.
* Ensure appropriate documentation and maintenance of approval records in line with regulatory and internal governance requirements.

**E. Employee Trading Desk & Insider Trading Compliance**

* Oversee the functioning and monitoring of the Employee Trading Desk to ensure adherence to the organisation's Insider Trading Policy and applicable regulatory requirements.
* Monitor employee trading activities, pre-clearance requests and related compliance processes in accordance with established policies and procedures.
* Identify opportunities to strengthen existing controls through automation, system enhancements and process standardisation.
* Drive initiatives to improve monitoring efficiency, minimise manual intervention and enhance the effectiveness of insider trading compliance mechanisms.

**F. Regulatory Representation & Policy Advocacy**

* Represent the organisation in interactions and discussions with regulatory authorities on regulatory and compliance-related matters.
* Review SEBI consultation papers, proposed regulatory amendments and policy developments to assess their potential impact on the organisation.
* Prepare and provide inputs, observations and recommendations on proposed regulatory changes and consultation papers.
* Coordinate with relevant internal stakeholders to consolidate organisational perspectives and ensure appropriate representation before regulatory authorities.

**G. Regulatory Liaison & External Coordination**

* Establish and maintain effective working relationships with SEBI, stock exchanges, depositories, the Income Tax Department and other relevant regulatory authorities, as applicable.
* Coordinate with regulatory authorities on compliance-related matters, regulatory clarifications, information requests and official communications.
* Facilitate the preparation and submission of responses to regulatory queries, observations and information requests within prescribed timelines.
* Ensure appropriate follow-through, tracking and closure of regulatory matters through effective coordination and documentation.

  
  

Skills Required

* Graduate/Postgraduate in Commerce, Finance, Business Administration or Law. Professional qualification such as CA, CS or LLB will be preferred.
* 10-15 years of relevant experience in Regulatory Compliance within the BFSI, Securities Broking or Capital Markets industry.
* Strong knowledge of SEBI regulations, stock exchange requirements, regulatory reporting and statutory compliance.
* Hands-on experience in regulatory submissions, implementation of circulars, regulatory liaison, Insider Trading compliance and advertisement approvals.
* Strong analytical, interpersonal and stakeholder management skills, with excellent written and verbal communication abilities.
* Hands-on and execution-oriented, with a high degree of ownership, attention to detail and ability to manage multiple regulatory deliverables within defined timelines.
