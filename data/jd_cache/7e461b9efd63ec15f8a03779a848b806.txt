* Develop, implement, and maintain forecasting models using **Python** for energy portfolio management applications.
* Design and enhance predictive models across multiple forecasting horizons:

+ Day-Ahead Forecast
+ Intraday Forecast
+ Short-Term Load Forecast
+ Medium-Term Forecast
+ Long-Term Load Forecast
+ Annual Forecast
+ Weekly Forecast
+ Monthly Load Forecast
+ Seasonal Load Duration Forecast

* Build and optimize specialized forecasting models including:

+ Renewable Energy Forecast (solar, wind generation)
+ Weather Forecast integration models
+ Price Forecast models for energy markets
+ Frequency Forecast models for grid stability
+ Forecast Comparison and model benchmarking tools
+ Other advanced and experimental forecasting models
