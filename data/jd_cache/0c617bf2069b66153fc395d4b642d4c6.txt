Machine Learning Engineer - Voice Agents (SDE2)

Exp. Required: 3 to 5 years

**About the Opportunity:**

We are building and scaling a production-grade conversational AI platform that delivers real-time, personalised experiences across chat and voice. Our AI agents are already in production, and we are expanding voice capabilities, multilingual support, personalisation, and reliability to support a growing user base.

**What You Will Do:**

1. Scale Conversational AI in Production

• Own and scale live conversational AI pipelines, optimising reliability, latency, and response quality as traffic grows.

• Extend multi-turn conversations, context management, memory, and agentic workflows for high concurrency.

• Improve grounding, consistency, and relevance under real production loads.

2. Build and Scale Voice AI

• Build and scale real-time voice AI systems and conversational interfaces.

• Work on streaming ASR, TTS, turn-taking, and low-latency voice interaction.

• Unify chat and voice experiences through shared context and memory.

3. LLM and RAG Systems at Scale

• Build retrieval systems over domain knowledge, structured data, and user context using LLMs, embeddings, vector search, RAG, and tool calling.

• Improve chunking, retrieval, metadata filtering, reranking, and relevance as data and traffic grow.

• Evaluate open-source and proprietary models for product and deployment needs.

4. LLM Orchestration and Optimisation

• Design production model orchestration and routing based on task requirements, quality, latency, and cost.

• Build robust fallbacks, structured outputs, and model-specific workflows.

• Own token consumption, inference latency, and infrastructure cost as key operational metrics.

5. Personalisation and Memory

• Build AI systems that leverage user profiles, conversation history, preferences, goals, and contextual signals.

• Extend long-term memory and personalisation systems as the platform scales.

• Improve recommendations, ranking, and relevance for individual users.

6. Domain Intelligence and Recommendations

• Combine structured domain data, deterministic business logic, retrieval, ML models, and LLM reasoning.

• Develop reliable personalised insights and recommendation workflows.

• Integrate domain-specific computation and data into conversational experiences.

7. Evaluation and Quality in Production

• Build evaluation datasets and automated tests covering chat and voice AI.

• Measure response quality, retrieval relevance, grounding, hallucination, consistency, and personalisation.

• Use production feedback and offline evaluations to detect regressions and improve quality.

8. Production ML Engineering and Reliability

• Build and own ML/AI services from development through deployment and scaling.

• Work with Python, APIs, Docker, AWS, databases, caching, queues, and vector databases.

• Own service latency, reliability, scalability, debugging, and incident prevention.
