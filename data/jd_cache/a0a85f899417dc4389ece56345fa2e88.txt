About Us

Jefferies Financial Group Inc. (‘‘Jefferies,’’ ‘‘we,’’ ‘‘us’’ or ‘‘our’’) is a U.S.-headquartered global full service, integrated investment banking and securities firm. Our largest subsidiary, Jefferies LLC, a U.S. broker-dealer, was founded in the U.S. in 1962 and our first international operating subsidiary, Jefferies International Limited, a U.K. broker-dealer, was established in the U.K. in 1986. Our strategy focuses on continuing to build out our investment banking effort, enhancing our capital markets businesses and further developing our Leucadia Asset Management alternative asset management platform. We offer deep sector expertise across a full range of products and services in investment banking, equities, fixed income, asset and wealth management in the Americas, Europe and the Middle East and Asia.

Innovation Hub Overview

Jefferies is creating a Technology Innovation Hub in Pune, a greenfield opportunity to build the systems that power global markets. As our first India technology center, this hub brings together hands on builders who engineer the platforms behind Jefferies’ growth across capital markets, investment banking, and institutional securities. We’re scaling toward an elite team of 500 engineers while maintaining the agility, ownership, and meritocratic spirit that defines Jefferies. From cloud and data to AI, risk, and core business technologies, teams in Pune will lead high impact work with a global mandate.

IT Infrastructure Technology

Jefferies’ IT Infrastructure Technology team builds and runs the core technology backbone that enables the firm to operate globally. It covers enterprise infrastructure engineering and operations while driving modernization initiatives such as Cloud Adoption and Network Resiliency. The group supports critical platforms including networking, end-user computing, cloud, databases, server/Unix engineering, communications, and global data centers (including low-latency colocations near exchanges).

Key functions:

* Networking / Telecom / Multimedia Engineering
* Windows / Desktop / Messaging Engineering.
* Private / Public Cloud / Database / Unix Engineering.
* Data Centers.

Position Overview

We are seeking a Cloud Engineer to help design, build, and operate enterprise cloud and Kubernetes platforms. This role will focus on delivering secure, reliable, scalable, and automated platform capabilities that enable application teams to adopt cloud-native technologies effectively.

The ideal candidate will have strong engineering experience in either AWS or Microsoft Azure, hands-on Kubernetes knowledge, and experience using infrastructure-as-code and DevOps practices to manage cloud environments at scale.

Responsibilities

* Design, build, and operate secure, scalable, and resilient cloud platform capabilities across AWS and Microsoft Azure.
* Engineer and support enterprise Kubernetes platforms, including managed services such as Amazon EKS and Azure Kubernetes Service.
* Manage Kubernetes platform capabilities including cluster lifecycle, upgrades, networking, ingress, DNS, security, observability, capacity, and operational governance.
* Develop and maintain reusable infrastructure-as-code modules and patterns using Terraform.
* Enable application teams to onboard applications and infrastructure through standardized, automated, and self-service platform capabilities.
* Build and support CI/CD and GitOps-based deployment workflows for cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes platforms.
* Build automation that solves complex problems with Agentic AI solutions
* Implement cloud governance, security controls, identity and access management, and policy enforcement through automation.
* Partner with application, architecture, cybersecurity, networking, and operations teams to deliver cloud-native solutions.
* Troubleshoot complex cloud and Kubernetes platform issues across infrastructure, networking, security, and application integration layers.
* Improve platform reliability through monitoring, observability, automation, operational standards, and continuous engineering improvements.
* Support cloud modernization and migration initiatives, including the transformation of legacy applications into cloud-native architectures.
* Evaluate emerging cloud and Kubernetes technologies and recommend practical improvements to the platform.
