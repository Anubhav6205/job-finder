**Summary**
**Position Summary**
**Applied AI Engineer III – Pursuits**
As an Applied AI Engineer III, you will actively engage in your engineering craft, taking a hands-on approach to building and enhancing high-visibility, full-stack products that serve the business and its users. Your expertise will be pivotal in delighting customers and users, while driving tangible value across Deloitte’s product and AI investments. You will leverage your extensive engineering craftsmanship across full-stack software engineering and modern frameworks—together with applied AI fluency that lets you build GenAI and agentic capabilities directly into the products you deliver—consistently demonstrating your strong track record in delivering high-quality, outcome-focused solutions. Working with Pursuit Leadership and Pursuit Subject Matter Experts, you will help create AI-enabled solutions for the Pursuits team that address identified business problems and improve how teams develop differentiated, high-quality client pursuits. The ideal candidate will be a dependable team player, collaborating with cross-functional teams to design, build, and ship products end to end, from concept through production.
  
  
**Work you'll do**
As an Applied AI Engineer on the Pursuit Management team, you will be responsible for…
  
  

* Design, build, and enhance full-stack and AI-enabled products that support the Pursuit Management team across the pursuit lifecycle, translating identified business and user problems into production-ready solutions.
* Lead requirements analysis, technical design, development, testing, integration, and production support while ensuring solutions align with product architecture, business priorities, and customer outcomes.
* Develop and integrate generative AI and agentic capabilities, including large language model integrations, retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, model evaluation, and orchestration workflows.
* Apply rapid experimentation, automated software delivery practices, and iterative development to deliver scalable, supportable products and improve quality, reliability, and maintainability.
* Partner with product management, experience, delivery, and engineering teams—and engage customers when needed—to evaluate trade-offs across feasibility, usability, value, performance, and cloud, inference, and token costs.

**The team**
The Pursuit Management team is a full-service group of pursuit managers, graphic designers, editors, project managers/coordinators, multimedia specialists, and researchers who directly support client service teams in differentiating Deloitte and winning more business. We deliver support throughout the proposal process, from scoping meetings and written proposal submission through orals preparation and debriefing. This includes developing strategic win themes and written, oral, and visual messaging used consistently in all prospect interactions and pursuit deliverables, as well as facilitating a smooth, rigorous pursuit process.

**Qualifications**
**Required:**

* Bachelor’s degree in computer science, software engineering, data science, or machine learning,
* 7-9 years of full-stack software engineering experience using Angular or React; Node.js, Python, C#, .NET, or Java; Structured Query Language (SQL) or non-relational (NoSQL) databases; and unit testing frameworks.
* 3+ years of experience building artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) and agentic applications, including generative AI (GenAI), large language model (LLM) integration using OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source models; retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines; prompt engineering; vector databases; model evaluations; AI agent orchestration; and PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain, or LangGraph.
* 3+ years of cloud-native engineering experience using Function as a Service (FaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), or microservices on Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS), or Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or Vertex AI; application-level infrastructure as code; and cloud, inference, and token cost management.
* Experience applying Business Context Diagrams (BCD), sequence, activity, state, entity-relationship, and data-flow diagrams; object-oriented programming (OOP) and object-oriented design (OOD); data structures; algorithms; code instrumentation; AI-augmented, specification-driven development; and one or more of the following: Extreme Programming (XP), Lean, development, security, and operations (DevSecOps), site reliability engineering (SRE), Azure DevOps (ADO), GitHub, SonarQube, MLflow, LangFuse, or LangSmith.
* You must reside within a commutable distance of your assigned office with the ability to commute daily, if required, without the need for overnight accommodation.
* You can expect to co-locate on average 2 times a week with variations based on types of work/projects and client locations.

**Preferred:**

* Experience developing AI-enabled products for content management, proposal, pursuits, business development, or professional services workflows.
* Experience implementing automated deployment, testing, and monitoring practices for cloud-native products.
