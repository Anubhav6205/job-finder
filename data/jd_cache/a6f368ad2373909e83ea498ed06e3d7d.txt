The Purview AI Classification team builds advanced AI capabilities to identify and classify sensitive information at enterprise scale, with a strong focus on Named Entity Recognition (NER) and rigorous AI evaluation. We develop and improve ML/NLP models for complex entity recognition scenarios and build evaluation methodologies to systematically measure accuracy, precision, recall, robustness, and model improvements across diverse datasets and real-world conditions.  
  
The Software Engineer will design and build scalable AI-powered classification and NER capabilities, with a focus on improving quality, performance, and reliability. The role will also contribute to evaluation infrastructure and partner with applied science and product teams to productionize model improvements at enterprise scale.

 **Responsibilities**  

* + Design, develop, and ship scalable AI-powered classification and NER systems, from service architecture and APIs through production deployment and integration.
  + Build and productionize capabilities for complex entity recognition scenarios, including All Full Names, Medical Terms, and Physical Addresses.
  + Develop production-grade code in C#, Python, etc., with strong focus on scalability, performance, security, testability, and maintainability.
  + Build and evolve AI evaluation infrastructure for datasets, automated evaluation, grading, regression detection, quality measurement, and reporting.
  + Integrate ML, LLM, and Generative AI capabilities into enterprise-scale classification systems and optimize them for real-world workloads.
  + Partner closely with Applied Science and Product Management to translate model improvements and customer requirements into robust production capabilities.
  + Own production quality across reliability, latency, throughput, observability, accuracy, and cost efficiency.
  + Build engineering systems for CI/CD, experimentation, safe rollout, monitoring, telemetry, and live-site operations for AI-powered services.
  + Apply Responsible AI, privacy, security, and compliance principles throughout system design and implementation.

  
**Qualifications**  

* **Qualifications: Minimum Requirements**
* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related technical field with a minimum of 5 years of experience.
* Strong programming skills in C#, C++, Python, or similar languages.
* Experience designing, developing, and operating large-scale distributed or cloud services.
* Solid understanding of software engineering fundamentals, including system design, testing, debugging, performance, and reliability.
* Experience working with AI/ML systems or integrating ML models into production applications/services.

* **Qualifications: Other/Preferred Requirements**

  + Security, compliance, or privacy-sensitive system experience.
