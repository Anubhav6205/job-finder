Role Overview
  
An Infrastructure Engineer is responsible for designing, implementing, managing, and supporting the infrastructure that enables an organizations IT systems and services to run smoothly. This includes working with hardware, networking, virtualization, cloud environments, and security systems to ensure the organizations IT infrastructure is efficient, reliable, and scalable. Infrastructure engineers often work closely with software engineers, IT support teams, and system administrators to optimize performance, mitigate risks, and deliver high-quality services to end-users.
  
  
**What will you do in this role:**  

* Manage the design, procurement, installation, upgrading, operation, control, maintenance, and effective use of specific technology services.
* Follow standard approaches and established design patterns to create new designs for systems or system components. Apply agreed standards and tools to achieve a well-engineered result.
* Carry out and also improve system software development and maintenance tasks by updating the product design to automate routine system administration tasks using standard tools and software development practices.
* Contribute to identification and prioritized adoption of Technology Engineering community practices within the products where you work.
* Work within a matrix organizational structure, reporting to both the functional manager and the Product manager.
* Participate in Product planning, execution, and delivery, ensuring alignment with Product goals.

**What should you have:**  

* Bachelors’ degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or any Technology stream.
* 3 years of hands-on experience working with network and system administration for Linux/Unix and Windows servers.
* Experience with traditional disaster recovery data backup solutions (Commvault, Acronis).
* Cloud and Virtualization Platforms: Proficiency in virtualization (Vmware, Hyper-V, Nutanix) and familiarity with cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) platforms.
* Networking: Understanding of virtual networks, VPNs, and firewalls.
* Operating Systems: Knowledge of Windows and Linux operating systems.
* Scripting and Automation: Skills in scripting languages like Bash or PowerShell and familiarity with infrastructure automation and management tools (Ansible, Terraform).
* DevOps practices: Experience with Git, infrastructure as code (IaC), observability, and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD).
* Security Best Practices: Familiarity with IT security practices and compliance.
* Problem-Solving: Ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues.
* Product and customer-centric approach.
* Familiarity with Prompt Engineering and AI-Powered Coding Assistants like GitHub Copilot.
* Familiarity with Agile way of working.
* Experience with other programming languages (Python, Java) is a nice to have.
