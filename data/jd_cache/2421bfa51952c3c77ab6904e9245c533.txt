Job Description – AI & STEM Engineer (Fresher)

STEMx Innovations Pvt. Ltd. | Full-Time

Experience: Freshers (0–1 Year)

Qualification: Diploma / B.E. / B.Tech / B.Sc. / BCA in Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, Robotics or related technical fields.

About the Role

STEMx Innovations Pvt. Ltd. is looking for enthusiastic and passionate freshers to join our team as AI & STEM Engineers. Candidates will be responsible for assisting in delivering Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, and STEM education in schools through practical demonstrations, hands-on activities, and innovative projects.

Key Responsibilities

* Conduct Robotics, AI, and STEM learning sessions in partner schools.
* Assist in conducting hands-on workshops and technical demonstrations.
* Guide students in developing basic robotics and AI-based projects.
* Teach basic concepts of Arduino, sensors, electronics, IoT, and programming.
* Introduce students to fundamental AI concepts and real-world applications.
* Assist students in assembling, testing, and troubleshooting robotics models.
* Encourage creativity, innovation, critical thinking, and problem-solving.
* Prepare project demonstrations and classroom activities.
* Support students in robotics competitions and innovation challenges.
* Coordinate with school teachers and management for smooth session delivery.

Required Skills

* Basic understanding of Robotics, AI, and STEM concepts.
* Knowledge of Arduino, electronics, sensors, or microcontrollers is an advantage.
* Basic programming knowledge (Python, C, or C++).
* Interest in robotics projects and emerging technologies.
* Good communication and presentation skills.
* Willingness to learn and adapt to new technologies.
* Ability to interact with and engage school students.
* Willingness to travel to assigned schools.

Eligibility Criteria

* Freshers can apply.
* Final-year students and recent graduates are eligible.
* Candidates from technical and engineering backgrounds are preferred.
* Candidates with academic projects related to Robotics, AI, IoT, or Electronics will have an added advantage.
* No prior teaching experience is required.

Pay: ₹20,000.00 - ₹22,000.00 per month

Benefits:

* Health insurance

Work Location: In person
