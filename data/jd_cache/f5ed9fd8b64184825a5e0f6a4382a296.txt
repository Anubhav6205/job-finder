Key Responsibilities
  
  

* Design, develop, test, and optimize prompts for LLMs such as OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama, and other generative AI models.
* Apply advanced prompting techniques to improve response accuracy, relevance, consistency, and overall model performance.
* Evaluate LLM outputs, identify issues such as hallucinations and inconsistent responses, and continuously refine prompts.
* Build and maintain reusable prompt libraries and templates for various business use cases.
* Work on LLM-powered applications using APIs, Python, and AI frameworks to integrate prompt-based solutions into SaaS products.
* Collaborate with AI Engineers, Data Scientists, and Product Teams to develop and enhance AI-driven features.
* Stay updated with emerging LLM technologies, prompt engineering techniques, and Generative AI best practices.

**Key Qualifications & Experience**

* 2–4 years of experience in Prompt Engineering, Generative AI, AI/ML, NLP, or related domains.
* Hands-on experience with LLMs such as OpenAI GPT, Claude, Llama, or similar models.
* Strong understanding of prompt engineering techniques and LLM evaluation methodologies.
* Working knowledge of Python, REST APIs, and LLM integrations.
* Familiarity with AI frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, Strands, or CrewAI.
* Basic understanding of RAG, embeddings, vector databases, and model fine-tuning.
* Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

**Preferred Tech Stack**

* LLMs: OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama
* Frameworks: LangChain, LangGraph, Strands, CrewAI
* Programming: Python, Node.js, JavaScript
* Backend: FastAPI, REST APIs
* Databases: PostgreSQL, MS SQL, Redis
