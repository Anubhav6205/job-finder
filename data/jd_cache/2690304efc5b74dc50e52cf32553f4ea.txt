**Job Summary**  

Net Full Stack with Angular, TypeScript, Microservices, Docker, and Container Developer - Pune

As a **Senior Full Stack Engineer**, you will design, develop, and enhance scalable enterprise applications within the FIS Private Equity Suite platform. You will work across the full technology stack, leveraging **Angular, C#, .NET, microservices, and containerized architectures** to deliver secure, high-performing solutions for global clients. This role requires strong technical leadership, hands-on development expertise, and the ability to drive modernization initiatives using modern engineering practices, including **AI-assisted development with GitHub Copilot**. Success is measured by the quality, scalability, reliability, and business impact of the solutions delivered.

**Responsibilities**  

* Design, develop, and maintain enterprise-grade applications using **Angular**, **TypeScript**, **C#**, and **.NET** technologies.
* Develop and enhance **microservices-based solutions** and distributed application architectures.
* Build responsive, accessible, and user-centric web interfaces that provide an exceptional client experience.
* Design and implement RESTful APIs and backend services, ensuring scalability, performance, and security.
* Develop and deploy applications using **Docker**, containerization technologies, and modern CI/CD pipelines.
* Collaborate with product, UX/UI, architecture, DevOps, and engineering teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions.
* Leverage **GitHub Copilot** and AI-assisted engineering tools to improve development productivity, code quality, and innovation.
* Participate in architecture discussions and contribute to application modernization initiatives, including cloud-native and container-based deployments.
* Write clean, maintainable, testable, and well-documented code following engineering best practices.
* Perform code reviews, mentor junior developers, and contribute to engineering standards and continuous improvement initiatives.
* Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve complex application issues while improving overall system performance and reliability.
* Support Agile delivery practices including estimation, sprint planning, development, testing, and release activities.
* Analyze complex technical challenges and recommend scalable, maintainable, and resilient solutions.
