Summary
  
  
AI & Data Platforms (AiDP) is IS&T's engine for AI-powered innovation. The team brings together data, application development, and machine learning — including generative AI — along with data services and customer success functions, to help IS&T build solutions more efficiently and streamline the adoption and embedding of generative AI across Apple. The Applied Machine Learning team within the AI & Data Platform organisation drives large-scale machine learning and GenAI workflows across Apple's enterprise ecosystem. Our platforms power real-time fraud decisioning in the critical path of customer transactions across Apple Retail, Online Store, Media, and Support, where reliability, scale, and low latency are fundamental.
  
  
Description
  
  
This role sits in the Reliability & Platform Engineering organization, but it is not a traditional operations role. You will write software that builds the platform: automation, deployment pipelines, developer tooling, and services running on Kubernetes and AWS. You will work alongside and be mentored by senior engineers, contributing across the full lifecycle from design to production
  
  
Minimum Qualifications  
  
  

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical discipline from a reputed, top-tier engineering institution.
* 1-3 years of industry experience; internships, co-ops, research, and significant projects count
* Strong programming skills in Python, with a solid understanding of software development fundamentals
* Good understanding of data structures, algorithms, operating systems, and networking fundamentals.
* Understanding of distributed systems concepts through coursework or projects
* Familiarity with Linux and Git, along with exposure to containerisation, Kubernetes, or public cloud platforms such as AWS or Alibaba Cloud.
* Good understanding of databases, including relational database concepts and the ability to write efficient SQL queries.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, a willingness to learn, and effective communication abilities.

  
  
  
Preferred Qualifications  
  
  

* Internship or project experience with Kubernetes, AWS, Terraform, or CI/CD tools
* Exposure to ML inference, GenAI, or LLM-based applications
* Interest in or exposure to model serving frameworks
* Familiarity with NoSQL databases such as Cassandra, Solr, OpenSearch
* Basic familiarity with monitoring tools such as Prometheus or Grafana
* Open-source contributions or personal projects applying AI to automation
