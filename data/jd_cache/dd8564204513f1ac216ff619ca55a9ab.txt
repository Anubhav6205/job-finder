**AI-QA Engineer**

Unlock your potential with Dassault Systèmes, a global leader in Scientific Software Engineering as an AI Quality Assurance Engineer in Pune, Maharashtra!
  
As an AI QA Engineer, you will be participating in Qualification of Web and/or Client-Server AI Applications. These Applications are used by our customers to design, develop, produce and market their Products and/or Services to end consumers. You will work directly with Product Define, Development, Testing, Project Management teams to ensure we are delivering high-quality, innovative products with an emphasis on innovation, speed to market and sustainability. You will lead in execution and team adoption of testing, automation tools & best practices.

**Role description & responsibilities**

* Design, develop, and execute functional, integration, regression, and automation test cases for AI-based applications.
* Perform AI/LLM application testing, including hallucination checks, bias detection, prompt validation, and responsible AI assessments.
* Evaluate RAG-based applications, retrieval quality, embeddings, semantic similarity, and LLM response accuracy.
* Test AI agents, MCP integrations, tool interactions, and non-deterministic agent responses.
* Develop and maintain automation scripts for Web Services/APIs using frameworks such as Selenium and Playwright.
* Investigate failed test cases and automation scripts, report defects, and provide defect and product quality metrics to the QA Manager.
* Integrate AI model, prompt, and agent workflow evaluations into CI/CD pipelines.
* Work closely with cross-functional and remote teams in an Agile/POD environment to ensure compliance with SQA standards and deliverable quality.

**Qualifications**

* BE/B.Tech/M.E/M.Tech professional who has 2-4 years of experience in Manual and Automation Testing of AI based Software Applications involved in development and execution of functional, integration & regression tests of both Web and Client-Server Applications using best in class AI concepts and strategies.
* Working Knowledge and hands-on experience in Core AI & LLM systems as user and tester
* Experienced in define and implementation of various testing strategies for AI virtual assistants, RAG based applications.
* Experience with using AI-assisted coding tools and methods
* Automation Testing of Web Services/APIs’ & good understanding of its Automation Framework (like Selenium, Playwright).
* Should have the motivation and ability to learn new Tools (including in-house ones), and Processes in order to deliver global objectives.
* Experience in any of the scripting language like Java/Python is must.
* Good Knowledge of SDLC/STLC Processes and Tools.
* Investigate failed test cases/ automated scripts and report issues in defect tracking system, and provide Defect and Product metrics to QA Manager as appropriate.
* Work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure project compliance with SQA standards and overall deliverable quality.
* Work with remote teams in an agile/agile POD environment.
* Knowledge/Experience of working in a DevOps/CI-CD Environment would be an added advantage.
* Ability to communicate well, work as part of a team and interact effectively with others.
* Displays initiative, self-motivation, and effective time-management skills.

**What’s in it for you**

* Be at the forefront of building software products that are being deployed in mission-critical projects world wide
* Be offered avenues to develop yourself for career progression
* Not a low-level development opportunity, as you would get to work in the real business world and with a wide range of customers & coworkers
