**About Position:**

  

We are looking for a Data Scientist specializing in LLM Fine-Tuning and Clinical NLP to lead the model-science side of LoRA adaptation for a healthcare-focused solution. The role will focus on preparing and curating clinical datasets, fine-tuning LLMs using LoRA/PEFT, designing evaluation frameworks, and measuring model quality against clinical standards. The person will work closely with ML Engineers and clinical/domain experts to ensure the adapted model produces accurate, safe, and clinically relevant outputs.

  

* **Role: Data Scientist**
* **Location: All Persistent Locations**
* **Experience: 8 to 12 Years**
* **Job Type: Full-Time Employment**
  

**What You'll Do:**

  

* Design, develop, and optimize machine learning and deep learning models using Python and PyTorch.
* Train, fine-tune, and evaluate Large Language Models (LLMs) for various business applications.
* Utilize Hugging Face libraries and ecosystems for model development, fine-tuning, and deployment.
* Implement parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques such as LoRA to optimize model performance and resource utilization.
* Conduct model experimentation, benchmarking, and performance analysis to improve accuracy and efficiency.
* Manage model training workflows, experiment tracking, and reproducibility using MLflow or Weights & Biases.
* Collaborate with data scientists, engineers, and product teams to integrate machine learning solutions into production systems.
* Develop and maintain data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model validation pipelines.
* Monitor model performance and recommend improvements through continuous experimentation and evaluation.
* Contribute to AI best practices, coding standards, and machine learning engineering frameworks.
* Participate in technical reviews, architecture discussions, and knowledge-sharing sessions.
* Stay updated with emerging trends and advancements in Machine Learning, Generative AI, and LLM technologies.
  

**Expertise You'll Bring:**

  

* 8 to 12 years of experience in Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, or Data Science.
* Strong proficiency in Python programming and software engineering best practices.
* Hands-on experience with PyTorch and modern deep learning frameworks.
* Strong expertise in Hugging Face libraries and transformer-based models.
* Experience training, evaluating, and optimizing Large Language Models (LLMs).
* Knowledge of parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques, including LoRA.
* Hands-on experience with experiment tracking and model lifecycle management using MLflow or Weights & Biases.
* Strong understanding of machine learning algorithms, deep learning architectures, and model evaluation techniques.
* Experience working with large datasets and distributed training environments.
* Familiarity with model performance optimization and hyperparameter tuning.
* Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities.
* Experience collaborating in Agile and cross-functional engineering environments.
* Excellent communication and stakeholder collaboration skills.
* Ability to translate business requirements into scalable AI solutions.
