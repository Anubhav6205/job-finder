**Data Engineer – Data Pipelines & GenAI Reporting | About You**

As a Data Engineer – Data Pipelines & GenAI Reporting, you will build standardized data pipelines that provide trusted, high-quality information for enterprise reporting and GenAI applications. You will be responsible for implementing ingestion workflows, applying established architecture standards, incorporating data-quality controls, and building reporting artifacts that support business users and AI-enabled solutions.

**Data Engineer – Data Pipelines & GenAI Reporting | Day-to-Day**

* Design, develop, and maintain standardized data pipelines based on established enterprise architecture and engineering standards.
* Build data ingestion and transformation workflows that integrate source data into trusted reporting and GenAI-ready datasets.
* Implement data quality checks and validation controls to ensure data accuracy, completeness, consistency, and reliability.
* Develop reporting artifacts in partnership with business and technical teams, ensuring data is structured appropriately for downstream consumption.
* Build and support reports, email-enabled outputs, and GenAI chat links, including access patterns appropriate for different user personas.
* Apply established architectural patterns, coding standards, and security requirements while creating clear technical and operational handoff documentation.
* Collaborate with Senior Data Engineers, Test Engineers, and business users to troubleshoot data issues and improve production reliability.

**Data Engineer – Data Pipelines & GenAI Reporting | Skills & Experience**

* 3+ years of Data Engineering experience developing production-grade data pipelines, ingestion processes, transformations, and reporting datasets.
* Strong skills in SQL and data transformation, with experience working with structured enterprise data and scalable pipeline architectures.
* Experience implementing data quality checks, validation rules, reconciliation processes, and production monitoring within data pipelines.
* Understanding of data modeling, reporting structures, security, and role/persona-based data access principles.
* Experience supporting analytics, reporting, or GenAI-enabled applications that consume enterprise data.
* Strong troubleshooting, documentation, and collaboration skills, with experience following established architecture and engineering standards.
