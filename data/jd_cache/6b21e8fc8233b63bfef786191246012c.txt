**What you'll do**
------------------

  
  

The Cloud Platform Engineer role plays a key role in the development, management, support, and maintenance of Docusign’s Cloud infrastructure and services. An ideal candidate is an experienced engineer that has proven expertise in development and implementation of GenAI stacks, enabling Cloud services and CI/CD automation, while partnering with different engineering teams. We invest in tooling, automation, and infrastructure that reduces friction for engineers to develop and manage software and services. The successful candidate must be self-directed and self-motivated, yet capable of taking instruction and feedback.

This position is an individual contributor role reporting to the Director, Developer Platform Engineering.

**Responsibility**

* Design, build, and maintain secure, scalable, highly available, and cost-efficient AWS cloud foundations across multi-account and multi-environment architectures
* Establish cloud foundation standards covering networking, IAM, landing zones, governance, security guardrails, and connectivity
* Develop reusable infrastructure modules and automated provisioning workflows using Terraform and Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
* Build platform automation and self-service capabilities that enable engineering teams to provision and operate cloud resources securely and efficiently
* Drive cloud security, observability, and reliability engineering; troubleshoot complex infrastructure issues, lead root-cause analysis, and continuously improve platform resilience
* Own FinOps practices, including cloud cost monitoring, optimization, resource tagging, cost allocation, and spend governance
* Define and implement Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCDR) strategies, including backup, recovery, disaster recovery planning, and resilience testing

**What you bring**
------------------

  
  

**Basic**

* 8+ years of experience in cloud infrastructure or platform engineering, including 5+ years of hands-on experience designing, building, and operating AWS cloud environments
* Strong expertise in AWS cloud foundations, including multi-account architecture, landing zones, networking, IAM, governance, security guardrails, and core cloud services
* Hands-on proficiency in Terraform, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), scripting (Python/Bash), and automated infrastructure provisioning
* Strong understanding of cloud security, reliability engineering, observability, incident management, and production operations
* Experience implementing cloud cost optimization practices, backup and recovery strategies, and business continuity and disaster recovery (BCDR) solutions

  

**Preferred**

* Working knowledge of Azure or GCP, including their core cloud foundation services and architecture
* Experience building internal developer platforms, self-service infrastructure capabilities, and reusable cloud automation workflows
* Experience with Kubernetes/EKS, GitOps, policy as code, and cloud security tooling
* Familiarity with FinOps practices, cloud cost allocation, resilience testing, and disaster recovery planning
