Job Overview

We are looking for a skilled and enthusiastic
**Agentic AI Engineer**
with 2–3 years of hands-on experience in developing AI-powered applications, LLM-based solutions, and autonomous AI agents.

The ideal candidate should have practical experience working with Large Language Models (LLMs), AI agent frameworks, prompt engineering, API integrations, and Python-based development.

You will be responsible for designing, developing, testing, and deploying intelligent AI agents capable of understanding user requirements, making contextual decisions, executing tasks, and interacting with external systems and business applications.

Key Responsibilities

* Design and develop autonomous AI agents using modern Agentic AI frameworks and technologies.
* Build LLM-powered applications using OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, or other foundation models.
* Develop multi-agent workflows and AI-driven automation solutions for enterprise use cases.
* Work with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, or AutoGen.
* Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using vector databases and embedding models.
* Integrate AI agents with REST APIs, databases, third-party applications, and enterprise systems.
* Develop intelligent chatbots, AI assistants, and task-oriented conversational applications.
* Implement prompt engineering, context management, memory, tool calling, and function calling.
* Work with vector databases such as Pinecone, ChromaDB, FAISS, or Weaviate.
* Develop backend services and AI application APIs using Python and FastAPI or Flask.
* Evaluate AI model performance, troubleshoot issues, and optimise accuracy, latency, and response quality.
* Implement appropriate security, validation, monitoring, and human-in-the-loop mechanisms.
* Collaborate with software developers, technical architects, and project teams to deliver production-ready AI solutions.
