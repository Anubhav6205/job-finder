Job Title: Machine Learning Advisor
  
  
You will be joining a team transforming healthcare and improving the lives and vitality of the millions of members we serve. We leverage cutting edge Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) algorithms to develop AI powered solutions healthcare applications. We are looking for Forward Deployed Engineer with strong experience on LLM’s and frameworks to develop GenAI / Agentic solutions. Good understanding on Deep learning and strong Python full stack engineering skills are required as part of this role. Candidate should have full stack development expertise to leverage Large Language Models (LLMs) and use them in the enterprise in a compliant and secure manner. The work you do will impact millions of customers, members, and employers that rely on Cigna every day. Extreme focus on speed to market and getting Products and Services in the hands of customer and passion to transform healthcare is key to the success of this role.
  
  
**Responsibilities**

* Embed with Business and Product Development to ship production AI systems.
* Architect scalable, resilient generative and agentic AI solutions for the business.
* Build with large language models, open-source models, machine learning, and numerical frameworks.
* Maintain pipelines for preprocessing, feature extraction, model training, and evaluation.
* Build agentic workflows with tool limits, human oversight, and safe fallbacks.
* Optimize generative models for quality, scalability, latency, and efficiency.
* Apply responsible AI through use limits, evaluation, traceability, and human approval.
* Translate model, infrastructure, and business AI trends into architecture decisions.
* Practice AI-driven development: agents plan and build; humans approve critical decisions.
* Run AI DLC inception, Run AI DLC construction, AI proposes architecture, code, and tests; humans approve.
* Keep one traceable context from business intent through code, tests, and operations.
* Lead real-time team review of AI plans, designs, and generated code.
* Measure field outcomes and return supportable systems to Product Development.
* 11+ years of total experience.
* 6+ years of experience in Fullstack development (Frontend, Backend Services, DB and Cloud)
* 5+ Programming experience in Python, computer vision, self-supervised learning, transfer learning, and reinforcement learning.
* 2+ year in developing Agentic AI solutions and its integration with other systems.
* 4+ years of experience in deep learning architectures, and Transformers, with a strong understanding of deep learning techniques such as GPT, VAE, and GANs.
