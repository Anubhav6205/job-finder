**Job description**
===================

**About the Role**

We are looking for an experienced **QA Engineer / SDET** to join the team that focusses on building robust test automation and validating the **functionality, reliability, scalability, and performance of a large-scale distributed file-storage platform** .

  

**Key Responsibilities**

* Design and develop **end-to-end automation frameworks** for distributed storage and file-system functionality.
* Automate functional, integration, and end-to-end qualification for **storage protocols, REST APIs and CLI workflows.**
* Develop automated workloads using tools such as **FIO, VDBench, MLPerf and IO500** .

* Implement **chaos and fault-injection testing** to validate high availability, node failovers, and disaster recovery
* Integrate automated test suites into **CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins)** for continuous qualification gates
* Diagnose system failures, triage logs and core dumps, and collaborate with development teams to reproduce and resolve defects
* Collaborate using Agile tools such as **Jira, GitHub, and Confluence**
* Leverage **AI-based developer and testing tools** to improve test coverage, script generation, and defect analysis.

  

**Required Skills & Experience**

* **5+ years** of experience in software test automation or SDET roles for distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, or storage platforms
* Strong programming skills in **Python** (specifically writing modular test harnesses with pytest) and **Bash** scripting
* Experience developing scalable **test automation frameworks** , not just automated testcases.
* Hands-on experience with **NFS/NFS-Ganesha** ; pNFS knowledge is a plus.
* Experience with storage/performance tools such as **FIO, VDBench, IO500, tcpdump/Wireshark, or eBPF** .
* Experience with **Git, Jenkins/GitHub Actions, REST APIs, Containerization concepts and Kubernetes** .
* Solid understanding of **distributed systems concepts** , high availability, and fault tolerance
* Familiarity with storage concepts and protocols such as **NFS, SMB, S3, or POSIX file systems**
* Strong **Linux operating system** administration, troubleshooting, and network debugging skills (tcpdump, routing, logs)
* Hands-on experience integrating test automation into **Jenkins or similar CI/CD systems**

  

**Nice to Have**

* Exposure to **NVMe/NVMe-oF, SPDK, RocksDB, WAL/transaction processing, or distributed storage systems**
* Familiarity with performance benchmarking tools ( **fio** ) and telemetry systems ( **Prometheus/Grafana** ).

  

**Education**

* **BE/BTech or ME/MTech in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field**
