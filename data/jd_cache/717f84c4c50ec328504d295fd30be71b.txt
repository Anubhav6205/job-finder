**Job Summary**
---------------

VOLVO FG  
"Description:

The Opportunity  
Dev Team Assistant (DTA) has successfully completed its pilot phase and is now preparing for enterprise-wide rollout across Volvo Group. We are looking for a Senior AI Engineer who enjoys balancing hands-on development with architectural thinking and wants to help scale an enterprise AI platform used across Volvo Group.  
This is an opportunity to work hands-on with modern GenAI technologies, agent systems, RAG architectures, and influence the strategy for a product that will make a real difference for development teams across the organization.

About the Product and Team  
Dev Team Assistant (DTA) is an AI-powered assistant helping development teams quickly find answers, guidance, and resources grounded in Volvo Group knowledge. DTA serves as the orchestration layer for a growing ecosystem of AI assistants, making scalability, routing, observability, and user experience key challenges moving forward.  
As adoption grows, our focus is on expanding our ecosystem of AI assistants, improving routing and orchestration, enhancing response quality and performance and continuously improving user experience.  
The team consists of three developers and a Digital Product Owner working closely together in an agile and highly collaborative environment. We value transparency, curiosity, and a culture where everyone shares knowledge and supports each other.  
  
What You’ll Do  

* Design and implement AI-powered capabilities within DTA
* Develop and improve agent-based workflows and orchestration patterns
* Improve platform performance, reliability, and scalability
* Take ownership of key technical decisions and help shape the long-term AI architecture for DTA.
* Integrate new assistants and AI services into the platform
* Contribute to DevSecOps practices and engineering excellence
* Act as a senior technical contributor and sparring partner within the team
* Thrive in a team where everyone's voice is heard, initiative is encouraged, and collaboration comes naturally.

What We’re Looking For  
Several years of professional software engineering experience, with strong Python skills and experience building production-grade AI solutions.

Required:  

* Python skills.
* Experience with several of the following:
* LLM applications
* RAG architectures
* agent-based systems.
* LangGraph and/or LangChain,
* Evaluation and optimization of AI assistants
* Azure OpenAI
* FastAPI
* Azure Web Apps
* Communicative, proactive, and transparent in your work.
* Comfortable navigating emerging technologies and solving problems where there is no established playbook.

Nice to have:  

* Frontend experience with React and TypeScript is preferred.
* Good understanding of DevOps: pipelines, CI/CD, test automation, application security, cloud, network, Terraform.
* Solid experience with Microsoft Azure in a controlled environment."
