**This role is for one of our clients**

Industry: Software Development

Seniority level: Associate Level

  

Min Experience: 2+ years  
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
JobType: full-time

We are looking for a skilled and customer-focused Application Support Engineer to provide technical and functional support for software applications. The role involves managing customer-reported issues, troubleshooting application-related problems, identifying effective solutions, and ensuring timely resolution of technical queries.

The ideal candidate should have experience in Application Support, Product Support, or Customer Support, along with strong problem-solving and communication skills. The individual will work closely with internal technical teams to investigate complex issues, ensure smooth application functioning, and deliver a high-quality support experience to international clients.

**Requirements**
----------------

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

* Take ownership of customer-reported issues and ensure they are resolved within defined timelines.
* Research, diagnose, troubleshoot, and identify appropriate solutions for application and system-related problems.
* Understand the application from both functional and technical perspectives to provide effective support.
* Follow established procedures to escalate unresolved or complex issues to the appropriate internal teams.
* Refer to internal knowledge bases, technical documentation, and external resources to identify accurate solutions.
* Ensure all customer issues, incidents, and resolutions are properly logged and tracked.
* Prioritize and manage multiple open support requests while maintaining service quality.
* Prepare accurate and timely reports on support activities, issue status, and resolutions.
* Document technical knowledge, troubleshooting steps, and solutions through internal notes, knowledge articles, and user manuals.
* Coordinate with relevant teams to investigate recurring issues and improve the overall support process.

**Required Skills & Qualifications**
------------------------------------

* Prior experience as an Application Support Engineer, Product Support Engineer, or Customer Support Engineer.
* Strong understanding of application support processes and troubleshooting methodologies.
* Ability to quickly learn and gain expertise in organization-specific software applications.
* Strong analytical and complex problem-solving abilities.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
* Ability to diagnose, investigate, and resolve application-related issues.
* Familiarity with support workflows, issue tracking, and escalation procedures.
* Ability to manage multiple support tickets and prioritize tasks effectively.
* Willingness to work in rotational shifts.
